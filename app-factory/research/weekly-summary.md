### Analysis Framework Ready

For each app, answer 7 key questions:
1. What specific pain does it solve?
2. Who is the exact user?
3. What's the "unbundling" angle?
4. What's the distribution moat?
5. What would a "copycat" version look like?
6. Does it cross-sell to our orchestras?
7. Can we build MVP in <7 days with <$50 API costs?

### Scoring Systems

#### Replicability Score (1-10)
- **9-10:** Build MVP in 2-3 days, clear demand, low competition
- **7-8:** Build in 1 week, moderate competition, needs validation
- **5-6:** Build in 2 weeks, requires unique data/partnerships
- **1-4:** Too complex, requires team, or market too crowded

#### Synergy Score (1-10)
- **10:** Directly uses KidScan + Nudge + Directory Beast
- **8-9:** Uses 2 of our orchestras
- **6-7:** Uses 1 orchestra
- **1-5:** Standalone, no cross-sell potential

### Initial Research (2026-04-19)

#### Greg Isenberg's Micro-SaaS Blueprint
1. **Build niche internet audience first** – choose one platform, dominate it
2. **Create lead magnets** – free communities, templates, e-books to capture emails
3. **Unbundle existing apps with 1 killer feature** – find what giants do poorly
4. **Change pricing** – subscription fatigue; offer lifetime deals or pay-per-usage
5. **Automate everything** – AI handles 80% of development and marketing
6. **Tweet/share progress daily** – share revenue numbers for authenticity

#### App Portfolio Success Pattern
- **Solo dev with 30-app portfolio:** $22k/month MRR
- **Strategy:** Portfolio compounds through cross-promotion
- **MRVL Technologies (17 apps in 6 months):** "Each new app is a chance to cross-promote the others"
- **Toss mini-app platform:** 5 mini apps in one month → $19k/month revenue

#### Profitable Micro-SaaS Examples
| App | Niche | MRR | Key Insight |
|-----|-------|-----|------------|
| EZ Fulfill | Shopify CSV tracking | $8k | One founder, built weekends |
| Vendor Hawk | Amazon seller protection | $12k | Prevents hijackers stealing Buy Box |
| PODTurbo | Print-on-demand automation | $25k | Solo founder in Malaysia |
| Hypefury | Twitter scheduling | $70k | MVP in 6 weeks on sister's couch |
| Carrd | One-page sites | $1M+ ARR | Started as solo itch-scratcher |
| WooCurve | WooCommerce upsells | $45k | Acquired by GoDaddy |

### Success Factors Identified
1. **Razor-thin niche:** Serve specific users, not everyone
2. **Solves painful, specific problem:** Users actively search for solution
3. **One founder, lean operation:** Low overhead = profitable at low MRR
4. **Charges $10-$100/month:** Or one-time $49-$99 for directories
5. **Validated MVP in 1-2 weeks:** Not months of development
6. **ASO + organic growth:** App store optimization drives growth
