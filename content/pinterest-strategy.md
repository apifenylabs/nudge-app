# Pinterest Strategy — Family Travel Asia

## Overview

Pinterest is a visual search engine, not social media. Family travel content performs exceptionally well because parents actively search for vacation ideas, itineraries, and destination guides. Our strategy focuses on:

1. **Rich Pins** — Each blog post gets 3 pins with SEO-rich titles, keyword descriptions, and board assignments
2. **Pillar Pins** — Evergreen, high-value content optimized for long-term search discovery
3. **Board Strategy** — Topic clusters organized by destination and travel theme
4. **Consistent Uploads** — Batch upload scripts enable regular pinning cadence

## Key SEO Keywords for Family Travel Asia

- Family travel Asia, Asia with kids, family-friendly resorts, luxury family travel
- [Destination] with kids: Bali with kids, Singapore with kids, Tokyo with kids, etc.
- Family travel tips, kids clubs, family resort guides, best time to visit
- First-class family flying, private transfers, butler service, Michelin dining

## Pin Design Guidelines

- **Dimensions:** 1000 × 1500 px (2:3 ratio — standard vertical pin)
- **Text overlay:** Bold, large title, 2-3 words max per line, high contrast
- **Branding:** Family Travel Asia logo + URL overlay at bottom
- **Colors:** Warm, aspirational — teal, coral, gold accents
- **Imagery:** Authentic family moments, not stock photos of models looking posed

## Upload Cadence

- Batch upload 10-15 pins/week via Pinterest Upload CSV
- 3 pins per blog post, spread across different boards
- Fresh pins perform better than repins

## Board Categories

| Board Name | Focus | Pins |
|---|---|---|
| Asia Family Travel Tips | General advice, guides, itineraries | All evergreen content |
| Bali With Kids | Bali-specific resorts, activities, tips | Bali-tagged posts |
| SE Asia Family Vacations | Thailand, Singapore, Cambodia, Philippines | Southeast Asia guides |
| Luxury Family Resorts | High-end properties, reviews, comparisons | Resort-focused posts |
| Tokyo & Japan With Kids | Japan-specific travel guides | Japan content |
| Family Travel Hacks | Budget, timing, booking tips | Budget/strategy posts |
| Maldives Family Paradise | Maldives resorts and family guides | Maldives content |

## Rich Pin Setup

- Ensure blog posts have Open Graph meta tags
- Add `article:tag` meta tags for destination keywords
- Use Pinterest Tag for conversion tracking
- Verify domain on Pinterest Business account
