# Family-Friendly Restaurants in Bangkok for Picky Eaters

Every parent knows the struggle: you're in Bangkok — a world-class food city — and your toddler wants chicken nuggets and plain rice.

Don't panic. Bangkok is surprisingly accommodating for picky eaters. The city has everything from dedicated children's cafes to Thai restaurants that will happily serve plain rice, grilled chicken, and mild noodle dishes. You just need to know where to go.

Here are the best family-friendly restaurants in Bangkok that even the fussiest little eaters will love.

---

## Quick Tips for Dining with Picky Eaters in Bangkok

1. **Ask for "mai phet"** (ไม่เผ็ด) — means "not spicy." Most restaurants will accommodate.
2. **Thai fried rice (khao pad)** is almost always kid-safe — plain, with egg, no spice.
3. **Pad Thai** can be ordered without chilli flakes. Ask for "mai sai prik."
4. **Chicken skewers (gai yang)** from street stalls are grilled, mild, and toddler-approved.
5. **7-Eleven** is your emergency backup — steamed buns, milk, and small snacks everywhere.

---

## 1. KidZania Bangkok — The Indoor City Restaurant

**Vibe:** Part of the KidZania indoor theme park at Siam Paragon. The on-site restaurant serves burgers, pasta, pizza, and chicken nuggets — all the classic picky-eater staples.

**Why it works:** Your kid eats because they're in the middle of a fun day. The restaurant is designed for families, with high chairs, a kids' menu, and staff who are used to toddlers.

**Address:** 5th Floor, Siam Paragon, Rama I Road
**Price:** $$ (USD 8-15 per adult meal)
**Special feature:** The restaurant is inside KidZania itself — eat while your kid plays.

**Nearby:** After lunch, walk to Siam Ocean World (aquarium) or the Erawan Shrine. Both are within the Siam area.

---

## 2. Salsa Cocina — Mexicana for the Whole Family

**Vibe:** Colourful Mexican restaurant in Sukhumvit with a dedicated kids' play area.

**Why it works:** Mexican food is naturally kid-friendly — mild quesadillas, cheesy nachos, beans and rice. The play area has a slide, ball pit, and toys, so kids can burn off energy while parents enjoy their margaritas.

**Kids' menu:** Mini cheese quesadilla, chicken taquitos, plain rice and beans. All served with fruit juice and a small toy.

**Address:** 17/1 Sukhumvit Soi 23 (Asok area)
**Price:** $$ (USD 10-18 per adult meal)
**Play area:** Indoor, air-conditioned, with supervision

**Pro tip:** Go for weekday lunch when it's quiet. The play area gets packed on weekends.

---

## 3. Bitterman — The Western Fallback

**Vibe:** Stylish Western restaurant in Ekkamai with excellent breakfast and brunch.

**Why it works:** Bitterman serves proper Western food that picky kids recognise — pancakes, scrambled eggs, spaghetti Bolognese, grilled chicken with mash. The atmosphere is relaxed and family-friendly.

**Kids' menu:** Yes, with smaller portions at half the adult price. Spaghetti, chicken fingers, fish and chips, grilled cheese sandwich.

**Address:** 231/3 Sukhumvit Soi 63 (Ekkamai)
**Price:** $$$ (USD 12-20 per adult meal)
**Special feature:** Great coffee for parents — proper espresso, not instant.

**Nearby:** Ekkamai has several kid-friendly cafes within walking distance, including Kiddee (a dedicated indoor playground cafe).

---

## 4. Khao Tom — The Thai Street Food That Toddlers Love

**Vibe:** Classic Thai rice soup (jok) restaurant that's popular with families.

**Why it works:** Khao tom (rice soup) and jok (congee) are naturally mild, soft-textured, and perfect for toddlers. Most restaurants will serve it plain with a side of poached chicken or fish. No spice, no strong flavours.

**Best spot:** **Jok Prince** at Yaowarat (Chinatown) — open early morning until noon. A bowl of jok with century egg and pork (about USD 2) is a perfect breakfast for picky toddlers.

**Alternative:** **Chong Kee** on Soi Charoen Krung 43 — less famous but more family-friendly with high chairs and air conditioning.

**Price:** $ (USD 1-3 per bowl)

**What to order:**
- Jok (congee) with pork — mild, warm, soft
- Plain rice with steamed fish (pla neung) — ask for no chilli
- Khao tom gai (rice soup with chicken)

---

## 5. Coco Walk — The Food Court Solution

**Vibe:** Air-conditioned food court with 20+ stalls, near the BTS, at Siam Square.

**Why it works:** Food courts are the secret weapon for picky eaters in Bangkok. Everyone gets what they want — mom gets spicy papaya salad, dad gets pad kra pao, and the toddler gets plain fried rice and watermelon juice.

**Best stalls for kids:**
- **Khao Pad stall** — plain fried rice, 50 baht
- **Khao Man Gai stall** — chicken and rice, 50 baht
- **Fruit stall** — fresh mango, watermelon, dragonfruit

**Address:** Siam Square Soi 5, near BTS Siam
**Price:** $ (USD 1-3 per dish)
**Special feature:** Central location, clean, air-conditioned. Perfect for a quick lunch between shopping at Siam Paragon and MBK.

---

## 6. The Never Ending Summer — Upscale But Kid-Friendly

**Vibe:** Beautiful Thai restaurant in a converted warehouse by the Chao Phraya River. Don't let the name fool you — it's very family-friendly.

**Why it works:** The staff are exceptionally kind to children. They'll bring out plain rice, steamed vegetables, and a tiny portion of the chef's special mild green curry without any chilli. The riverside setting and open kitchen also fascinate kids.

**Kids' menu:** Not a formal one, but they'll make anything kid-friendly on request.

**Address:** 41/5 Charoen Nakhon Road, Khlong San (near ICONSIAM)
**Price:** $$$$ (USD 15-25 per adult meal)
**Reservations:** Essential on weekends. Book 2-3 days ahead.

**Pro tip:** Go for early dinner (5:30-6pm) to catch the sunset over the river. Kids can watch boats go by while waiting for food.

## The 7-Eleven Lifeline

Bangkok's 7-Eleven stores are a godsend for parents of picky eaters. They're on virtually every corner and stock:

- **Fresh milk** (plain and chocolate)
- **Yoghurt tubes** (fruit flavours)
- **Steamed buns** (pork, taro, red bean — mild)
- **Rice triangle (onigiri)** — plain salmon or tuna
- **Fruit cups** (fresh-cut mango, watermelon, pineapple)
- **Bread** (white toast, plain)
- **Sandwich triangles** (ham and cheese, egg mayo)

A full "emergency meal" for a toddler costs less than USD 3. Keep one in your bag.
