# Senior-Friendly Travel Asia - DATA.md

## Initial Destinations (15 cities)

### Tier 1: Top Senior-Friendly (Score 9.0+)

1. **Singapore 🇸🇬** — Score: 9.2
   - Why: World-class MRT with elevators at every station, English widely spoken, excellent healthcare (Mt. Elizabeth, Gleneagles), flat terrain, clean streets, senior discounts on attractions, wheelchair-friendly museums & Gardens by the Bay.
   - Top spots: Gardens by the Bay, Marina Bay Sands SkyPark, Singapore Botanic Gardens, National Museum, Chinatown Heritage Centre
   - Best time: Jan–Mar (cooler, less humid)
   - Accessibility: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

2. **Taipei, Taiwan 🇹🇼** — Score: 8.9
   - Why: Excellent MRT with accessibility features, very safe even at night, affordable healthcare, senior discounts (free MRT for 65+), clean air, great street food that's easy on digestion, English-friendly tourist infrastructure.
   - Top spots: Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall, Taipei 101, Elephant Mountain (easy trail), National Palace Museum, Beitou Hot Springs
   - Best time: Oct–Apr
   - Accessibility: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

3. **Hong Kong 🇭🇰** — Score: 8.8
   - Why: Octopus card for easy transit, extensive MTR with elevators, English signage everywhere, world-class hospitals, senior discounts (HK$2 travel scheme for 65+ on public transport), efficient taxis, excellent food variety.
   - Top spots: Victoria Peak (tram), Star Ferry, Hong Kong Park, Kowloon Walled City Park, Tian Tan Buddha (cable car option)
   - Best time: Oct–Mar (mild)
   - Accessibility: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

4. **Kyoto, Japan 🇯🇵** — Score: 8.7
   - Why: Omotenashi (attentive service culture), excellent public transport, clean and safe, senior-friendly temples with ramps, accessible toilets everywhere, zen gardens perfect for slow exploration, superb healthcare.
   - Top spots: Kinkaku-ji, Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, Philosopher's Path, Fushimi Inari (partial), Ryoan-ji Temple
   - Best time: Mar–May, Oct–Nov
   - Accessibility: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

### Tier 2: Highly Recommended (Score 8.0–8.9)

5. **Tokyo, Japan 🇯🇵** — Score: 8.8
   - Why: Best public transit in the world, priority seating culture, accessible stations, English signage, exceptional healthcare, incredibly safe, endless dining options with dietary awareness.
   - Top spots: Ueno Park, Meiji Shrine, Shinjuku Gyoen, Asakusa Senso-ji (ramps), TeamLab (accessible floors)
   - Best time: Mar–Apr, Oct–Nov
   - Accessibility: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

6. **Chiang Mai, Thailand 🇹🇭** — Score: 8.5
   - Why: Slow-paced lifestyle, affordable long-stay options, excellent private hospitals (Chiangmai Ram, Bangkok Hospital Chiang Mai), many expat seniors, many temples with gentle paths, great food scene, cooking classes.
   - Top spots: Doi Suthep (cable car available), Wat Phra Singh, Night Bazaar, Mae Sa Waterfall, Nimman area for cafes
   - Best time: Nov–Feb (cool season)
   - Accessibility: ⭐⭐⭐

7. **Seoul, South Korea 🇰🇷** — Score: 8.4
   - Why: Modern subway with accessibility signage, senior-friendly culture, excellent hospitals (Severance, Samsung Medical), safe, great public parks, senior discounts on culture/transit.
   - Top spots: Gyeongbokgung Palace, Namsan Cable Car, Bukchon Hanok Village, Seoul Forest, Hangang River Cruise
   - Best time: Apr–Jun, Sep–Nov
   - Accessibility: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

8. **Penang, Malaysia 🇲🇾** — Score: 8.3
   - Why: Affordable, excellent street food, English widespread, good hospitals (Penang Adventist, Island Hospital), many expat seniors, flat George Town suitable for walking, heritage shophouses, slow-pace exploration.
   - Top spots: Pinang Peranakan Mansion, Kek Lok Si Temple (escalators), Penang Hill funicular, Clan Jetties, Batu Ferringhi
   - Best time: Dec–Feb
   - Accessibility: ⭐⭐⭐

### Tier 3: Good Options (Score 7.5–7.9)

9. **Hoi An, Vietnam 🇻🇳** — Score: 7.9
   - Why: Flat pedestrian-friendly Old Town, affordable luxury stays, great food scene, tailors for custom clothing, peaceful atmosphere, many spas. Some cobblestone but mostly accessible.
   - Top spots: Old Town lantern-lit streets, Japanese Covered Bridge, An Bang Beach, Tra Que Vegetable Village, cooking classes
   - Best time: Feb–May
   - Accessibility: ⭐⭐⭐

10. **Bali Ubud, Indonesia 🇮🇩** — Score: 7.8
    - Why: Strong wellness/spa culture, many accessible villas, great for slow-travel, beautiful rice terraces (with caution), yoga/meditation focus, excellent private drivers available.
    - Top spots: Ubud Monkey Forest (paved paths), Tegallalang Rice Terrace (view deck), Ubud Palace, Saraswati Temple, Campuhan Ridge Walk
    - Best time: Apr–Oct
    - Accessibility: ⭐⭐

11. **Bangkok, Thailand 🇹🇭** — Score: 7.8
    - Why: World-class hospitals (Bumrungrad, Bangkok Hospital — JCI accredited), affordable luxury, extensive food scene, many English speakers. Transit accessibility varies — use taxis/Grab.
    - Top spots: Grand Palace, Wat Pho, Chatuchak Market (morning, less crowded), Jim Thompson House, Asiatique Riverfront
    - Best time: Nov–Feb
    - Accessibility: ⭐⭐

12. **Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia 🇲🇾** — Score: 7.7
    - Why: English widely spoken, affordable, good hospitals, senior-friendly culture, KL free bus service (Go KL City Bus), many shopping malls with great accessibility.
    - Top spots: Petronas Towers & KLCC Park, Batu Cables (cable car), Islamic Arts Museum, Lake Gardens, KL Bird Park
    - Best time: Dec–Feb
    - Accessibility: ⭐⭐⭐

### Tier 4: Emerging Options (Score 7.0–7.4)

13. **Da Nang, Vietnam 🇻🇳** — Score: 7.5
    - Why: Beautiful beach city, flat coastline, modern airport, affordable resorts with accessibility, My Son ruins (some paths), good seafood, fewer crowds than Hoi An.
    - Top spots: My Khe Beach, Marble Mountains (elevator), Son Tra Peninsula, Dragon Bridge, Linh Ung Pagoda
    - Best time: Feb–May
    - Accessibility: ⭐⭐⭐

14. **Luang Prabang, Laos 🇱🇦** — Score: 7.3
    - Why: Laid-back UNESCO town, small walkable center, beautiful Mekong views, early morning alms-giving ceremony, many charming cafes. Limited medical but peaceful.
    - Top spots: Mount Phousi (200+ steps — skip if mobility limited), Kuang Si Waterfalls (lower pools), Royal Palace Museum, Night Market, Mekong cruise
    - Best time: Nov–Feb
    - Accessibility: ⭐⭐

15. **Siem Reap, Cambodia 🇰🇭** — Score: 7.2
    - Why: Angkor Wat is accessible with tuk-tuks, many accessible hotels, affordable, beautiful Tonle Sap lake, excellent food scene. Some temple steps are steep but main temples have options.
    - Top spots: Angkor Wat (ground level), Bayon Temple, Ta Prohm (paved central path), Angkor National Museum, Old Market area
    - Best time: Nov–Feb
    - Accessibility: ⭐⭐
