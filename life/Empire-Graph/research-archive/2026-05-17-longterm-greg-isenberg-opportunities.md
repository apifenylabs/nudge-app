## Pipeline Assessment Methodology

Each idea was scored against our operating constraints:
- **Zero domain cost** — must build on free Vercel tier or existing infra
- **AI solopreneur stack** — must leverage our existing skills (Next.js, Python, APIs)
- **Revenue model** — subscriptions, PDF sales, affiliates, or API pricing (no physical goods)
- **Existing infra** — does it piggyback on something we already built?
- **Time to first $** — can we ship revenue within 2 weeks?

## Scoring Legend

🟢 Building now | 🟡 Backlog (good fit, later) | 🔴 Scrap (wrong model/domain/cost)
