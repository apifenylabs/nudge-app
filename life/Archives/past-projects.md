# Past Projects — Failed Ideas & Lessons

## Common Failure Pattern
Most failed ideas died from **over-engineering before traffic**.

**The cycle:**
1. Exciting concept
2. Build beautiful architecture
3. Spend weeks polishing features
4. Launch to crickets
5. Abandon

**The lesson:** Ship fast. Monetize later. Prove traffic exists before investing in complexity.

## Notable Dead Ends

### Various "Beast" Directories
Several directory projects that got architecture-first treatment but never shipped to real users. Beautiful shells, empty shelves.

### App Factory
Over-engineered app generation idea. Spent too long on the builder, not enough on proving anyone wanted to use it.

## Lessons Captured
1. **Traffic first, polish second** — zero traffic sites don't need 10/10 design
2. **Affiliate revenue needs signups** — build the affiliate link system, but don't expect revenue until someone actually signs up
3. **Free tiers are your friend** — Vercel free tier can run everything. Don't pay for infrastructure until you have paying users
4. **One site working > ten sites half-built** — focus on getting 1 site to $100/mo before building #2
