## Aqua Orchestrator Bugs Found & Fixed (17:30-18:06 HKT)

### Bug 1: Execution path broken — `place_tpsl_group()` sends entry+TP/SL as single grouped call
- `HyperliquidConnector.place_tpsl_group()` was sending entry + TP + SL as one `grouping="normalTpsl"` bulk order
- **HL SDK returns `no_position`** because `normalTpsl` requires an existing position to anchor TP/SL orders
- Result: ALL live trades silently failed — ETH LONG ×4, SPX SHORT, WIF LONG all hit this
- **Legacy path** (`main_bot.py` + `set_tpsl_on_position`) does it correctly: entry first, then TP/SL in second call
- **Fix**: Rewrote `place_tpsl_group()` to split into 2 phases:
  1. `exchange.market_open()` for market entry
  2. `exchange.bulk_orders([TP, SL], grouping="normalTpsl")` after fill confirmed, reading actual fill size
- Still needs confirmation from CEO whether to test with micro-transaction or ship

### Bug 2: No per-strategy coin whitelist in Aqua orchestrator
- The orchestrator loops ALL symbols from `config.SYMBOLS` for ALL strategies
- This causes btc_stat_arb to scan SPX (not a crypto perp, CFG index) and other irrelevant coins
- Legacy config has `STAT_ARB_SYMBOLS`, `BB_1H_SYMBOLS`, `FUNDING_PROXY_SYMBOLS` etc.
- **Not fixed yet** — CEO didn't explicitly ask for this fix in the thread, he noted SPX and WIF shouldn't be there

### Bug 3: `oi_gate_check` crash on first ETH LONG signal (Jun 10 00:45)
- Filter chain crashed with `TypeError: cannot unpack non-iterable bool object`
- First ETH LONG signal aborted the cycle; subsequent cycles re-ran and hit Bug 1
- **Not fixed yet** — filter chain behavior in `run_filter_chain` when oi_gate_fn returns a malformed type

### Key Decision Record
- **SPX and WIF in prod signals**: CEO rightfully called this out. SPX isn't even a real crypto; legacy config `SYMBOLS` includes it but per-strategy allowlists filter it. Aqua doesn't have per-strategy filters.
- **btc_stat_arb ETH LONG**: Not "BTC only" — it's "alts vs BTC". ETH LONG at Jun 10 00:45 was a legitimate signal (z-score -2.7). Would NOT have been blocked by strategy logic.
- **CEO wants execution to mirror legacy exactly**: "did you not copy the execution methods and mistakes we've learned in legacy?" Valid — I deviated by trying to optimize the grouped call. Should have kept the 2-phase pattern.

### Next Steps (waiting CEO)
1. Confirm whether to micro-test the 2-phase fix or ship it
2. Need to backfill per-strategy coin whitelists in orchestrator (or CEO may do it as part of a bigger sweep)
3. Filter chain crash needs investigation — may be a `check()` import alias issue

# Late Session (22:03-22:54 HKT) — FVG Validation Failure + Liquidity Engine Built

## Critical Finding: FVG Fill Strategy DEAD on any timeframe with realistic execution
After CEO pushed back on my inflated numbers, I stress-tested every assumption:

### What I proved wrong:
1. **Initial 70-75% WR was look-ahead bias** — I was entering on the same candle the FVG filled (candle[i]), which means I knew the gap would fill before the candle closed. Realistic execution (enter at next candle open = candle[i]+1) drops WR to **38-42% with negative expectancy across ALL pairs**.

2. **All 5 entry modes tested**:
   - Mode 1 (limit order at gap midpoint during fill candle = 67-73% WR) — **look-ahead, not real**
   - Modes 2-5 (market entry at next candle open, various SL/TP rules) — **37-44% WR, all negative**
   - Only Mode 1 works and it's impossible to execute

3. **NY ORB + FVG combined (what CEO actually asked for):**
   - TAO hits 58% WR, +0.22R, 1.39 PF — barely positive
   - SOL, ETH, BTC: 52-54% WR, 1.07-1.17 PF — marginal
   - POPCAT: flat negative
   - **Not deployable**

4. **Root cause:** Crypto 15m fills gaps in 0-1 bars. By the time you can enter at next open, edge evaporated. This isn't Forex.

### 17m investigation (CEO's question about non-standard timeframes)
- 17m gave 64% WR with 1.78 PF but only 25 trades — **sample size too small to be meaningful**
- Verify split had 75-90% WR purely from tiny N (7-25 trades) — noise, not signal
- **No timeframe magic exists** — the gap-fill delay problem is inherent to crypto sub-1h

### Live order book analysis (22:37-22:54 HKT)
I built a fully working WebSocket L2 stream to HL:
- **3 simultaneous streams**: l2Book (114 snapshots/30s), trades (110 trades/30s), BBO
- SOL imbalance swung from -0.51 to +0.13 over 30s while mid held ~$65.58 — textbook accumulation
- Wall clustering detected: strong ask walls at $65.57-60 (1000-5000 contracts), weaker bids at $65.56-58 (500-2000)
- ETH mid tracked: $3530.85 → $3531.85

### What works differently
**The fix not in timeframes but in execution method:**
- Market orders at next open → loses (38-42%)
- **Limit orders at pre-calculated sweep levels** → likely wins because you enter BEFORE the move, not after
- The liquidity model tells WHERE price will sweep (wall levels), and we place limit entries there

### Built and running:
1. `hl_liquidity_engine_simple.py` — L2 stream, wall detection, imbalance tracking, trade flow
2. Wall detection: finds clusters >3x median size at each level
3. Trade flow tracking: real-time buy/sell direction detection
4. Imbalance calculation: rolling 20-snapshot

### Key Decision Record
- **"Why did you build this fast but not before?"** — Correct answer: I didn't. The previous builds (NY ORB standalone, FVG standalone) ALL failed when properly validated. The liquidity engine is the first thing that might work, and even this hasn't been backtested yet.
- **CEO explicitly wants**: "go do more back and fwd test", "prove it first, never believe wholeheartedly"
- **Hard lesson**: The 70% WR claim was from bugs (look-ahead bias). The real numbers are worse. Always check with 1-bar delay before reporting.
- **Cron status**: The crons reported NY ORB and NY MM as "built" before they were validated. CEO called this out. Fix: don't report strategies in heartbeat until forward-tested.
