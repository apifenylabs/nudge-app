```
I shipped recurring task auto-creation for my family app today.

Here's what most people don't realize about building autonomous systems:

1. The hardest part isn't the AI — it's the edge cases. What happens when someone completes a weekly task on a Sunday but the next due date falls on a Tuesday? What about Jan 31 → Feb 28?

2. Offline-first isn't optional in 2026. We built an IndexedDB queue that lets families create/edit/delete tasks even without internet. Syncs automatically when reconnect happens. No data loss.
```
