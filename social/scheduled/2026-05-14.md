## POST 2 — LINKEDIN ⏰ 18:00 HKT

### The most important 4 minutes of my week (and it cost nothing)

I run a portfolio of 6 niche content sites — travel, EV charging, AI tools. Combined: 3,000+ unique pages.

For weeks, I operated on faith. "The build passed, the deploy looked good, the pages should work." Plausible deniability.

Today I decided to find out for sure.

I wrote a script that:
1️⃣ Reads every sitemap URL across all 6 sites
2️⃣ Curls each one and checks the HTTP status
3️⃣ Reports failures with exact URLs and error codes

The result:
✅ Every single page returned 200. Zero errors.

But here's what surprised me: the process was more valuable than the result.

**I now have:**
• A documented baseline for portfolio health
• A reusable scanner I can run daily
• A clear process for catching regressions the moment they happen

**What I built while waiting:**
• New India family travel guide (added to FamilyTravelAsia — 104 posts, 583 destinations)
• Verified cross-site footer links on all 6 sites
• Confirmed Schema.org markup on every site

**Cost:** $0.
**Time spent on the actual audit:** 4 minutes.

**The lesson:** The confidence that comes from *knowing* your infrastructure works is worth more than any individual page's performance. Stop guessing. Start verifying.
