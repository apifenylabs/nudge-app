# The Aman Experience: 5 Most Family-Friendly Aman Resorts in Asia

Aman has long been synonymous with discreet, exquisite luxury. The brand's ethos — spaces that foster peace, privacy, and connection — translates remarkably well to family travel when you choose the right property. Unlike overtly *family-branded* resorts, Aman doesn't offer kids clubs with mascots or water parks. Instead, it offers something far more valuable: **space to be together, beautifully.**

## Key Takeaways
- Amanpuri in Phuket is the most family-adaptable property in the portfolio
- Multi-bedroom villas offer true family separation while keeping everyone together
- Expect to pay $1,200-3,500/night depending on villa size and season
- Best for families who value privacy, space, and cultural immersion over scheduled activities

## 1. Amanpuri, Phuket, Thailand

Aman's flagship since 1988 remains one of the most family-adaptable properties in the portfolio. The private pavilions and villas offer generous space — some with private pools and gardens where children can play freely. The beach is gentle and swimmable, and the property's cultural immersion program (Thai cooking classes, monk blessings, traditional dance) fascinates children aged 6 and above. The real draw for families? The multi-bedroom villas that let everyone have their own wing while sharing a common living area.

## 2. Aman Tokyo, Japan

This is the city hotel that changes how families experience Tokyo. Located in the Otemachi financial district, every suite offers floor-to-ceiling views of the Imperial Palace gardens and Mount Fuji on clear days. The spaciousness is extraordinary by Tokyo standards — even entry-level rooms are 55+ square meters. The concierge team can arrange private after-hours visits to teamLab Borderless, sumo stable visits, and kimono-wearing experiences that feel exclusive rather than touristy. Teens particularly love the 30-meter pool that feels suspended over the city.

## 3. Amankora, Bhutan

For families seeking something genuinely transformative, the five-lodge Amankora circuit through Bhutan is unmatched. Each lodge offers family suites, and the gentle hiking between valleys works well for children aged 8 and up. The resident monks at each property will give private blessings, and the hot stone baths at dusk are a ritual every family remembers forever. Bhutan's commitment to Gross National Happiness means children are welcome everywhere — temples, festivals, and restaurants alike.

## 4. Amanoi, Vietnam

Set on a protected bay in Ninh Thuan province, Amanoi offers some of the most family-friendly villa configurations in the Aman portfolio. The three-bedroom villas with private infinity pools are exceptionally popular with extended families. The spa has family treatment packages, and the national park adjacent to the property offers gentle hikes with endemic wildlife. Be prepared for the remoteness — this is a destination in itself, not a stopover.

## 5. Amanera, Dominican Republic

While technically in the Caribbean, Amanera deserves mention as Aman's most active property. For families with active older children (10+), the combination of surfing on Playa Grande, golf on the only course co-designed with a nature reserve, and canopy tours makes this the most adventure-ready Aman in the portfolio. The two-bedroom casitas with private plunge pools hit the sweet spot for families.

> **The Luxury Family Verdict:** Aman properties are exceptional for families who value privacy, space, and cultural immersion over scheduled activities. Book multi-bedroom villas at Amanpuri or Amanoi for the most seamless family experience. Expect to pay $1,200-3,500 per night depending on villa size and season.
