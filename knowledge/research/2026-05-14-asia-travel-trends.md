# Research: May 14, 2026 — Asia Travel Trends

## Sources
- TravelAgeWest: Asia Travel Trends 2026
- TravelAndTourWorld: Global Travel Trends 2026
- TTG Asia: Agoda survey on purpose-led travel
- The Senior List: Senior Travel Statistics 2026

## Key Findings

### 1. Asia = #1 Global Travel Growth Engine for 2026
- Eastern Asia + SE Asia = 31.7% of global demand growth
- Japan leads: travelers seeking beyond "Golden Route" (Tokyo → Kyoto)
- Vietnam emerging as major growth destination
- Source: Mabrian travel intelligence data

### 2. Senior Travel Market
- 37% of all travelers are seniors (2023 data)
- Cost and health remain barriers → opportunity for budget/content
- Senior tourism market growing substantially → validates Senior-Friendly Travel Asia
- **Implication:** Our senior content on budget travel, accessible hotels, and slow travel directly addresses real market needs

### 3. Purpose-Led Travel (Sustainability)
- 77% of Asian travelers consider sustainability important (up from 68%)
- Growing demand for community-supporting and eco-friendly experiences
- **Implication:** Add "sustainable family travel" and "eco-friendly activities" categories to Family Travel and Kids Activities sites

### 4. Family Travel Content Opportunities
- Malaysia = "most underrated family destination" (English-speaking, budget-friendly, Legoland)
- First-time Asia with kids → huge search opportunity
- **Implication:** Prioritize Malaysia content on Family Travel, Kids Activities, and Senior sites
