## Google AI Mode Impact on Travel Affiliate SEO

**Source:** [RankTracker](https://www.ranktracker.com/blog/google-ai-mode-for-travel-affiliates/)

AI Mode disrupts traditional travel affiliate SEO by generating itineraries, destination shortlists, "best places to stay" summaries directly in search results.

### What's Affected (CTR pressure):
- "Things to do in [city]" pages
- "[City] itinerary" content
- "Best time to visit [destination]"
- Generic "Top 10" listicles
- Basic hotel listicles

### What Still Works
Pages that AI Mode **can't replace**:
- **Booking-intent pages** ("Book [hotel] in [city] with family")
- **Specific logistics** ("How to get from Bangkok to Chiang Mai by EV")
- **Experience-led content** ("What it's actually like staying at [specific resort] with a toddler")
- **Validation content** (honest reviews, comparisons, specific product IDs)
- **Niche high-intent queries** (EV-friendly hotels in [city], kids clubs in [city])

### Recommendation
Shift content strategy toward:
1. **Booking-intent**: Create pages for specific hotel/activity bookings with explicit affiliate CTAs
2. **Unique data**: Station databases, property comparison tables, real pricing
3. **Experience content**: Personal narratives, specific recommendations with product IDs
4. **Cross-site booking funnels**: EV → hotels → activities → insurance
