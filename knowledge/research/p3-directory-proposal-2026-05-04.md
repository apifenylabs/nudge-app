## Proposal: Digital Nomad Family Asia

### Thesis
The intersection of "digital nomad" and "family with kids" is an underserved niche with strong commercial intent. Parents want to know: good internet, safe neighborhoods, international schools, family activities, coworking with kids, and family-friendly accommodation.

### Why This Niche
1. **Commercial intent** — Hotels, serviced apartments, coworking spaces, schools, and local services will pay for listings
2. **Search volume** — "best cities for digital nomads with family", "nomad family friendly accommodation [city]", "international schools Bangkok"
3. **Fragmentation** — No single directory dominates this space. Most content is on blogs/Substack
4. **Monetization** — Paid listings (coworkings, schools, hotels), affiliate (travel insurance, gear), featured spots
5. **Synergy** — Leverages existing family-travel directory codebase + knowledge of Asia

### Recommendation
**Digital Nomad Families Asia** (`digitalnomadfamilies.asia` or `nomadfamilies.asia`) — highest synergy with existing assets and strongest long-term monetization.

### Monetization Model
- Free basic listings for cities/neighborhoods
- Premium listings ($29-99/mo) for hotels, coworking spaces, schools
- Featured articles/sponsored content ($199)
- Affiliate links (travel insurance, gear, booking.com)
- Future: premium membership ($5/mo) for exclusive guides
