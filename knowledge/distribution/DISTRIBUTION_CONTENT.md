### Post 2: r/ElectricVehicles — EV Road Trip in Asia
```
Title: Did a 1,200km EV road trip across Southeast Asia in a rented BYD Atto 3 — AMA about charging infrastructure

Body: Just got back from driving Bangkok → Chiang Mai → Chiang Rai and back in a rental EV. Total: ~1,200km over 10 days with two kids in the back seat.

The good:
- Thailand actually has decent CCS2 coverage on major highways
- Charging cost was ~60% less than petrol for the same route
- Hotels with destination chargers are becoming common

The bad:
- PEA VOLTA stations sometimes have one busted stall
- Google Maps charging data is unreliable outside Bangkok
- You WILL need a backup plan for rural areas

The ugly:
- Renting an EV in most of SEA still requires calling 3-5 rental agencies
- Most hotel "EV charging" means a wall socket

Built a free directory of 1,125 EV charging stations across Asia with hotel recommendations: ev-charging-asia.vercel.app

Anyone else done long-distance EV travel in SEA? What was your experience?
```
