# App Factory Research Report - 2026-04-19

## Research Source: User-provided App Factory Researcher Blueprint

### **Key Framework: Greg Isenberg's Micro-SaaS Blueprint**
1. **Build niche internet audience first** – choose one platform, dominate it
2. **Create lead magnets** (free communities, templates, e-books) to capture emails
3. **Unbundle existing apps with 1 killer feature** – find what giants do poorly, make it your whole business
4. **Change pricing** – subscription fatigue is real; offer lifetime deals or pay-per-usage
5. **Automate everything** – AI handles 80% of development and marketing
6. **Tweet/share progress daily** – share revenue numbers for authenticity

### **App Portfolio Success Pattern**
- **Solo dev with 30-app portfolio:** $22k/month MRR
- **Strategy:** Shifted from single-app obsession to portfolio strategy
- **Approach:** Built fast, shipped fast, focused on essential core features only
- **MRVL Technologies (17 apps in 6 months):** "A portfolio compounds. Each new app is a chance to cross-promote the others. Users of one app discover two more. The network effect is slow but it's real — and it costs nothing"
- **Toss mini-app platform:** Solo developer launched 5 mini apps in one month → generated KRW 25 million (~$19k USD) monthly revenue

### **What Makes Apps Succeed (Research-Backed)**
From analyzing 27+ successful micro-SaaS examples:

| Success Factor | How to Apply |
|----------------|--------------|
| Razor-thin niche | Serve "Google Sheets add-on for Etsy sellers" not "CRM for everyone" |
| Solves a painful, specific problem | Users actively search for solution |
| One founder, lean operation | Low overhead = profitable at low MRR |
| Charges $10-$100/month | Or one-time $49-$99 for directories |
| Validated MVP in 1-2 weeks | Not months of development |
| ASO + organic growth | App store optimization drives majority of growth |
