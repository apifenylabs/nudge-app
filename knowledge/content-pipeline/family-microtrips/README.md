## Content Format (per microtrip)

### Family Travel Version
- 600-800 words
- Budget breakdown ($$)
- Kid-friendly activity highlights
- Where to eat (families)
- Logistics (public transport, taxis)
- Pro tip section

### Luxury Version
- 400-600 words
- Best luxury hotel in area
- Fine dining with kids (places that welcome children)
- Exclusive experiences
- Price range ($$$)
- When to book (seasonal)

### EV Version
- 300-500 words
- Charging stations along route
- Total range, elevation changes
- Family-friendly EV: space for car seats, trunk space
- Charging tips (off-peak, apps to use)
