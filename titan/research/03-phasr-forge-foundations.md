# TITAN — Phase 1 Foundations (Messages 2/3)

Last updated: 2026-05-20 | Source: Wosobu's Phase 1 research delivery

## 1. Titan Vision Statement
"Titan is the Solo Leveling Steam of the Agent Economy — the visual, gamified, certified operating system where every human builds, levels-up, and owns their personal swarm of god-tier autonomous agents."

- **Consumer**: Start as caretaker nurturing a cute digital pet agent → customize skins → unlock skill trees → graduate to full swarm orchestration. Agents run finances/crypto, book travel, manage work, plug into robot brains.
- **Creator**: Replit-style Skill Forge for non-coders and pros. Creators own IP; Titan takes marketplace/royalty fees only.
- **Enterprise**: Secure BYO manifests — employees onboard personal Titan swarm into corporate workspaces without IP leakage.
- **Future-Proof**: When robots arrive, Titan agents become the brain. First-mover god-tier agents rule personal economies.

**One-liner**: "Every human pivots from chatting with GPT like Google → building their own Titan swarm that makes them money and levels them up like Solo Leveling."
