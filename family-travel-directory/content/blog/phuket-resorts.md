# Best Family-Friendly Resorts in Phuket with Kids Clubs (2026)

Phuket is one of Asia's best family beach destinations — but not all resorts are created equal when it comes to kids. We've tested the top resorts with our own kids (ages 3 and 6) to find which ones truly deliver supervised care, engaging activities, and genuine parent downtime.

## Key Takeaways
- Club Med Phuket is the gold standard — kids' club runs 9 AM–9 PM with age-specific programs from 2–17
- Sunwing Kamala is purpose-built for toddlers and preschoolers with its own water park
- Holiday Inn Patong offers the best value with waterslides and lagoon pools starting at $100/night
- Always check age limits — most resort clubs start at age 4, so families with younger kids need Club Med or Sunwing

## What Makes a Great Kids Club?

- **Supervised hours** — at least 6 hours/day, including evening sessions
- **Age-appropriate programs** — separate activities for toddlers, kids, and tweens
- **Qualified childcare professionals** — trained staff, not just hotel employees filling in
- **Indoor-outdoor balance** — AC playrooms for hot afternoons, outdoor playgrounds for morning fun
- **Meals included** — some programs feed kids so parents can enjoy an adult dinner

## 1. Club Med Phuket

**Rating:** 5/5 — The Gold Standard

Club Med Phuket is purpose-built for families and sets the benchmark for kids' clubs in Asia.

**Kids Club Programs:**
- Petit Club (2–3 years): Supervised play, arts, music, naps
- Mini Club (4–10 years): Trapeze, sailing, arts and crafts, sports
- Passworld (11–17 years): Teens-only activities and social events

**Why it works:** Trained G.O.s (Gentle Organizers) keep kids engaged from 9 AM to 9 PM while parents enjoy adult-only spaces. The kids eat together too — genuine time off.

**Pool:** Freeform pool with kids' section and water features.
**Best for:** Families who want genuine time off while kids have a vacation of their own.
**Price:** ~$250–400/night all-inclusive. [Book Club Med Phuket on Klook](https://www.klook.com/search/?keyword=Club+Med+Phuket&aid=38VWJMX).

## 2. Anantara Vacation Club Mai Khao

**Rating:** 4.5/5

**Kids Club Programs:**
- Anantara Kidz Club (4–12 years): Thai boxing, fruit carving, Batik painting
- Supervised from 9 AM to 6 PM
- Evening babysitting available (extra cost)

**Unique Feature:** The longest lagoon pool in Phuket (300m) — kids can swim from their room to the restaurant.

**Best for:** Extended stays with apartment-style accommodation (full kitchen, separate bedrooms).
**Price:** ~$200–350/night for 2-bedroom.

## 3. Holiday Inn Resort Phuket (Patong)

**Rating:** 4.5/5

**Kids Club Programs:**
- Planet Trekkers (4–12 years): Video games, arts, outdoor activities
- Supervised from 8 AM to 6 PM

**Unique Feature:** Massive lagoon-style pool complex with waterslides. Kids' pool area has splash pads and fountains.

**Best for:** Families who want Patong Beach access with a resort bubble.
**Price:** ~$100–200/night — best value on this list. [Check Phuket family resorts on Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Phuket+Thailand&aid=2875669).

## 4. Sunwing Resort Kamala

**Rating:** 4.5/5

This resort is designed specifically for families with young children. The entire property is kid-focused.

**Kids Club Programs:**
- Sunwing Kids Club (1–12 years): Multiple age groups
- Water play area and dedicated kids' pool
- Daily activity schedule with shows, treasure hunts, and pool games

**Unique Feature:** Kids' water park with small slides, splash pads, and a pirate ship play structure.

**Best for:** Families with toddlers and preschoolers.
**Price:** ~$120–250/night.

## 5. JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa

**Rating:** 4/5

**Kids Club Programs:**
- Kids Club (4–12 years): Thai cooking, Batik painting, beach games
- Supervised from 9 AM to 5 PM

**Unique Feature:** Turtle release program at nearby Mai Khao beach. The resort grounds are massive with multiple pools.

**Best for:** Families who want a large resort with plenty of space and options.
**Price:** ~$150–350/night.

## 6. Centara Grand Beach Resort Phuket (Karon)

**Rating:** 4/5

**Kids Club Programs:**
- Kids Club (4–12 years): Daily activities, games, crafts
- Teens Club (13–17 years): Gaming, sports, movie nights

**Unique Feature:** Multiple pool areas including a dedicated kids' pool with water features. Direct beach access to Karon Beach.

**Best for:** Families who want a variety of dining and activity options.
**Price:** ~$130–280/night.

## 7. Kata Beach Resorts (Multiple Options)

Kata Beach has several family-friendly resorts clustered together:
- **Kata Thani** — Large pool, kids' club, direct beach access
- **Kata Palm Resort** — Multiple pools, family rooms, budget-friendly
- **Club Med Kata** — All-inclusive with excellent kids' club

**Best for:** Families who want easy beach and restaurant access in a family-focused area.

## Tips for Choosing a Phuket Family Resort

1. **Check kids club age limits** — some don't accept under 4
2. **Look for "Kids Stay Free" programs** — Holiday Inn and Sunwing offer this
3. **Consider location** — Mai Khao is quiet, Patong is busy, Kata is balanced
4. **Read recent reviews** — kids club quality can change with management
5. **Book directly** for best family packages with meals included
