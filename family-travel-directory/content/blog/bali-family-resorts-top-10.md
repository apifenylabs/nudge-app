# Top 10 Bali Family Resorts for 2026

Bali remains one of Asia's top family destinations — and for 2026, the island has more family-friendly resorts than ever. From sprawling Nusa Dua beachfront properties with dedicated kids' clubs to intimate Ubud jungle villas with private pools, Bali offers incredible value compared to the Maldives, Hawaii, or the Caribbean.

This guide covers the **10 best Bali family resorts** for 2026, with detailed breakdowns of kids' clubs, pools, rooms, dining, and pricing for a family of four.

---

## How We Selected These Resorts

Every resort on this list was evaluated on five family-specific criteria:
1. **Kids' Club Quality** — Activities, age ranges, languages spoken
2. **Pool Situation** — Dedicated kids' pools, depth ranges, water features
3. **Room Configuration** — Family suites, connecting rooms, extra bed policies
4. **Location Suitability** — Beach access, restaurants nearby, safety
5. **Value for a Family of 4** — Cost per night including breakfast

## 1. The Westin Resort Nusa Dua — Best Overall Value

**★★★★ | Family of 4: ~$200-350/night (incl. breakfast)**

The Westin Nusa Dua is consistently rated the best-value family resort in Bali. The **Westin Family Kids' Club** is one of the largest on the island (300+ sqm) with dedicated zones for toddlers (2-4), kids (5-8), and tweens (9-12).

**Why it stands out:**
- **Two waterslides** — one gentle for toddlers, one fast for older kids
- **Lagoon-style pool** with dedicated kids' section (0.3-0.6m depth)
- **Kids under 12 eat free** from the Prego kids' menu
- **Heavenly Beds** for parents; complimentary rollaway beds for kids
- EV charging available (4 x 22kW AC chargers)

**📍 Location:** Nusa Dua — beachfront, walkable to Bali Collection shopping

## 2. Padma Resort Ubud — Best Jungle Escape

**★★★★ | Family of 4: ~$150-250/night (incl. breakfast)**

Perched on a hill in Payangan, Padma Resort Ubud boasts Bali's most stunning infinity pool overlooking the jungle. The kids' club is excellent, featuring nature walks, organic gardening, and Balinese dance lessons.

**Why it stands out:**
- **Massive infinity pool** with a kid-friendly shallow section (0.4m)
- **Free kids' club** for ages 4-12 with twilight activities

- **Bicycle tours** through Ubud's villages and rice terraces
- **Incredible value** at $150-250/night for the quality

**📍 Location:** Payangan, northern Ubud — 60 min from airport

## Booking Tips for the Best Family Rates

1. **Book direct with breakfast included** — Most resorts include breakfast for the whole family when booked direct
2. **Watch for "Kids Stay Free" promos** — Club Med, Movenpick, and Westin frequently run these
3. **Booking.com Family Room filter** — Use the links above for pre-filtered searches
4. **Book 3-4 months ahead** for July-August (peak school holiday)
5. **Consider shoulder season** — May-June or September-October for the best weather and rates
6. **Ask about interconnecting rooms** — Many resorts will comp the second room for families with 2+ kids

## FAQ

### Are Bali resorts baby-friendly?
Yes. Most 4-5 star resorts offer baby cots, high chairs, bottle warmers, baby food on request, and professional babysitting ($5-10/hour).

### What's the best area for toddlers?
**Sanur** (flat, calm sea, cycling path) or **Nusa Dua** (gated security, shallow pools). Avoid Ubud's steep stairs and Uluwatu's cliffs for young kids.

### Do I need a car in Bali?
Not if you stay in one area. If you plan to visit multiple regions, hire a private driver (¥500,000-700,000/day ≈ $35-50).

### Is EV charging available?
Yes — all resorts listed above have EV charging on-site. Download the **Go-Ion** app to find additional stations.
