### Singapore with Kids: The Ultimate Family Itinerary for 5 Days

Are you planning a family vacation to Singapore with your kids? With its vibrant culture, world-class attractions, and kid-friendly activities, Singapore is an ideal destination for families. In this article, we'll share our ultimate 5-day family itinerary for exploring Singapore with your little ones.

### Day 1: Gardens by the Bay

Our first day in Singapore starts at the iconic Gardens by the Bay. This stunning park features over 1 million plants and trees from around the world, including towering Supertree structures that will leave your kids in awe. The park is easily accessible via public transportation, with several MRT stations nearby.

#### Tips for Visiting Gardens by the Bay with Kids:

*   **Stroller-friendly**: The gardens have wide pathways and stroller-friendly paths, making it easy to navigate with your little ones.
*   **Baby Care Facilities**: There are designated baby care facilities available at the park, equipped with changing tables, diaper-changing stations, and more.
*   **Food Options**: Enjoy a variety of dining options, including food courts, cafes, and restaurants serving international cuisine.

#### Must-see Attractions:

1.  Supertree Grove: Explore the largest collection of Supertrees in one place.
2.  Cloud Forest: Marvel at the stunning cloud-shaped structures and diverse plant species. [Book Gardens by the Bay tickets on Klook](https://www.klook.com/search/?keyword=Gardens+by+the+Bay+Singapore&aid=38VWJMX).
3.  Flower Dome: Discover over 1,000 types of plants from around the world.

**Hawker Food Pricing**: Expect to pay around SGD 5-10 (USD 3.50-7) per meal for a kid-friendly dish at hawker centers like Maxwell Food Centre or Newton Food Centre.

### Day 2: Zoo and River Wonders

On our second day, we head to the Singapore Zoo and River Wonders. The zoo is home to over 2,800 animals from around the world, with many species in naturalistic habitats. River Wonders offers a unique opportunity to explore the city's waterways while learning about its rich history and ecosystem.

#### Tips for Visiting Zoo and River Wonders with Kids:

*   **Stroller-friendly**: The zoo has wide pathways and stroller-friendly paths, making it easy to navigate with your little ones.
*   **Baby Care Facilities**: There are designated baby care facilities available at the park, equipped with changing tables, diaper-changing stations, and more.
*   **Food Options**: Enjoy a variety of dining options, including food courts, cafes, and restaurants serving international cuisine.

#### Must-see Attractions:

1.  Night Safari: Experience the thrill of nighttime adventures in a unique setting.
2.  River Safari: Take a boat ride along the Singapore River to explore its rich history and ecosystem.

**Budget Breakdown**:
*   Zoo admission: SGD 20 (USD 14) per adult, SGD 12 (USD 8.50) per child (3-12 years)
*   River Wonders admission: SGD 20 (USD 14) per adult, SGD 12 (USD 8.50) per child (3-12 years)

### Day 3: Sentosa Island

On our third day, we head to Sentosa Island for a fun-filled day of thrill rides and attractions.

#### Tips for Visiting Sentosa Island with Kids:

*   **Stroller-friendly**: Some attractions are stroller-friendly, but others may not be suitable due to height or accessibility constraints.
*   **Baby Care Facilities**: There are designated baby care facilities available at the resort, equipped with changing tables, diaper-changing stations, and more.
*   **Food Options**: Enjoy a variety of dining options, including food courts, cafes, and restaurants serving international cuisine.

#### Must-see Attractions:

1.  Universal Studios Singapore: Explore the Wizarding World of Harry Potter or experience thrilling rides like Battlestar Galactica.
2.  S.E.A. Aquarium: Discover an incredible array of marine life from around the world.

**Accommodation Options**: Consider staying at Sentosa Beach Hotel or Equinox Residences, both offering affordable and convenient accommodations for families. [Browse Singapore family hotels on Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Singapore&aid=2875669).

### Day 4: Chinatown and Kampong Glam

On our fourth day, we explore Singapore's rich cultural heritage in Chinatown and Kampong Glam. This historic district is home to vibrant street art, delicious food, and unique shopping experiences.

#### Tips for Visiting Chinatown and Kampong Glam with Kids:

*   **Stroller-friendly**: The area has narrow streets and alleys, so it's essential to plan ahead and choose stroller-friendly routes.
*   **Baby Care Facilities**: There are designated baby care facilities available at the district, equipped with changing tables, diaper-changing stations, and more.
*   **Food Options**: Enjoy a variety of street food and local cuisine at hawker centers like Maxwell Food Centre or Newton Food Centre.

#### Must-see Attractions:

1.  Chinatown Heritage Centre: Learn about Singapore's rich cultural heritage and historical significance.
2.  Kampong Glam Mosque: Explore this stunning mosque, one of the oldest in Singapore.

**Budget Breakdown**:
*   Hawker food meal: SGD 5-10 (USD 3.50-7) per person
*   Street art tour: SGD 20-30 (USD 14-21)

On our final day, we have the option to either explore more of Sentosa Island's attractions at Universal Studios Singapore or enjoy a relaxing free day in Singapore.
