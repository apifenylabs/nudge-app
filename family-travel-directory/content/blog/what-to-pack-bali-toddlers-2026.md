# What to Pack for Bali with Toddlers

Packing for a Bali trip with a toddler is a delicate balance. Bring too much and you're hauling a massive suitcase through the airport. Bring too little and you're overpaying at tourist shops in Seminyak.

The truth? **Bali has excellent shops and pharmacies.** You can buy most baby essentials there — often cheaper than at home. But some things are worth bringing from home.

Here's our definitive packing list for a Bali family holiday with a baby or toddler.

---

## The Golden Rule: Pack Light, Buy Local

Bali's major towns — Seminyak, Kuta, Sanur, Ubud, and Canggu — all have well-stocked:
- **Apotek** (pharmacies) — Baby formula, nappies (diapers), wipes, Calpol
- **Supermarkets** — Bintang Supermarket, Pepito Express — snacks, water, sunscreen
- **Baby stores** — Mothercare (Kuta), Baby Shop (Seminyak) — toys, clothes, swim nappies

You do NOT need to bring a month's supply of nappies. A 3-day supply to get started is plenty.

## What NOT to Bring

- ❌ **Big stroller** — The footpaths in Seminyak, Ubud, and Canggu are uneven
- ❌ **Too many nappies** — Buy Pampers at Pepito Express, cheaper than bringing
- ❌ **Heavy winter clothes** — Unneeded, even in "rainy season"
- ❌ **Baby bath** — All hotels have bathtubs or showers
- ❌ **One-time-use plastic items** — Bali has a plastic ban; bring reusable alternatives

## The Bali Baby Carrier Strategy

Bali footpaths are notoriously uneven, particularly in Ubud (temples, rice terraces) and Seminyak (narrow sidewalks with motorbikes). A lightweight stroller is fine for:
- Walking along Sanur beachfront path
- Shopping at Beachwalk Mall (Kuta)
- Resorts and hotel grounds

But for everything else, **bring a baby carrier**. The ErgoBaby, Tula, or a simple ring sling will save your back — and your sanity.

For toddlers aged 2+, a **backpack carrier** (like Osprey Poco) is excellent for Ubud's Campuhan Ridge Walk and the Tegalalang Rice Terraces. You can rent these in Bali, but bringing your own ensures a good fit.

## The Bali Medicine Kit

Don't rely on finding your preferred medications in Bali. Pack a small medical kit:

- **Children's paracetamol** (Panadol / Calpol)
- **Children's ibuprofen** (Nurofen)
- **Oral rehydration salts** (Hydralyte or similar)
- **Antihistamine** (Zyrtec / Claratyne) — for unexpected allergies
- **Hydrocortisone cream** (1% — for insect bites)
- **Band-aids** (various sizes)
- **Thermometer**
- **Tweezers** (for splinters — Bali has plenty of bamboo and wood)

Apotek (pharmacies) in tourist areas stock most basics, but brands differ and English labels aren't guaranteed.

## Quick Pre-Trip Checklist

- [ ] Check passport expiry (6 months validity needed for most nationalities)
- [ ] Apply for e-Visa on arrival (USD 35/person, children free)
- [ ] Book airport transfer through **Klook** (cheaper than taxi counters)
- [ ] Download Gojek app (for food delivery and taxi)
- [ ] Pack swim nappies and reef-safe sunscreen
- [ ] Charge tablet / phone with downloaded shows for the flight

## The Verdict

**Bring:** Sunscreen (your preferred brand), insect repellent, baby carrier, small first-aid kit, confirmation that your accommodation provides a cot.

**Buy in Bali:** Nappies, wipes, snacks, beach toys.

**Leave at home:** Your expectations of cleanliness and order — Bali is chaotic, beautiful, and worth every moment. Embrace the mess.
