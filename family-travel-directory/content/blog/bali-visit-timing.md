# Best Time to Visit Bali with Kids: Month-by-Month Guide (2026)

Planning a family trip to Bali but not sure when to go? Bali's tropical climate, school holiday crowds, and seasonal activities make timing critical — especially with kids. Get it right and you'll have empty beaches, perfect weather, and affordable prices. Get it wrong and you'll battle crowds, rain, and inflated rates.

## Key Takeaways
- **April and September** are the best months — shoulder season with great weather, moderate crowds, and fair prices
- **June–August** offers guaranteed sunshine but highest prices and crowds
- **January–March** has the lowest prices (40–60% below peak) but expect afternoon rain
- Sanur on the east coast is drier during wet season — a smart choice for rainy month visits

## Quick Overview

| Season | Months | Weather | Crowds | Best For |
|--------|--------|---------|--------|----------|
| Peak Dry | May–Sept | Sunny, 27–32°C | Very high | Beach, outdoor |
| Shoulder | Apr, Oct | Mix sun/rain | Medium | Best value |
| Wet Season | Nov–Mar | Rainy, 26–30°C | Low–Med | Budget |

## January — Wet Season, Low Crowds

**Weather:** Rainy season peak. Afternoon downpours (1–2 hrs). High humidity.
**Crowds:** Low — post-New Year lull.
**Activities:** Cooking classes, temple visits, museum days.
**Pros:** Lowest hotel prices of the year.
**Cons:** Flooding risk in Ubud and Seminyak.

## February — Wet Season

**Weather:** Similar to January. Nyepi (Day of Silence) falls on March 29 in 2026.
**Crowds:** Low except Chinese New Year.
**Activities:** Ubud Monkey Forest, cooking classes, waterfalls.
**Pros:** Best deals of the year.
**Cons:** Beach days interrupted by rain.

## March — Transition Month

**Weather:** Improving. Less rain, more sunshine.
**Crowds:** Low — one of the quietest months.
**Activities:** Mix of beach and culture.
**Pros:** Warm water, few tourists.
**Cons:** Still some rain.
*Pro tip:* Head to Sanur (east coast) — it's drier with calm waters for kids.

## April — Shoulder Season (Our Top Pick)

**Weather:** Dry season begins. Sunshine most days.
**Crowds:** Moderate — Easter brings some families.
**Activities:** Snorkeling, beach days, [Waterbom Bali tickets on Klook](https://www.klook.com/search/?keyword=Waterbom+Bali&aid=38VWJMX), temple visits.
**Pros:** Perfect weather, fewer crowds, great prices.
**Cons:** Some Easter price hikes.

## May — Start of Peak Season

**Weather:** Dry, sunny, beautiful.
**Crowds:** Increasing — schools start summer breaks.
**Activities:** Prime beach season, turtle release programs.
**Pros:** Guaranteed sunshine.
**Cons:** Prices rising.

## June — Peak Season

**Weather:** Perfect. Sunny and dry.
**Crowds:** Very high — northern hemisphere summer holidays.
**Activities:** All outdoor activities at 100%.
**Pros:** Perfect weather guaranteed.
**Cons:** Highest prices, most crowded.

## July — Peak Season

**Weather:** Dry and sunny. Coolest month (still 27°C+).
**Crowds:** Maximum.
**Activities:** Waterparks, beach clubs, Bali Arts Festival.
**Pros:** Coolest temperatures, cultural events.
**Cons:** Most expensive, most crowded.

## August — Peak Season

**Weather:** Dry and sunny.
**Crowds:** Still high.
**Activities:** Best surfing conditions, Bali Kite Festival.
**Pros:** Best beach conditions.
**Cons:** Crowded everywhere.

## September — Shoulder Season (Runner-Up Pick)

**Weather:** Still dry and sunny.
**Crowds:** Decreasing after August peak.
**Activities:** Great all-around month.
**Pros:** Best weather + decreasing crowds.
**Cons:** None significant.

## October — Shoulder Season

**Weather:** Some rain returns. Warmest water of the year.
**Crowds:** Low.
**Activities:** Swimming and snorkeling excellent.
**Pros:** Warmest water, few tourists.
**Cons:** Some rain.

## November — Wet Season Begins

**Weather:** Rain returns. Humidity climbs.
**Crowds:** Low.
**Activities:** Cultural focus — temple visits and cooking.
**Pros:** Very low prices.
**Cons:** Rain interrupts outdoor plans.

## December — Wet Season + Holidays

**Weather:** Rainy season. Can rain for hours.
**Crowds:** Christmas and New Year crowds spike.
**Activities:** Holiday celebrations, festive markets.
**Pros:** Festive atmosphere.
**Cons:** Rainy + crowded + expensive.

## The Verdict

Best months for families are **April and September** — shoulder season gives you the sweet spot of good weather, manageable crowds, and reasonable prices. If you need guaranteed sunshine, **June–August** delivers but costs more. For budget travelers, **February–March** offers 40–60% savings in exchange for afternoon rain.
