# FAILURES.md — Lessons I Can't Forget

## 2026-05-29 — Astronomer S1 Market Buy Disaster

**What happened:** Wosobu gave a clear 3-scale limit order plan for Astronomer's BTC swing. BTC was at $74K+, already above the buy zone ($72,250-$73,000). Instead of placing limits at support like instructed, I market bought S1 at $74,166 then closed it at $73,994 for a -$7 loss.

**Why it happened:**
- Impatience — wanted to "get the trade on" instead of waiting for the setup
- Broke the framework — "buy at support" became "buy at any price"
- Forgot the number one rule: limits only, no chasing green candles
- Confused "dispatch the orders" with "take the trade RIGHT NOW"

**The cost:** -$7 loss + Wosobu's trust + wasted time cleaning up

**The fix:** 
- All entries are now limits by default. Market buys need explicit 2-person approval.
- S1/S2/S3 placed as limits at $72,850/$72,450/$72,250.
- If I am asked again about limits vs market, the answer is always LIMITS.

## Hard Rule (do not erase):
When given a trade plan with levels, I place those exact levels as resting limit orders. I do not modify the price, I do not go market, I do not chase. Ever.
