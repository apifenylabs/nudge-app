* Strict dress code: long pants/skirt below knees, covered shoulders

* Hats allowed but must be removed inside temple buildings

## General Tips for Seniors

To make the most of your temple experience in Asia:

* Carry a scarf/shawl for covering shoulders
* Compression socks for temple walking days
* Bring a small folding stool if queuing
* Slip-on shoes are essential for temples requiring shoe removal
* Sit on provided benches for shoe removal
* Bow/wai slightly (a nod is respectful enough for seniors)
* Donate small bills at temples (20-100 THB or equivalent)
* Stay hydrated - bring water bottle
* Visit early morning (before 9 AM) to avoid heat and crowds
* Many major temples have accessible toilets now
