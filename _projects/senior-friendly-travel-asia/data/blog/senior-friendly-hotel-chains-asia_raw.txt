## What Seniors Should Look For in Hotel Rooms

As a senior traveler, you want to ensure your accommodations are comfortabl
comfortable and safe. Here are the key things to look for in hotel rooms:

- Roll-in showers vs bathtubs (bathtubs with grab bars are ideal)
- Grab bars near toilet
- Room near elevator on quiet floor (ask for 'quiet floor' and 'near elevat
elevator')
- 24-hour room service
- English-speaking staff at reception
- Good lighting (many Asian hotel rooms are dim)
- Non-slip bathroom floors
- Visual fire alarms for hearing impaired
- Lowered peepholes and closet rods

- Join loyalty programs before booking

- Consider 'Club' floors for quieter atmosphere and included meals

## Red Flags

Be aware of these common issues when booking a hotel:

- Rooms with steps inside (common in some Asian hotels)
- No elevator access (ask before booking budget hotels)
- Glass shower doors without grab bars
- Deep bathtubs with no railings
- Rooms far from elevator on long corridors

#### Avoid:
Rooms with steps or lack of accessibility features
Hotels without elevators or accessible rooms
Deep bathtubs or glass shower doors without grab bars
Long corridors between rooms and the elevator"
