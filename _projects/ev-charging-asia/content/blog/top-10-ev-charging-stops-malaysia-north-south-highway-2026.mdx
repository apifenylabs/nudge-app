# Top 10 EV Charging Stops on Malaysia's North-South Highway (2026 Guide)

**Last updated: May 17, 2026**

Malaysia's North-South Expressway (PLUS) is Southeast Asia's most EV-ready highway. Spanning 740 km from Johor Bahru to Penang, it now features over 50 EV charging stations at R&R stops — more than any other highway in the region. In 2026, Malaysia passed the milestone of 2,500 public charging stations, with PLUS accounting for the densest concentration.

We've ranked the top 10 charging stops along the PLUS Highway based on charger speed, amenities, location, and family-friendliness. These are the stops you'll want to plan your journey around.

## 1. Sungai Perak U — KM 195 (Northbound)

**Charging Score: 9.8/10** | **Chargers:** 4× DC 200 kW | **Connectors:** CCS2 | **Payment:** Shell Recharge or Touch 'n Go eWallet

The undisputed king of PLUS highway charging. Sungai Perak U (Utara = Northbound) opened in late 2024 and immediately became the benchmark for all Malaysian highway charging stations.

- **Charging experience:** Four ultra-fast 200 kW DC chargers mean even at 80% capacity, two stalls are available. A 15-minute charge adds 200 km+ for most EVs.
- **Amenities:** Massive air-conditioned food court (15+ stalls), clean family bathrooms with baby-changing stations, surau, dedicated children's playground with shaded seating, 24-hour convenience store
- **Kid highlights:** The playground is fenced and shaded — let kids burn energy while you charge. The food court has a KFC and McDonald's with indoor play zones.
- **Traffic tip:** Northbound is busiest on Fridays 2-6 PM and eve of public holidays. Southbound peaks Sundays 12-5 PM.

---

## 2. Pagoh B — KM 162 (Southbound)

**Charging Score: 9.7/10** | **Chargers:** 3× DC 180 kW | **Connectors:** CCS2 | **Payment:** Shell Recharge

Pagoh B is the gateway charging station for anyone driving from Singapore to KL. It's the first major R&R after the Johor stretch and a must-stop for EVs leaving Singapore.

- **Charging experience:** Three 180 kW Shell Recharge units. Typically uncongested on weekdays. A 15-minute top-up gives you enough for the remaining 200 km to KL.
- **Amenities:** The R&R has a 7-Eleven, Family Mart, Burger King, Starbucks, clean toilets with bidet spray, and a large sheltered seating area.
- **Kid highlights:** The outdoor playground has swings and a slide. The large open field next to the R&R is perfect for a post-charge stretch.
- **Pro tip:** From Singapore, this is the ideal first charge. Charge before eating — the 15-minute charge is done before your food arrives.

---

## 3. Ayer Keroh B — KM 197 (Southbound)

**Charging Score: 9.5/10** | **Chargers:** 2× DC 150 kW + 2× AC 22 kW | **Connectors:** CCS2, Type 2 | **Payment:** ChargEV / JomCharge

Ayer Keroh is the exit for Malacca and one of the most popular stops on the PLUS highway. The two DC chargers are supplemented by AC for overnight stays.

- **Charging experience:** 150 kW DC fast chargers mean 80% in 20 minutes for most EVs. The AC chargers are perfect if you're staying overnight at a nearby Malacca hotel.
- **Amenities:** Large R&R with Kentucky Fried Chicken, Old Town White Coffee, a souvenir shop, clean surau, and petrol station with tyre pump.
- **Kid highlights:** The animatronic dinosaur display at the R&R entrance is a hit with younger kids. Great photo opportunity.
- **Pro tip:** If you're skipping Malacca, this is still a worthy bio-break stop. The bathrooms are reliably among the cleanest on the PLUS highway.

---

## 4. Rawang B — KM 430 (Southbound)

**Charging Score: 9.4/10** | **Chargers:** 4× DC 200 kW | **Connectors:** CCS2 | **Payment:** Shell Recharge

Rawang is the northern gateway to Kuala Lumpur, and this charging station is a recent addition with cutting-edge 200 kW capability.

- **Charging experience:** Four ultra-fast chargers in a covered bay. 200 kW means some EVs can go from 20-80% in under 15 minutes. The station has a dedicated waiting lounge.
- **Amenities:** Brand new R&R with food court, A&W, Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf, baby room, and EV lounge with Wi-Fi.
- **Kid highlights:** The R&R has a dedicated kids' corner with colouring books and a small indoor play space.
- **Pro tip:** Use this as your last charge before entering KL city traffic. Arrive in KL with 80%+ battery for a worry-free city stay.

---

## 5. Tapah R&R — KM 305

**Charging Score: 9.3/10** | **Chargers:** 2× DC 120 kW + 2× AC 22 kW | **Connectors:** CCS2, Type 2 | **Payment:** ChargEV / JomCharge

Tapah sits at a strategic junction — it's the gateway to Cameron Highlands, making it essential for anyone heading into the mountains.

- **Charging experience:** 120 kW DC is fast enough for a 20-minute top-up. The AC chargers are ideal for those continuing to Cameron Highlands — charge fully here before the mountain ascent.
- **Amenities:** The Tapah R&R has a large food court with Malay, Chinese, and Indian stalls, a 7-Eleven, and a surau.
- **Kid highlights:** The R&R is next to the Tapah River — you can see monitor lizards sunbathing on the riverbank (from a safe distance). Kids love this.
- **Pro tip:** Charge to 90%+ here if heading to Cameron Highlands. The 45 km mountain climb consumes 15-20% extra battery compared to highway driving.

---

## 6. Ipoh Selatan R&R — KM 274

**Charging Score: 9.2/10** | **Chargers:** 2× DC 120 kW | **Connectors:** CCS2 | **Payment:** JomCharge

Ipoh Selatan is perfectly positioned for an Ipoh day trip or as a mid-point charge between KL and Penang.

- **Charging experience:** Two 120 kW DC chargers in a covered bay. Typically has short queues on weekends but moves quickly.
- **Amenities:** The R&R has excellent food options — the nasi lemak here is famous among locals. Clean toilets, surau, and minimart.
- **Kid highlights:** Ipoh Selatan's snack shop sells white coffee candy and tau fu fah (soy pudding) — a treat after hours in the car.
- **Pro tip:** Jump off the highway and drive 5 km into Ipoh town. The Concubine Lane and Old Town area has fantastic street art and the best white coffee in Malaysia.

---

## 7. Seremban A — KM 257 (Northbound)

**Charging Score: 9.1/10** | **Chargers:** 2× DC 180 kW | **Connectors:** CCS2 | **Payment:** Shell Recharge

Seremban A is one of the newer charging stations on the PLUS highway, offering fast 180 kW capability at the Negeri Sembilan junction.

- **Charging experience:** The 180 kW chargers are fast and rarely busy — most travellers from KL to Singapore skip Seremban in favour of Pagoh.
- **Amenities:** Food court with local favourites, Family Mart, clean bathrooms, surau.
- **Kid highlights:** Seremban is famous for its seafood — the R&R food court has a seafood corner that kids enjoy.
- **Pro tip:** A hidden gem for southbound travellers. Most people rush past Seremban to Ayer Keroh or Pagoh, but this station is consistently less crowded.

---

## 8. Juru B — KM 148 (Southbound)

**Charging Score: 9.0/10** | **Chargers:** 2× DC 150 kW | **Connectors:** CCS2 | **Payment:** ChargEV

Juru is the Penang gateway — it's the last major charging stop before crossing the Penang Bridge. Essential for anyone heading to Penang Island.

- **Charging experience:** Two 150 kW chargers. A 20-minute charge here ensures you can explore Penang without range anxiety.
- **Amenities:** Large R&R complex with McDonald's, 7-Eleven, food court, surau, and a car wash.
- **Kid highlights:** The R&R has a small arcade with claw machines and racing games — classic highway fun.
- **Pro tip:** Don't skip this charge even if your battery looks okay. The Penang Bridge crossing uses more battery than expected, and Penang's inner-city chargers are slower (22 kW AC mostly).

---

## 9. Gunung Semanggol — KM 220

**Charging Score: 8.8/10** | **Chargers:** 2× DC 100 kW | **Connectors:** CCS2 | **Payment:** JomCharge

Gunung Semanggol sits between Ipoh and Penang in Perak. The 100 kW chargers are adequate but slower than the newer installations.

- **Charging experience:** 100 kW means 20-80% takes about 25 minutes. The chargers are well-maintained but older models.
- **Amenities:** Basic R&R with a food court, convenience store, and surau. Less impressive than the premium stops above.
- **Kid highlights:** The nearby Gunung Semanggol hill is visible from the highway and makes a good conversation starter about volcanoes (it's an extinct limestone hill).
- **Pro tip:** Use this as a backup if Juru or Ipoh Selatan are full. The drive between Ipoh Selatan and Juru is only 126 km — you can easily skip this stop.

---

## 10. Yong Peng B — KM 115 (Southbound)

**Charging Score: 8.7/10** | **Chargers:** 2× DC 100 kW | **Connectors:** CCS2 | **Payment:** ChargEV

Yong Peng is the main charging stop in northern Johor, serving travellers between Singapore and KL. It's a reliable mid-point charge.

- **Charging experience:** 100 kW DC chargers. A 30-minute charge gives you 200 km of range — sufficient for the remaining 200 km to Singapore.
- **Amenities:** Standard R&R with food court, 7-Eleven, surau, and clean toilets.
- **Kid highlights:** The R&R's food court has a roti canai stall where kids can watch the dough being hand-tossed.
- **Pro tip:** If you're heading to Singapore, charge here to avoid the expensive electricity at Singapore chargers (SGD 0.55-0.70/kWh vs MYR 0.50-1.20/kWh).

## Essential Tips for PLUS Highway EV Charging

- **Peak hours cost more:** Some networks (Shell Recharge) charge higher rates on weekends and public holidays. Check before plugging in.
- **Plan for the 200 km gap:** The stretch between Yong Peng (KM 115) and Pagoh (KM 162) has no DC chargers — only 47 km, but if you miss Yong Peng, you'll be in trouble.
- **Bring a Type 2 cable:** Some older ChargEV stations only provide the wall unit — you need your own cable.
- **Download before you go:** Cellular coverage is excellent along the entire PLUS highway, but some apps (especially JomCharge) require downloads before departure.
- **Hot weather = reduced range:** Malaysia's 33-35°C heat means more A/C usage. Expect 10-15% range reduction in extreme heat.
