**Last updated: May 27, 2026**

## Detailed Rankings

### 1. The Ritz-Carlton Hong Kong ⭐ 9.5/10

**Charging:** 120 kW DC (CCS2) + 22 kW AC (Type 2) — Free for guests

Hong Kong's highest hotel (floors 102-118 of the ICC) also has the **best EV charging facilities** in the city. A rare Hong Kong hotel with DC fast charging, managed by valet for a fully seamless experience. Fully charges a Tesla Model Y LR from 10% in about 35 minutes.

**Best for:** Luxury travellers who want the best charging in the city with 5-star service.

### 2. The Murray Hong Kong ⭐ 9.3/10

**Charging:** 100 kW DC (CCS2) + 22 kW AC (Type 2) — Free for guests

Hong Kong's most iconic heritage-to-modern hotel conversion pairs landmark architecture with practical EV infrastructure. The concierge team is trained on EV procedures, and the Central location is ideal for business travellers.

**Best for:** Business travellers and couples wanting a design-forward stay with reliable EV charging.

### 3. Four Seasons Hong Kong ⭐ 9.2/10

**Charging:** 120 kW DC (CCS2) + 22 kW AC (Type 2) — Free with valet

As expected from Four Seasons, the EV experience is seamless — you never touch the cable. Drop your keys, and your car is charged and ready when you return. Connected to IFC Mall for shopping while you charge.

**Best for:** Ultra-luxury family stays and travellers who want zero-effort EV charging.

### 4. W Hong Kong ⭐ 9.0/10

**Charging:** 150 kW DC (CCS2, Tesla compatible) — Free for guests (valet)

W Hong Kong has the **fastest hotel EV charger in Hong Kong** at 150 kW DC — faster than most public charging stations in the city. Can add 200+ km of range in 15 minutes. Connected to ELEMENTS mall with additional AC chargers.

**Best for:** Design-conscious travellers and young families who want the fastest charging in town.

### 5. The Upper House ⭐ 8.8/10

**Charging:** 4 x 22 kW AC (Type 2) — Free for guests

The most awarded boutique hotel in Hong Kong, with the most EV charging bays in this list's top tier. Pacific Place connected for shopping. Wide EV bays designed for driver door clearance — a rarity in Hong Kong.

**Best for:** Boutique hotel lovers and family travellers wanting multiple charging options.

### 6. Grand Hyatt Hong Kong ⭐ 8.5/10

**Charging:** 22 kW AC (Type 2) — HKD 50/hr

Convention business powerhouse with reliable AC charging. Parking attendants monitor EV bays and move non-EV cars. Walking distance to HKCEC, Admiralty, and Wan Chai waterfront.

**Best for:** Business travellers attending HKCEC events and overnight guests.

### 7. Kerry Hotel Hong Kong ⭐ 8.3/10

**Charging:** 6 x 22 kW AC (Type 2) — Free for guests

Leads the **family-friendly EV category**. With six AC charging bays — the most of any Hong Kong hotel by bay count — and a wide, uncrowded car park, this is the safest bet for guaranteed charging.

**Best for:** Families with young kids and anyone prioritising charging availability.

### 8. Rosewood Hong Kong ⭐ 8.2/10

**Charging:** 90 kW DC (CCS2) + 22 kW AC (Type 2) — Free with valet

Stunning harbour views with fast DC charging on the Kowloon side. Integrated with K11 MUSEA for additional AC chargers. Butler-level service extends to EV charging.

**Best for:** Couples and luxury travellers wanting Kowloon side access with DC.

### 9. Hotel ICON ⭐ 8.0/10

**Charging:** 22 kW AC (Type 2) — Free for guests

Punches above its weight — free AC charging with no time limits. Room rates 60-70% lower than comparable TST luxury hotels. Close to Hung Hom MTR and East Rail Line for Shenzhen arrivals.

**Best for:** Budget-conscious EV travellers and visiting academics.

### 10. Holiday Inn Golden Mile ⭐ 7.5/10

**Charging:** 7 kW AC (Type 2, single-phase) — Free for guests

Free, no-frills AC charging at the most affordable hotel on this list (from HKD 800/night). Central TST location near Star Ferry and Harbour City. 24-hour attendant parking.

**Best for:** Budget travellers and short overnight stays.
