# Top 10 EV Road Trip Destinations from Hong Kong (2026 Guide)

**Last updated: May 17, 2026**

Hong Kong is the perfect launchpad for EV exploration into mainland China's Greater Bay Area and beyond. With the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge and deep integration of charging networks across the Pearl River Delta, an EV from Hong Kong can now reach Guangzhou (2 hours), Zhuhai (90 minutes), and even Guilin (5 hours) without range anxiety.

We've ranked the top 10 EV road trip destinations from Hong Kong based on driving distance, charging infrastructure, family appeal, and border crossing practicality.

## 1. Shenzhen — China's EV Capital (50 km)

**Trip Rating: 9.8/10** | **Duration:** 2-3 days | **Difficulty:** Easy

Shenzhen has more EVs than any city outside Shanghai — over 1 million by 2026. The city's charging infrastructure is mind-boggling: over 50,000 public chargers with 95% uptime.

- **Route:** Hong Kong → Shenzhen Bay Port → Nanshan / Futian
- **Charging:** BYD, TELD, and State Grid stations everywhere. Shenzhen has one charger for every 3 registered EVs.
- **Kid highlights:** Window of the World (miniature landmarks), Splendid China Folk Village, OCT Harbour Ferris Wheel, Shenzhen Science Museum
- **Where to stay:** The Ritz-Carlton Shenzhen (chargers in carpark) or InterContinental Shenzhen

## 2. Guangzhou — Cathedral & Dim Sum (180 km)

**Trip Rating: 9.6/10** | **Duration:** 2-3 days | **Difficulty:** Easy

The capital of Guangdong province offers world-class dim sum, UNESCO-worthy architecture, and family-friendly attractions galore. The Guangzhou-Shenzhen-Hong Kong corridor has the densest charging network in China.

- **Route:** Hong Kong → Shenzhen → Nansha Bridge → Guangzhou
- **Charging:** TELD DC stations at every highway rest stop. Guangzhou city has 20,000+ chargers.
- **Kid highlights:** Chimelong Safari Park (drive-through safari), Canton Tower observation deck, Chen Clan Academy, Guangzhou Zoo — one of China's best
- **Where to stay:** The Garden Hotel Guangzhou (EV charging) or Four Seasons Guangzhou

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [The Garden Hotel Guangzhou](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=The+Garden+Hotel+Guangzhou&aid=2875669)
**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Four Seasons Guangzhou](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Four+Seasons+Guangzhou&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** Visit Chimelong on a weekday to avoid crowds. The hotel has dedicated EV parking with chargers.

## 3. Zhuhai & Macau via the Bridge (70 km)

**Trip Rating: 9.5/10** | **Duration:** 2-3 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate

The spectacular Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge (55 km) is an EV marvel. The bridge itself has monitoring systems and emergency charging support, though you won't need it for the 45-minute crossing.

- **Route:** Hong Kong → HZMB Port → Zhuhai → Macau
- **Charging:** HZMB parking has DC chargers. Zhuhai city has excellent TELD and State Grid coverage.
- **Kid highlights:** Chimelong Ocean Kingdom (the world's largest aquarium), Zhuhai Beach, Macau Tower, Macau Science Centre
- **Where to stay:** Hilton Zhuhai (chargers available) or The Ritz-Carlton Macau

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Hilton Zhuhai](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Hilton+Zhuhai&aid=2875669)
**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [The Ritz-Carlton Macau](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=The+Ritz-Carlton+Macau&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** You cannot drive a Hong Kong-registered car into Macau without special permits. Park in Zhuhai and walk across the border, or take the ferry from Shekou.

## 4. Huizhou & Xunliao Bay Beach (150 km)

**Trip Rating: 9.3/10** | **Duration:** 2-3 days | **Difficulty:** Easy

Huizhou's west coast beaches are Hong Kong's closest escape. Xunliao Bay offers 27 km of white sand beaches, and the drive is a scenic coastal route.

- **Route:** Hong Kong → Shenzhen → Yanba Highway → Xunliao Bay
- **Charging:** State Grid chargers at the Xunliao Bay visitor centre. The Yanba Highway rest stops also have DC chargers.
- **Kid highlights:** Xunliao Bay beaches (calm swimming), hot springs at Huidong, Huizhou West Lake
- **Where to stay:** Marriott Xunliao Bay Resort (has EV charging) or Crowne Plaza Huizhou

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Marriott Xunliao Bay Resort](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Marriott+Xunliao+Bay+Resort&aid=2875669)

## 5. Foshan & Zhaoqing — Lingnan Culture (250 km)

**Trip Rating: 9.1/10** | **Duration:** 3-4 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate

Foshan is the home of Cantonese opera, kung fu (Ip Man, Bruce Lee), and Lingnan architecture. Zhaoqing's Seven Star Crags is a stunning limestone karst landscape.

- **Route:** Hong Kong → Guangzhou → Foshan → Zhaoqing
- **Charging:** TELD stations in Foshan (plentiful) and Zhaoqing city centre.
- **Kid highlights:** Bruce Lee Ancestral Hall, Foshan Martial Arts Museum, Zhaoqing Seven Star Crags, Dinghu Mountain
- **Where to stay:** Hilton Foshan (EV charging) or the New World Hotel Zhaoqing

## Hong Kong → Mainland China Border Guide

### Vehicle Permits

| Permit Type | Cost | Validity | Processing Time |
|-------------|:----:|:--------:|:--------------:|
| Guangdong plate | CNY 30,000-50,000 | Lifetime | 1-2 weeks |
| Temporary import permit | ~HKD 1,500 | 7 days | 2-3 days |
| Hong Kong-plated car permit | HKD 5,000+ | 1 year | 2-4 weeks |

For most travellers, the **temporary import permit** via a licensed agent is the most practical option. Costs ~HKD 2,000 including agent fees.

### Required Documents

- Hong Kong vehicle registration
- Third-party insurance valid in mainland China (CNY 300+)
- Valid driving license (Hong Kong + International Driving Permit)
- Vehicle inspection certificate (valid within 6 months)
- Border crossing permit from China Customs

### Best Border Crossings

| Crossing | Best For | Wait Time (Weekday) | Notes |
|----------|:--------:|:-------------------:|-------|
| Shenzhen Bay Port | Nanshan, Guangzhou | 20-40 min | Busiest but fastest processing |
| Lok Ma Chau | Futian, Longgang | 15-30 min | Good for Shenzhen north |
| HZMB Zhuhai | Western GBA | 10-20 min | Best for Zhuhai/Macau |
| Sha Tau Kok | Eastern GBA | 10-15 min | New, quiet crossing |

## EV-Specific Tips for China Trips

### Charging in China 🇨🇳

- **China uses GB/T standard** — you'll need a **CCS2-to-GB/T adapter** for your Hong Kong EV. Tesla has GB/T adapters at service centres.
- **AutoNavi (Gaode Maps)** is essential — it shows charger locations, real-time availability, pricing, and integrates WeChat Pay.
- **TELD (T Good)** is the largest charging network in southern China with over 50,000 stations.
- **State Grid** is the backup network — more expensive but ubiquitous.
- **Supercharging speeds** are real: 350 kW+ chargers are common at highway rest stops. Shenzhen has 600 kW chargers in some locations.

### Apps to Download

| App | Purpose | Required For |
|-----|---------|:------------:|
| AutoNavi (Gaode) | Navigation + charging | Essential |
| TELD | Charging network | Southern China |
| State Grid e-charge | Backup network | Nationwide |
| WeChat | Payment | All services |
| Alipay | Payment | Back up WeChat |

### Essential Gear

| Item | Why |
|------|-----|
| CCS2-to-GB/T adapter | Required for non-GB/T vehicles |
| Chinese SIM card | China Mobile or China Unicom (data + calls) |
| VPN | For access to Google/WhatsApp/Instagram |
| WeChat with Chinese payment | For all tolls, parking, charging |
| Phone mount | AutoNavi navigation is essential for China driving |
