# Bangkok EV Charging Guide 2026: Complete City Guide

Bangkok's EV charging infrastructure has undergone a remarkable transformation. As of mid-2026, the Thai capital boasts over **4,000 public charging points** across more than **800 locations**, making it Southeast Asia's most EV-friendly metropolis. From luxury mall charging to expressway fast-charging hubs, here's everything you need to know.

## Key Takeaways

- **4,000+ charging points** across Bangkok — more than any other Southeast Asian city
- **Free charging at major malls**: Siam Paragon, CentralWorld, EmQuartier, Iconsiam
- **CCS2 is the standard** — most DC fast chargers deliver 50–350 kW
- **PTT Station EA Anywhere** has the widest highway coverage; Haup leads in the city center
- **Cost**: ฿0–9/kWh ($0–0.25), with many malls offering free charging as a customer perk

## Best Places to Charge in Bangkok

### 🏬 Shopping Malls (Free or Low-Cost Charging)

Bangkok's mega-malls are the crown jewel of the city's EV infrastructure. Most offer **free EV charging** to shoppers:

- **Siam Paragon** — B2 level dedicated EV zone, 22 kW AC, free for shoppers. 10+ bays with attendants managing queue
- **CentralWorld** — 50+ EV bays across multiple levels including DC fast chargers at Zone A car park. Free AC charging
- **EmQuartier / Emporium** — 7 kW AC free charging at B2 and B3, 8 bays each. Get there early on weekends
- **Iconsiam** — Free 22 kW AC charging at basement parking. Spectacular riverfront while you wait
- **Central Embassy** — Free dedicated EV bays with 22 kW AC. Ultra-luxury setting, rarely full
- **The Mall Thapra / Bangkapi** — EA Anywhere partnership, 50 kW DC fast chargers available for a fee

> 💡 **Pro tip**: Arrive at malls before 11 AM on weekends to guarantee a charging bay. Weekday evenings after 7 PM are also competitive at Siam Paragon and CentralWorld.

### ⛽ PTT Station Fast Chargers

PTT has aggressively deployed **350 kW ultra-fast chargers** at their Blue Station network:

- **PTT Vibhavadi-Rangsit** — 350 kW, 4 bays, 15 min to 80%
- **PTT Rama II** — 150 kW, ideal for heading south
- **PTT Bang Na-Trat** — 2 × 150 kW, strategic for Suvarnabhumi Airport access
- **PTT Srinakarin** — 120 kW, serves the east side of the city
- **PTT Ngamwongwan** — 150 kW, covers the northern suburbs

### 🛣️ Expressway & Highway Charging

For getting in and out of Bangkok, these stations are strategically located:

- **EA Anywhere Bang Pa-In** — 150 kW, at the start of the northbound route
- **EA Anywhere Rama II** — 120 kW, perfect for southbound departures
- **Haup Bang Na** — 120 kW, arterial for Pattaya-bound EVs
- **Charge+ Don Mueang** — 60 kW, near the airport for last-minute top-ups

## Charging Apps You Must Have in Bangkok

1. **EA Anywhere** — The most comprehensive map. Supports RFID card payment
2. **Haup** — Best for city-center stations. Supports QR and credit card
3. **PTT Blue** — Required for PTT Blue Station 350 kW chargers
4. **MG Charge** — Needed for MG-branded stations (but open to all EVs)
5. **Charge+** — Good for hotel and condo chargers
6. **Google Maps** — Now shows real-time EV charger availability in Bangkok

> 💡 **Download all of them before you arrive**. Network fragmentation means you'll need 3–4 apps to access all charging options.

## Where to Avoid Charging

- **On-street public chargers in Old City (Rattanakosin)** — sparse, mostly 7 kW AC, often ICEd
- **Khao San Road area** — no dedicated EV infrastructure yet
- **China Town (Yaowarat)** — extremely limited, tight streets make parking difficult
- **Weekend Chatuchak Market** — nearby Or Tor Kor market has chargers but huge queues on weekends

## EV Parking Tips for Bangkok

1. **Always use a mall's dedicated EV bay** — not all parking spaces marked "EV" have live charging (look for the charging cable)
2. **Register with EA Anywhere for RFID** — the card lets you tap and charge without fumbling with apps
3. **Weekend mall charging** — arrive by 10:30 AM or be prepared to wait 15–30 minutes for a bay at Siam Paragon
4. **Suvaranabhumi Airport** — Airport Rail Link's Makkasan station has EA Anywhere chargers. Long-term parking has limited EV bays — book in advance
5. **Don Mueang Airport** — EA Anywhere station nearby, but no dedicated EV parking in airport lots yet
6. **Check charger status before driving** — Google Maps and EA Anywhere app both show live availability

## Best EV for Bangkok

For navigating Bangkok's traffic, a midsize EV with **400+ km real-world range** is ideal. Top picks among Bangkok EV owners:

- **BYD Atto 3 / Dolphin** — most common EV on Bangkok roads, excellent parts availability
- **MG ZS EV / MG4** — strong dealer network with dedicated charging partnership
- **Tesla Model Y** — CCS2 compatible, no Tesla Supercharger dependency needed
- **Neta V** — budget option, popular with ride-hailing drivers
- **Great Wall Motor Ora Good Cat** — stylish city car with surprisingly good range

> All major EV brands have dealerships and service centers within 10 km of central Bangkok.
