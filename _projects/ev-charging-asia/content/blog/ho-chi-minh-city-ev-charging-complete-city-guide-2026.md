# Ho Chi Minh City EV Charging Complete City Guide 2026

Ho Chi Minh City — still called Saigon by everyone who lives here — is a city of 9 million people and 8 million motorbikes, where the energy is electric in every sense of the word. And in 2026, "electric" means something new: with over 80 public charging points across 5 competing networks and Vietnam's most concentrated VinFast density, Saigon has quietly become Southeast Asia's most practical city for EV ownership.

This guide covers every district, every network, every hotel with a plug, and every road trip you'll want to take from Saigon. Whether you drive a VinFast VF 9, a Tesla Model Y, a BYD Atto 3, or an MG4 — whether you live in a penthouse in Landmark 81, a villa in Thao Dien, or an apartment in Phu My Hung — this is your complete EV survival manual for Saigon 2026.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **80+ public charging points** across HCMC, concentrated in District 1, District 7 (Phu My Hung), Binh Thanh (Landmark 81 area), Tan Binh (airport corridor), and Thu Thiem
- **5 major networks**: V-Green (VinFast), EVOne, EBOOST, EVN, and private hotel/apartment chargers
- **Cost to full charge**: VND 165,000–330,000 (USD $6.80–13.60) depending on network and charger type
- **Best areas for EV owners**: Thao Dien (An Phu), Phu My Hung (Dist 7), and Thu Thiem (Dist 2) — wide streets, villa parking, and growing charger density
- **Hotel charging**: 10+ luxury hotels with dedicated EV parking, mostly free for guests
- **Apartment charging**: 20+ condo buildings in An Phu, Thao Dien, Phu My Hung, and the VinCity complexes have resident EV charging
- **VinFast VF series dominates** (~75% of HCMC EVs), but CCS2 is widely available for Tesla, BYD, MG, BMW i-series, and other imports
- **Road trip ready**: V-Green chargers now cover routes to Da Lat, Nha Trang, Vung Tau, Can Tho, and the Cambodian border

## EV Charging Networks in Ho Chi Minh City

### 1. V-Green (VinFast) — The Dominant Network

V-Green is Vietnam's largest EV charging network, and in HCMC it's the undisputed king. With roughly 50+ public DC charging locations across the city, you're never more than 3 km from a V-Green charger in the central districts.

**Connector:** GB/T (VinFast standard). CCS2 adapter available for non-VinFast vehicles.
**Power:** 60 kW DC standard, 120–180 kW at newer installations
**Locations:** Vincom malls, VinFast showrooms, petrol station forecourts, VinCity residential compounds, and AEON malls

| Location | District | Chargers | Power | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Vincom Center Landmark 81 | Binh Thanh | 8x DC | 60 kW | Vinhomes Central Park, massive parking |
| Vincom Center Thu Duc | Thu Duc (Dist 2 area) | 6x DC | 60 kW | Covers new east-side developments |
| Vincom Plaza Go Vap | Go Vap | 4x DC | 60 kW | North Saigon coverage |
| VinFast Store Nguyen Van Troi | Phu Nhuan | 4x DC | 60 kW | Airport corridor, usually available |
| VinFast Store Cong Hoa | Tan Binh | 6x DC | 120 kW | Near airport, newest DC installation |

| AEON Mall Binh Tan | Binh Tan | 6x DC | 60 kW | Covers western districts |

**Pricing:** VND 3,500/kWh (USD $0.14) at DC fast chargers. Free parking for first 2 hours at most Vincom locations when charging.

**How to use:** Download the V-Green app (iOS/Android), register with Vietnamese phone number, top up via bank transfer, Momo, or VNPay. Plug, scan QR code, authenticate.

## Area-by-Area Charging Guide

### District 1 (Central Saigon)

District 1 is HCMC's commercial, tourist, and business heart — and despite the narrow streets, dense traffic, and limited parking, it's actually well-covered for EV charging if you know where to go.

**Best options:**
- **Vincom Center Dong Khoi** (V-Green) — 6x DC 60 kW, basement B2, central
- **Saigon Centre / Takashimaya** (EVOne) — 4x DC 240 kW, fastest in Dist 1
- **The Reverie Saigon** (EVOne) — 2x DC 120 kW, valet charging available
- **Park Hyatt Saigon** (EVOne) — 2x DC 120 kW, dedicated EV bay
- **InterContinental Saigon** (EVOne) — 2x DC 120 kW
- **Sheraton Saigon** (EVOne) — 2x DC 120 kW
- **EBOOST Trang Tien area** — 2x DC 60 kW, near Notre-Dame

**Pro tip:** Saigon Centre (Takashimaya) basement B2 has 4x 240 kW EVOne chargers — the most powerful in the city center. A 15-minute charge at 240 kW adds 120+ km range. Park here, shop Takashimaya, eat in the basement food court, and you'll never need a dedicated charging stop in Dist 1.

**Watch out for:** Vincom Dong Khoi gets congested on weekends — queues form at the 6 chargers. If it's busy, ride the elevator up to Takashimaya (5-minute walk through the B2 tunnel connector).

---

### District 2: Thu Thiem & An Phu (Thao Dien)

District 2 was merged into Thu Duc City in administrative terms, but everyone still calls it District 2. This is Saigon's expat and luxury residential heart — and the fastest-growing EV district.

**Thu Thiem New Urban Area:**
Wide boulevards, modern planning, basement parking in every building. The Sala urban area has EBOOST AC charging throughout. EVOne has a station at the Thu Thiem bridge approach.

**Best options:**
- **EVOne An Phu** — 2x DC 120 kW, next to the Thu Thiem tunnel
- **EBOOST Sala** — 4x AC 22 kW
- **EBOOST The Sun Avenue** — 4x AC 22 kW
- **Vincom Thu Duc** (V-Green) — 6x DC 60 kW
- **British International School** (EVOne) — 2x DC 120 kW, public access on weekends

**Thao Dien (An Phu):**
This is the EV epicentre of Saigon. Hundreds of expat families drive EVs here, and villa driveway charging is becoming the norm. The streets are wide enough for parking, and most villas have gated driveways perfect for granny chargers or wall boxes.

**Best options:**
- **EVOne An Phu** — 2x DC 120 kW
- **EBOOST Cantavil** — 4x AC 22 kW (walking distance to Thao Dien restaurants)
- Thao Dien Green community — growing private charger sharing among neighbours

**Pro tip:** Thao Dien is the best district in HCMC for non-VinFast EV ownership. Wide streets, villa parking, and a close-knit community that shares charger info via WhatsApp and Facebook groups. If you're buying an EV in Saigon and can choose your neighbourhood, choose Thao Dien.

### District 3

D3 is a compact, central district with French colonial architecture, great food, and surprisingly good charging options.

**Best options:**
- **EVN HQ (Nguyen Dinh Chieu)** — 2x DC 60 kW
- **VinFast Store (Nguyen Van Troi)** — 4x DC 60 kW (corner of D3 and Phu Nhuan)
- **Melia Saigon** — 2x Type 2 AC, guest access
- **Hotel Nikko Saigon** — 2x Type 2 AC, guest parking

**Pro tip:** District 3's narrow streets make public parking a challenge. If you're staying in D3, choose a hotel with EV charging (see hotel section below). For visitors dining on the famous Nguyen Thuong Hien street food strip, the nearest reliable charger is at Vincom Dong Khoi (8-minute Grab ride away) — charge before you arrive.

### District 7 (Phu My Hung)

Phu My Hung is Saigon's most planned urban area — wide streets, dedicated parking, and a growing EV culture. This is arguably the easiest district in all of Vietnam for EV ownership.

**Best options:**
- **Crescent Mall** (V-Green) — 4x DC 60 kW
- **SC VivoCity** (V-Green) — 6x DC 120 kW — largest mall south Saigon
- **Nguyen Van Linh Petrol V-Green** — 4x DC 120 kW
- **EBOOST Saigon South** — 6x DC 60 kW
- **EBOOST Him Lam** — 4x AC 22 kW
- **Renaissance Riverside** — 2x Type 2 AC

**Pro tip:** SC VivoCity on Nguyen Van Linh is the best all-round charging destination in Saigon: 6x 120 kW DC V-Green chargers, massive parking, and the widest mall in the south. Charge while you catch a movie, eat at the food court, or let the kids play at the indoor playground. Park on B1 for closest charger access.

### Binh Thanh (Landmark 81 Area)

Vinhomes Central Park, anchored by the 461-metre Landmark 81 tower, is a self-contained EV world. This master-planned residential complex has more EV charging than some entire Vietnamese provinces.

**Best options:**
- **Vincom Center Landmark 81** (V-Green) — 8x DC 60 kW
- **Saigon Pearl** (EVOne) — 4x DC 120 kW
- **EBOOST Binh Thanh Hub** — 4x DC 60 kW
- **Vinhomes Central Park residential** — 10+ shared AC chargers for residents

**Pro tip:** If you live in Vinhomes Central Park, you essentially never need to charge outside the complex. The Vincom mall has 8 V-Green chargers, Saigon Pearl has 4 EVOne chargers, and the residential buildings have shared EBOOST AC options. Landmark 81 residents get priority at the Vincom chargers.

---

### Tan Binh (Airport Area)

Tan Son Nhat International Airport is Vietnam's busiest. EV charging here is practical but not abundant — plan ahead.

**Best options:**
- **VinFast Store Cong Hoa** (V-Green) — 6x DC 120 kW, newly upgraded
- **EBOOST Tan Son Nhat Airport** — 2x DC 60 kW, departure level
- **EVN Tan Binh** — 2x DC 60 kW (on Cach Mang Thang Tam, 5 min from airport)
- **VinFast Store Nguyen Van Troi** — 4x DC 60 kW (Phu Nhuan, near airport)
- **EBOOST Go Vap** — 4x DC + 4x AC, largest in north Saigon

**Pro tip for airport runs:** If you're heading to the airport to fly out, don't park at the airport chargers (expensive short-term parking). Instead, charge at VinFast Cong Hoa (120 kW, 15 min for 40%) on your way in, then park. The Cong Hoa store is 3 km from the airport — charge and go.

## Apartment & Villa EV Charging

### Residential Complexes

HCMC's apartment EV charging scene is the best in Vietnam, driven by the city's high concentration of master-planned residential communities.

**Vinhomes complexes** (Vinhomes Central Park/Binh Thanh, Vinhomes Grand Park/Dist 9, Vinhomes Golden River/Thu Thiem) all have V-Green chargers in visitor parking plus growing resident-installed options. VinCity Grand Park (Dist 9) has 12 DC V-Green chargers — an entire charging hub within a residential complex.

**Phu My Hung compounds** (Him Lam, Sunrise City, Sky Garden, Riverside) have EBOOST AC charging scattered throughout. The Phu My Hung management has been proactively installing shared chargers as demand grows.

**Thu Thiem developments** (Sala, The Sun Avenue, Estella Heights) all have EBOOST AC and selected DC charging. Thu Thiem's newer buildings (post-2023 construction) are required to include EV charging infrastructure.

### Villas in An Phu & Thao Dien

This is where HCMC EV owners have the best experience. Thao Dien and An Phu villas typically have:

- **Gated driveway**: Perfect for a 7–22 kW wall box
- **Granny charging**: Standard wall outlet in the garage or carport — slow but works overnight
- **Shared street parking**: Several Thao Dien streets now have discussion groups about installing shared on-street chargers

**Installation process for villas:**
1. No building management approval needed (you own the villa)
2. Install a 7–22 kW Type 2 wall box (approx. VND 12–20 million / USD $500–830)
3. Local electrician can wire it in 2–4 hours
4. Recommended brands: Zencar, Teison, or Tesla Wall Connector (available via Shopee Vietnam or Lazada)
5. Home electricity rate: VND 2,000–2,500/kWh (USD $0.08–0.10) — cheapest charging option

**Installation for apartments:**
1. Get written approval from building management (some require certified electricians)
2. Install wall box in your designated parking spot
3. Many buildings use a shared meter — expect VND 4,000–5,000/kWh (USD $0.17–0.21)
4. Cost: VND 15–25 million (USD $620–1,030)

**Pro tip for Thao Dien:** Many villa owners in Thao Dien and An Phu simply run a weatherproof outdoor extension cord from their house to the carport. With a Type 2 or granny charger, you get 2.3–7 kW overnight. For villa dwellers, this covers 95% of daily driving needs. You'll only use public DC chargers for road trips.

## Tips for Non-VinFast EV Owners in HCMC

Saigon is VinFast country. With ~75% of local EVs being VF 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9 models, the charging ecosystem is optimised for them. Here's how to survive and thrive with a Tesla, BYD, MG, or BMW i-series:

### 1. Get a CCS2-to-GB/T Adapter — Immediately

V-Green chargers use GB/T (Chinese standard, VinFast's chosen connector). You need a CCS2-to-GB/T adapter to charge at 50+ locations across Saigon. Without it, you're limited to EVOne, EBOOST, and EVN stations.

**Where to buy:**
- Tesla official adapter (approximately USD $300)
- Third-party adapters on Shopee/Lazada Vietnam (VND 3–8 million / USD $125–330)
- EVOne sometimes stocks adapters at their service locations

**Price note:** "Tesla chính hãng" (genuine Tesla adapter) on Shopee runs VND 7–9 million. Chinese third-party options work fine but check for active thermal management. Don't buy the cheapest one — you'll risk charging speed throttling.

### 2. Prioritise EVOne & EVN Stations

EVOne has native CCS2 and charges at 120–240 kW. For non-VinFast EVs, this is your primary fast-charging network in Saigon. Saigon Centre Takashimaya (4x 240 kW) and The Reverie Saigon (2x 120 kW) are your go-to central spots.

### 3. Hotel Charging Strategy

Book hotels with EVOne CCS2 chargers, not just "EV charging." Many hotels advertise "EV parking" but only have a granny outlet. The Reverie, Park Hyatt, InterContinental, Sheraton, and New World all have dedicated EVOne CCS2 DC chargers — book these.

### 4. Join the HCMC EV Owner Facebook Groups

The two essential groups:
- **"HCMC EV Owners"** (cộng đồng xe điện Sài Gòn) — 5,000+ members, real-time charger availability updates
- **"VinFast EV Club HCMC"** — useful even as a non-VinFast owner since V-Green network issues get reported here first

### 5. Carry a Type 2 Cable

Many hotel and apartment chargers are Type 2 AC. Your car likely came with one — keep it in the boot. If not, buy one on Shopee (VND 1–2 million / USD $40–85).

## Road Trip Connections from Ho Chi Minh City

### HCMC → Vung Tau (Distance: 100 km, Drive time: 2–2.5 hrs)

The closest beach escape from Saigon. Easy EV journey — you don't even need a full charge.

**Charging en route:**
- V-Green Long Thanh — 6x DC 180 kW (20 min from HCMC, 180 kW ultra-fast)
- V-Green Vung Tau — 4x DC 60 kW at Vincom Center Vung Tau
- EVOne at various Vung Tau hotels

**Pro tip:** Charge to 80% at V-Green Long Thanh on the way out of Saigon — 15 minutes at 180 kW for 40% charge. No need to charge in Vung Tau unless you're staying multiple days. The 220 km round trip uses about 40–50% of a standard 75 kWh battery.

### HCMC → Da Lat (Distance: 300 km, Drive time: 6–7 hrs)

The mountain city of Da Lat is a popular weekend escape. The road climbs from near sea level to 1,500 metres, with twisty mountain roads — expect 15–20% higher consumption on the climb.

**Charging en route:**
- V-Green DC (60 kW) at Dong Nai or Bao Loc — midway stop (150 km from HCMC)
- V-Green DC Da Lat — 4x DC 60 kW at Vincom Plaza Da Lat
- EVOne at select Da Lat hotels (Ana Mandara, Dalat Palace)

**Pro tip:** Charge fully in HCMC before departing. The midway stop at Bao Loc's V-Green station is essential — don't skip it. The mountain climb from Bao Loc to Da Lat (60 km of continuous incline) will drain your battery faster than expected. Arrive in Da Lat with 50%+ to navigate the hilly city.

### HCMC → Nha Trang (Distance: 430 km, Drive time: 6–7 hrs)

The coastal city of Nha Trang is a classic Saigon road trip destination. This follows QL1A highway with good V-Green coverage.

**Charging en route:**
- V-Green DC Phan Thiet (180 km from HCMC) — 4x DC 60 kW
- V-Green DC Ca Na (280 km) — 4x DC 60 kW
- V-Green DC Nha Trang — 8x DC 60 kW at Vincom Plaza Nha Trang
- EVOne Nha Trang (select hotels)

**Pro tip:** The best approach is to charge at Phan Thiet (coffee + bathroom break) then again at Ca Na (beach stop + lunch). The coastal highway past Ca Na has stunning sea views — you'll want to stop for photos anyway. Full read on the route in our [Vietnam EV Charging Guide 2026](/blog/vietnam-ev-charging-guide-2026).

### HCMC → Can Tho & Mekong Delta (Distance: 170 km, Drive time: 3 hrs)

The Mekong Delta is perfectly accessible for EVs now. Can Tho, the delta's largest city, has V-Green coverage.

**Charging en route:**
- V-Green DC at Trung Luong (halfway, 85 km) — 4x DC 60 kW
- V-Green DC Can Tho — 6x DC at Vincom Plaza Can Tho
- V-Green DC My Tho (optional first stop, 70 km)

**Pro tip:** The HCMC → Can Tho route is flat, smooth highway. You can comfortably do the round trip (340 km) on a single full charge in most modern EVs, but charge at Vincom Can Tho while you explore the Cai Rang floating market for peace of mind. For deeper Mekong exploration (Ben Tre, Tra Vinh, Soc Trang), charge in Can Tho first — small town charger coverage is sparse.

### HCMC → Phnom Penh & Cambodia (Distance: 230 km, Drive time: 4–5 hrs including border crossing)

Cross-border EV travel from HCMC is now feasible — but requires planning.

**Charging en route:**
- V-Green DC at Moc Bai border (90 km from HCMC, just before the border)
- EVN DC at Moc Bai (backup)
- V-Green DC or EVOne in Phnom Penh — growing but limited network
- Phnom Penh chargers: AEON Mall Phnom Penh (V-Green 60 kW), Exchange Square (EVOne 120 kW)

**Border crossing tips:**
- GB/T standard is used by both Vietnam and Cambodia (same as China)
- CCS2-to-GB/T adapter still needed for non-VinFast vehicles
- Vehicle import paperwork required — allow 1 hour at the border
- Download Cambodia's EV app (GO+) before crossing — cellular signal may change

## 6 Insider Tips for EV Living in HCMC

1. **Rainy season charging strategy (May–October)** — Saigon's monsoon floods certain areas badly. Know your chargers: avoid basement chargers in Districts 1, 3, and Binh Thanh during heavy rain (Vincom Dong Khoi ground-floor chargers are safer). V-Green's Nguyen Van Linh chargers in District 7 are flood-safe — elevated forecourt.

2. **Never trust the app to show all chargers** — HCMC EV owners maintain a private Google Map with ~15 undocumented chargers (hotel back-lot outlets, friendly villa owners, back-alley EBOOST units). Join Zalo/Facebook EV groups to get access.

3. **Early bird charging at Vincom Dong Khoi** — The 6 V-Green chargers at Vincom Dong Khoi fill up by 10:00 AM on weekdays and 09:00 AM on Saturdays. If you want a spot, arrive when the mall opens (09:30). By noon, queue wait is 20–40 minutes.

4. **Airport strategy: charge before you park** — For flights, charge at VinFast Cong Hoa (120 kW, 15 min, 3 km from airport), then Grab to terminal. Avoid the EBOOST airport charger — expensive short-term parking fees eat your savings.

5. **Thursday night is charging night in Thao Dien** — The Thao Dien farmers' market and Thursday night street food market in An Phu attract huge crowds. Restaurants on Xuan Thuy and Nguyen Van Huong streets often let EV owners plug into their wall outlets for the evening. Ask: "Cho toi sac xe dien nho mot ti duoc khong?" — most say yes.

6. **Mekong Delta weekend trips: charge at Trung Luong** — The halfway point between HCMC and Can Tho (Trung Luong rest stop) has 4x V-Green 60 kW chargers with a clean coffee shop. This is your must-stop for any delta trip. Skip it and you'll be nervous in My Tho.

## Final Verdict

Ho Chi Minh City in mid-2026 is the best city in Vietnam — and arguably Southeast Asia — for EV ownership. The charging density, the variety of networks, the luxury hotel support, and the practical apartment/villa charging options create a genuinely convenient environment for daily EV living.

The city's three best charging destinations cover 90% of needs:

- **Central Saigon drivers** → Saigon Centre Takashimaya (EVOne, 4x 240 kW)
- **Phu My Hung & south Saigon** → SC VivoCity (V-Green, 6x 120 kW)
- **Binh Thanh & Vinhomes residents** → Vincom Landmark 81 (V-Green, 8x 60 kW)

Thao Dien and Phu My Hung remain the most EV-friendly neighbourhoods in all of Vietnam — wide streets, villa parking, home charging, and a community that actually shares information. If you're buying an EV in Saigon and have a choice of where to live, those two areas will make your life significantly easier.

The road trip network is growing fast. Da Lat, Vung Tau, Can Tho, and Nha Trang are all practical day or weekend trips. The cross-border route to Phnom Penh is the first of its kind in Southeast Asia.

With 80+ public charging points and more being installed every month, Saigon has crossed the threshold from "possible with planning" to "just works." The motorbikes still rule the streets. But the cars? They're going electric.
