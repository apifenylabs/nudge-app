# Bali to Lombok by EV: Complete Guide to the Ferry Crossing + EV Charging [2026]

Bali and Lombok are two of Indonesia's most iconic islands — Bali with its surf breaks, rice terraces, and spiritual energy; Lombok with its raw volcanic landscapes, pristine beaches, and the world-class Mandalika MotoGP circuit. But until recently, taking your EV between them required careful planning and a tolerance for uncertainty.

In 2026, the Bali–Lombok EV corridor is finally practical. PLN (Indonesia's state utility) has expanded charging infrastructure on both islands, the Padangbai–Lembar ferry is EV-friendly, and Lombok's charging network is growing to support the Mandalika tourism zone. This guide covers everything: the ferry crossing, charging on both sides, battery strategy, itineraries, costs, and local tips.

## Route Overview: Padangbai (Bali) → Lembar (Lombok)

The only vehicle ferry route between Bali and Lombok departs from **Padangbai** (east Bali) and arrives at **Lembar** (southwest Lombok). This is a roll-on/roll-off (Ro-Ro) ferry operated by ASDP Indonesia Ferry — the state-owned ferry company.

| Detail | Information |
|--------|------------|
| **Route** | Padangbai (Bali) → Lembar (Lombok) |
| **Ferry type** | Ro-Ro (roll-on/roll-off) vehicle ferry |
| **Distance (sea)** | ~38 km across the Lombok Strait |
| **Crossing time** | 3-4 hours (public Ro-Ro) |
| **Vehicle fare (one way)** | Rp 250,000-400,000 (~$16-25 USD) |
| **Passenger fare (per person)** | Rp 30,000-50,000 (~$2-3 USD) |
| **Frequency** | 8-12 departures daily (6 AM – 8 PM) |
| **Operator** | ASDP Indonesia Ferry |

### Ferry Types: Public Ro-Ro vs Fast Boat

| Type | Duration | Vehicle Allowed? | EV-Friendly? | Best For |
|------|----------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| **Public Ro-Ro ferry** | 3-4 hours | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes (booked as vehicle) | Taking your own EV to Lombok |
| **Fast boat (passenger only)** | 1.5-2.5 hours | ❌ No | N/A | Leaving EV parked on Bali |

**Important for EV drivers:** Only the **public Ro-Ro ferry** accepts vehicles. Fast boats (operated by companies like Ekajaya, Scoot, and Bluewater Express) are passenger-only — they depart from Padangbai and Serangan (south Bali) and arrive at Bangsal (north Lombok) or Senggigi, but they cannot carry cars.

### Ferry Schedule & Booking Tips

- **Departures:** Ferries run roughly every 60-90 minutes from 6 AM to 8 PM. Morning departures (6-9 AM) are the most reliable for shorter wait times
- **Booking:** You can buy tickets at the Padangbai ferry terminal on the day, but during peak season (June-August, December-January, Easter), book **3-7 days in advance** through the ASDP Ferry app or at the terminal counter
- **EV-specific booking:** When booking, specify that you're driving an EV. ASDP does not have a formal EV policy (as of 2026), but they assign vehicle deck space based on size. Most standard EVs (Tesla Model Y, BYD Atto 3, Hyundai Ioniq 5, MG4) fit without issues
- **Arrival time:** Arrive at Padangbai 45-60 minutes before departure for check-in, vehicle weighing, and loading
- **Return trip:** The same route runs in reverse — Lembar → Padangbai. Same schedule applies. Book your return crossing at the same time if you have fixed dates

## Pre-Ferry Charging on Bali

Bali has the best EV charging infrastructure in Indonesia outside Java, with **80+ charging stations** concentrated in the south coast (Kuta, Seminyak, Canggu, Sanur) and Ubud. However, Padangbai is on the **east coast of Bali** — about 2 hours from the main charging hub of South Bali. Plan your last charge before heading east.

### Recommended Last Charge Before Padangbai

| Charger | Location | Type | Power | Distance to Padangbai | Best For |
|---------|----------|------|-------|----------------------|----------|
| **PLN SPKLU Amankila** | Manggis (Candidasa area) | DC Fast | 60 kW | 30 min (20 km) | Closest DC to Padangbai, resort setting |
| **PLN SPKLU Candidasa** | Candidasa main road | DC Fast | 50 kW | 35 min (25 km) | Easy access from the coastal road |
| **ChargeIN at Gianyar Supercharger Hub** | Gianyar (near Ubud) | DC Fast | 120 kW | 50 min (35 km) | Fastest charger in east Bali, new 120 kW units |
| **PLN SPKLU Sanur** | Sanur harbour area | DC Fast | 60 kW | 1 hr 15 min (55 km) | Good stop if coming from south Bali |
| **PLN SPKLU Seminyak** | Seminyak Village | DC Fast | 50 kW | 1 hr 40 min (70 km) | Useful if staying in Seminyak/Kuta before departure |

**Recommended strategy:**

1. **Start from South Bali (Kuta/Seminyak/Canggu):** Charge to 90% at the PLN SPKLU in Seminyak or at a hotel AC charger overnight. Drive east via Sanur and the coastal bypass. Stop for a top-up at Gianyar Supercharger Hub (120 kW, 15 min to 80%) if needed, or push directly to Amankila/Candidasa for the final charge.
2. **If staying in Ubud:** Charge overnight at your hotel (many Ubud resorts offer AC charging). In the morning, drive to Gianyar Supercharger Hub (15 min) for a DC top-up to 75%, then continue east to Padangbai (50 min drive).
3. **If already on east Bali (Amed/Candidasa):** Use the PLN SPKLU at Amankila (60 kW) or Candidasa (50 kW) for your final charge. These are 20-35 minutes from Padangbai — arrive at the ferry with 70-75%.

### Battery Strategy Before Boarding

| Step | Action | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| 1 | Charge to **70-75%** at your last Bali charger | Ferry operators don't have a formal % policy, but keeping under 80% is prudent for sea crossings |
| 2 | Factor in **5-10% phantom drain** during the 3-4 hour crossing | BMS, GPS, and any climate systems continue drawing power |
| 3 | Plan to arrive in Lembar with **55-65%** | Enough to reach your Lombok accommodation and plug in overnight |
| 4 | Have Lombok charging stops queued on Waze/Google Maps | Lombok has **5-7 public DC chargers** — fewer than Bali, so planning matters |

## The Ferry Crossing: What to Expect

### Padangbai → Lembar (Step by Step)

1. **Arrival at Padangbai (45 min early):** Enter the ferry terminal and queue in the vehicle check-in lane. Present your booking confirmation (printed or digital) and vehicle registration (STNK/Buku Kendaraan). Staff will direct you to the weigh bridge.
2. **Vehicle weighing:** All vehicles are weighed before boarding. Standard EVs (Tesla Model Y ~2,000 kg, BYD Atto 3 ~1,750 kg, Hyundai Ioniq 5 ~2,100 kg) are well within ferry limits. Oversized EVs (Hummer EV at 4,100 kg) would need special arrangements.
3. **Vehicle loading (15 min before departure):** Drive onto the ferry following staff hand signals. The vehicle deck can be tight — larger SUVs may need to fold side mirrors. Park in gear (P) with handbrake on.
4. **Sea crossing (3-4 hours):** Leave your vehicle and go to the passenger deck. The ferry has basic amenities — AC lounge seats, a warung (small food stall) selling instant noodles, coffee, and snacks, and a prayer room (musholla). **Do not stay in your vehicle during the crossing.**
5. **Arrival at Lembar (35 km southeast of Mataram):** Upon docking, wait for staff to signal. Drive off the ferry. You'll exit directly onto the Lembar port access road, about 30-40 minutes from Mataram.

### EV-Specific Ferry Tips

- **Turn off Sentry Mode / Dashcam** — saves ~5-8% battery over the 3-4 hour crossing
- **Enable "Car Wash" or "Transport" mode** if your EV has it — prevents alarms from triggering on the rocking ferry
- **Put windows slightly down (1-2 cm)** — the deck can get hot (35-40°C inside the metal ferry) and cracking the window prevents cabin heat buildup without running the AC
- **Leave climate off** — your car will be fine. The battery doesn't need cooling while parked
- **Keep your charging cable accessible** — not in the frunk, but in the boot or back seat. You'll need it on Lombok
- **Don't check charging apps during crossing** — cell reception in the Lombok Strait is patchy. Download offline maps for Lombok before departing

### Lombok Charging Strategy

| Trip Duration | Strategy |
|---------------|----------|
| **2-3 days** | Charge to 80% at PLN SPKLU Mandalika or Mataram on arrival. One DC charge is usually enough. Top up at hotel AC charger overnight. |
| **4-7 days** | Use overnight hotel AC charging + one mid-trip top-up at Mandalika or Senggigi DC charger. Plan your route to pass a charger every 2-3 days. |
| **7+ days (exploring whole island)** | Account for 2-3 DC charges total. The island is 80 km north-south and 70 km east-west — total driving is manageable even at battery-efficient speeds. |
| **Mandalika race season** | Book charging slots early during MotoGP/WSBK weekends (typically February/March and September/October). PLN adds temporary chargers but demand surges. |

**Important Lombok charging notes:**
- **PLN SPKLU stations require the PLN Mobile app** to activate — download and register before you go
- **Cell coverage is good** in Mataram, Senggigi, and Mandalika, but patchy on Mount Rinjani approaches and in East Lombok's interior. Download offline maps
- **Most hotel AC chargers use Type 2 plugs** — bring your own Type 2 cable as most hotels don't provide one
- **Emergency option:** Many hotels and homestays will let you plug into a regular wall socket (220V, 13A) for Rp 50,000-100,000 extra per night. With a granny charger, you get roughly 10-12 km/hour — enough for 100+ km overnight
- **Private/restaurant chargers:** Some cafes in Senggigi and Kuta Lombok have installed their own wall boxes. Ask at local warungs — word of mouth is still the best directory

## Tips for EV Drivers on the Bali-Lombok Route

### Before the Trip

- [ ] **Download PLN Mobile** — register and add payment (GoPay, OVO, or QRIS) before departing. Some stations require an Indonesian phone number for activation — buy a Telkomsel or XL SIM at the airport or a local shop
- [ ] **Download ChargeIN app** — good backup for Bali chargers, especially in the south
- [ ] **Pack your Type 2 charging cable** — almost all Lombok hotel chargers are AC Type 2 wall boxes. Most do NOT provide a cable
- [ ] **Bring a granny charger (portable EVSE)** — this is your emergency backup. A 220V outlet at a homestay or warung adds 10-12 km/hour. Even 8 hours of granny charging gives you 80-100 km of range
- [ ] **Download offline Google Maps for Bali + Lombok** — signal is good in most areas but patchy on the Lombok Strait crossing, in the Rinjani foothills, and on some east coast coastal roads
- [ ] **Carry your vehicle registration (STNK)** — you'll need it for the ferry booking and for any police checks
- [ ] **Join the "EV Indonesia" Telegram group** — real-time updates on charger status from local EV drivers

### At Padangbai Ferry Terminal

- [ ] Arrive **45-60 minutes early** (more on weekends/holidays)
- [ ] Queue in the vehicle lane and present booking confirmation
- [ ] Have your vehicle registration ready
- [ ] Follow staff directions for weigh bridge and deck assignment
- [ ] Set battery to **70-75%** before arrival (charge at Amankila or Candidasa)
- [ ] Turn off **Sentry Mode / Dashcam** before boarding
- [ ] Enable **Transport Mode** or switch off alarm systems

### On Lombok

- [ ] **Charge when you see an available DC charger** — Lombok has fewer than 10 public DC points. Don't wait until you're below 20%
- [ ] **Hotel AC charging is your lifeline** — confirm at check-in that the wall box is working and that you can use it overnight. Most hotels are happy to oblige
- [ ] **The island is manageable without daily DC charging** — Lombok is ~80 km north-south. A single DC charge + overnight AC is enough for 2-3 days of moderate driving
- [ ] **The road to Senaru (Rinjani)** is winding and uphill — expect 25-30% extra battery consumption. Charge fully before heading north from Senggigi
- [ ] **Mandalika area** has the best DC coverage (PLN SPKLU at Grid Pertamina) — use it as your mid-trip charging base
- [ ] **Night driving** on Lombok is not recommended — street lighting outside Mataram and Senggigi is poor. Find your accommodation before sunset

### Returning from Lombok

- [ ] Charge to **60-70% at PLN SPKLU Mandalika or Mataram** the morning before your return ferry departure
- [ ] Confirm return ferry booking at the Lembar terminal (or via the ASDP app) — same-day bookings are usually fine
- [ ] Arrive at Lembar **45 minutes early**
- [ ] After landing at Padangbai, drive to **Gianyar Supercharger Hub** (50 min) or **Amankila** (30 min) for a charge if heading south
- [ ] If heading to Ubud, drive directly (35-45 min from Padangbai) and charge at your hotel — many Ubud resorts offer free AC charging

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I take any EV on the Bali-Lombok ferry?
Yes. The ASDP Ro-Ro ferry accepts standard passenger vehicles, including EVs. Vehicle height (max ~2.5m) and weight (max ~3,500 kg) limits accommodate all current consumer EVs. Very low-clearance sports EVs (Porsche Taycan with lowered suspension) may scrape on the ferry ramp — drive slowly and at an angle.

### Do I need to book the ferry in advance for an EV?
During peak season (June-August, December-January, Nyepi/March, MotoGP/WSBK weekends), yes — book 3-7 days in advance through the **ASDP Ferry app** or at the terminal. Off-peak, same-day walk-up bookings are usually fine. Specifying "EV" isn't mandatory but helps with deck space allocation.

### Is it safe to take my EV on the Lombok Strait ferry?
Yes. Modern EV batteries are sealed and waterproof. The Lombok Strait is sheltered water (not open ocean) and the ferry deck is covered. The 3-4 hour crossing is in a covered, ventilated deck. Your EV will be fine.

### Can I charge at the ferry terminals?
Neither Padangbai nor Lembar has dedicated EV charging as of June 2026. The nearest chargers are:
- **Padangbai:** PLN SPKLU Amankila (20 km north) or Gianyar Supercharger Hub (35 km west)
- **Lembar:** PLN SPKLU Mataram (30 km north) — drive directly there from the terminal

### What if there are no chargers available on Lombok?
Lombok is 4,515 sq km (roughly 80 km by 70 km). Even with no public charging available:
- A full EV arriving at 65% has enough range for 1.5-2 days of moderate island driving
- Hotel AC charging (even a standard wall socket with a granny charger) adds 80-120 km overnight
- Most mid-range hotels in Senggigi, Mataram, and Mandalika now have at least one wall box or will let you use a wall socket

### Is Lombok's charging better than it was in 2025?
Significantly. In 2025, Lombok had only 3-4 public DC chargers. By mid-2026, PLN has added stations in Selong (east Lombok) and Praya (south), and Mandalika's Grid Pertamina station has been upgraded to a more reliable 60 kW unit. The network is still small (8-10 DC points) but strategically placed.

### Can I visit Gili Islands from Lombok in my EV?
You can drive to **Bangsal Harbour** (northwest Lombok, 25 min from Senggigi) where public boats depart for Gili Trawangan, Gili Air, and Gili Meno. Leave your EV at the secure parking lot at Bangsal (Rp 20,000-30,000/day). **There is no EV charging at Bangsal** — charge fully in Senggigi before heading north, and ensure you have enough for the return trip to your next charging stop.

### What's better for Lombok: public charging or hotel charging?
Both. Use **public DC charging** for the initial top-up on arrival and for mid-trip top-ups before long drives (e.g. Senggigi → Senaru, Mandalika → Selong). Use **hotel AC charging** overnight to cover daily driving needs. Most 4+ day trips on Lombok need exactly 2 DC charges + 3-4 nights of hotel AC charging.

## The Bottom Line

The Bali-Lombok EV ferry crossing is the most exciting inter-island EV route in Indonesia. The Padangbai → Lembar Ro-Ro ferry is EV-friendly and affordable, Bali's charging network is well-established, and Lombok's network — while still developing — is now sufficient for a week-long trip when combined with hotel AC charging.

**Key takeaways:**
- The ferry crossing costs Rp 250,000-400,000 for a vehicle and takes 3-4 hours
- Charge to 70-75% at Amankila or Gianyar before the ferry
- Lombok has 8-10 public DC chargers — PLNs at Mandalika, Mataram, Senggigi, Selong, and Praya are your key stops
- Hotel AC charging covers the rest — most mid-range resorts in Senggigi, Mandalika, and Kuta have wall boxes
- Bring a Type 2 cable and a granny charger — you'll need both
- The Mandalika area (south Lombok) has the best DC coverage — use it as your mid-trip charging hub

For the best experience, combine a 1-2 day Bali east-coast stay (Amed, Tirta Gangga, Candidasa) with 3-5 days exploring Lombok — from the beaches of Senggigi and Kuta to the waterfalls of Senaru and the rice terraces of Tetebatu. It's one of Indonesia's great EV road trips, and in 2026, it's finally practical.
