# Top 5 EV Road Trips in Thailand for Families 2026 — Routes, Charging & Kid-Friendly Stops

**Last updated: May 16, 2026**

Thailand is leading Southeast Asia's EV revolution in 2026 with **over 10,000 public DC fast chargers** and one of the highest densities of chargers per capita on the continent. For families, this means Thailand is the best country in Asia for EV road trips — safe highways, outstanding food at every stop, diverse scenery, and a growing network of hotels with destination charging.

- **Scenery and variety** — from mountains to beaches to jungles
- **Charging infrastructure** — reliability and density of charging points
- **Family-friendly activities** — kid-approved stops along the way
- **Seasonal suitability** — best times of year to drive

## 1. Bangkok → Chiang Mai — The ultimate Thai EV road trip

**Distance:** 696 km | **Duration:** 7-8 hours driving, 3-4 days recommended | **Difficulty:** Easy

This is the **crown jewel of Thai EV road trips**. The journey from Bangkok to Chiang Mai via Highway 1 (Phahonyothin Road) takes you from the bustling central plains through the historic city of Sukhothai, past rice paddies and sunflower fields, up into the misty mountains of northern Thailand.

### Charging Strategy

| Stop | Location | Charger Type | Power | Notes |
|------|----------|-------------|-------|-------|
| PTT EV Station, Ayutthaya | Kms 0-80 | DC CCS-2 | 180 kW | Full charge from Bangkok |
| PTT EV Station Phichit | Kms 80-340 | DC CCS-2 | 150 kW | Lunch stop, good to 80% |
| PEA Volta, Lampang | Kms 340-560 | DC CCS-2 | 120 kW | Quick top-up to continue |
| Destination: Chiang Mai | Kms 560-696 | Hotel charging | 7-22 kW AC | Overnight charge at hotel |

> 💡 **Pro tip:** Charge to 100% in Bangkok before departure. With a long-range EV (500+ km WLTP), you can skip the Phichit stop and do Lampang in one go. Budget-friendly EVs (Atto 3, MG4) should plan for two 15-20 minute fast-charging stops.

### Family Activities Along the Route

**Ayutthaya (80 km from Bangkok):**
- **Ayutthaya Elephant Palace & Royal Kraal** — interact with rescued elephants responsibly
- **Historic Park** — rent bicycles to explore ancient temple ruins. Most are flat and EV-roadtrip-friendly
- **Ayutthaya Floating Market** — traditional snacks, boat rides, puppet shows

**Sukhothai (440 km from Bangkok):**
- **Sukhothai Historical Park** — UNESCO World Heritage site, cycleable on footpaths
- **Ramkhamhaeng National Park** — hiking and waterfalls if you're feeling adventurous

**Chiang Mai (696 km):**
- **Night Safari** — kids love the nighttime tram ride through animal zones
- **Elephant Nature Park** — ethical elephant sanctuary (book ahead)
- **Doi Suthep** — cable car to the temple with panoramic views
- **Grand Canyon Chiang Mai** — water park with inflatable obstacle courses

### Where to Charge and Stay

Hotels in Chiang Mai are increasingly EV-friendly. Top picks:
- **InterContinental Chiang Mai** — 22 kW AC chargers, free for guests
- **Anantara Chiang Mai Resort** — 2x 11 kW Type 2 chargers
- **Debu Boutique Hotel** — 7 kW overnight charging

## 2. Phuket → Krabi — Tropical island hopping by EV

**Distance:** 170 km | **Duration:** 2.5 hours driving, 2-3 days recommended | **Difficulty:** Easy

The Phuket–Krabi coastal route via Highway 402 and Route 4 is one of Thailand's most scenic drives — limestone karsts rising from emerald sea, coconut groves lining the road, and long stretches of beachside highway. At just 170 km, most EVs can do this route without any charging, but top up in Phuket anyway.

### Charging Stops

| Location | Charger Type | Power | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|-------|
| Phuket (departure) | Various DC | 50-180 kW | Start with 100% |
| Phi Phi Island ferry point | N/A | N/A | Alternative to driving |
| Krabi (destination) | Hotel AC charging | 7-22 kW | Overnight charge |

> 💡 **Pro tip:** The route includes a short ferry crossing at Tha Lane Bay to reach certain Krabi beach resorts. This involves driving onto a flat-bed ferry — EVs with low ground clearance (MG4, BYD Seal) should be aware of the ramp angle.

### Family Activities

**Phuket:**
- **Phuket Elephant Sanctuary** — ethical, no-riding sanctuary
- **Phuket Aquarium** — small but engaging for younger kids
- **Cable Jungle** — zipline adventure for older children

**Krabi:**
- **Emerald Pool & Hot Springs** — natural thermal pools surrounded by jungle
- **Tiger Cave Temple** — 1,237 steps to the top (adventurous families only!)
- **Hong Islands** — private long-tail boat tour from Ao Nang
- **Four Island Tour** — snorkelling, beach time, and monkey watching

### Best Family Hotels

- **Rayavadee, Krabi** — 11 kW AC charging in designated bays
- **Amari Phuket** — 22 kW charger in parking lot
- **Santhiya Koh Yao Yai Resort** — 7 kW for overnight guests

## 3. Bangkok → Khao Yai — Nature escape for active families

**Distance:** 180 km | **Duration:** 2.5 hours driving, 2-3 days | **Difficulty:** Easy

Khao Yai National Park is Thailand's oldest and most accessible national park — a UNESCO World Heritage site with waterfalls, wildlife, hiking trails, and even wineries. The drive from Bangkok is short and easy, making it the perfect weekend EV road trip for families.

### Charging Strategy

| Stop | Location | Charger Type | Power | Notes |
|------|----------|-------------|-------|-------|
| Bangkok (departure) | Home/hotel | 7-22 kW AC | Start 100% |
| Khao Yai (arrival) | Hotel or PTT Pak Chong | DC 50-120 kW | Top-up if needed |

The 180 km route is a non-issue for any modern EV. Even the shortest-range options on our budget list (Neta V-II, Wuling Bingo) can make it with margin.

### Family Activities in Khao Yai

- **Khao Yai National Park** — guided tour to see elephants, gibbons, hornbills
- **PB Valley Khao Yai Winery** — kids can run the vineyard paths while parents taste
- **The Birder's Lodge** — bird-watching walk perfect for young naturalists
- **Scenic Farm** feed alpacas, rabbits, and goats — small animal paradise for kids
- **Palio Khao Yai** — Italian-style shopping village with gelato

### Where to Stay

- **Muthi Maya Forest Pool Resort** — 7 kW charging, kids' pool, and nature trail
- **Hotel VENTURI Khao Yai** — has 22 kW EV charging and a great children's playroom
- **InterContinental Khao Yai Resort** — beautiful property with multiple dining options

## 4. Bangkok → Hua Hin → Pranburi — The classic family beach retreat

**Distance:** 230 km | **Duration:** 3 hours driving, 2-3 days | **Difficulty:** Very Easy

The Royal Coast Road from Bangkok to Hua Hin is Thailand's most popular weekend getaway for Bangkok families. The route is flat, the roads are excellent, and Hua Hin offers some of the best family-friendly resorts in Thailand — many with EV charging installed.

### Charging Stops

| Stop | Location | Charger Type | Power | Notes |
|------|----------|-------------|-------|-------|
| Bangkok (departure) | Home/hotel | Various | Start 100% |
| Phetchaburi | PTT EV Station | 50-120 kW DC | Optional top-up, good lunch stop |
| Hua Hin | Hotel charging | 7-22 kW AC | Overnight charge |

> 💡 **Pro tip:** The PTT station at Cha-Am (just before Hua Hin) has a food court with halal and kid-friendly meals. Charge while eating — 30 minutes at 120 kW is plenty.

### Family Activities

**Hua Hin:**

- **Hua Hin Railway Station** — cute retro station for photos
- **Hutsadin Elephant Foundation** — gentle interactions with rescued elephants
- **Hua Hin Night Market** — cheap eats, toy stalls, and live music

**Pranburi / Khao Sam Roi Yot:**
- **Khao Sam Roi Yot National Park** — boat tour to Phraya Nakhon Cave temple
- **Pranburi Forest Park** — boardwalk through mangrove forest

## 5. Bangkok → Pattaya → Rayong — The beach-bum express

**Distance:** 160 km | **Duration:** 2 hours driving, 2-3 days | **Difficulty:** Very Easy

The shortest and easiest EV road trip on our list, Bangkok to Pattaya is the go-to choice for families with younger children who can't handle long drives. Extend down to Rayong for quieter beaches and the famous fruit orchards.

### Charging Strategy

| Stop | Location | Charger Type | Power | Notes |
|------|----------|-------------|-------|-------|
| Bangkok (departure) | Home | AC | Start 100% |
| Pattaya | Various DC chargers | 50-180 kW | Top-up at Central Festival |
| Rayong | Hotel AC | 7-22 kW | Overnight |

The 160 km trip is easily manageable even for the shortest-range EVs. The real challenge is choosing which family attraction to visit first.

### Family Activities

**Pattaya:**
- **Underwater World Pattaya** — 100-metre underwater tunnel aquarium
- **Ramayana Water Park** — biggest water park in Thailand, slides for all ages
- **Khao Kheow Open Zoo** — drive-through safari park with giraffes and zebras
- **Pattaya Dolphin World** — dolphin and seal shows

**Rayong:**
- **Suan Lamai Beach** — quieter alternative to Pattaya
- **Rayong Aquarium** — free entry, good for an hour
- **Mae Ramphueng Beach** — long sandy beach, coconut groves, seafood restaurants

### Where to Stay

- **Amari Pattaya** — 22 kW EV charger in underground parking
- **Veranda Resort Pattaya** — 50 kW DC fast charger on premises

### Best Times of Year for EV Road Trips

**Cool season (Nov-Feb):** Best time for all routes. Cool mountain air in Chiang Mai, pleasant beaches, and minimal rain. A/C usage is lower, improving EV range by 5-10%.

**Hot season (Mar-May):** The heat saps EV range. Expect a 10-15% reduction due to heavy A/C use. Stay hydrated and avoid driving 11 AM - 2 PM on longer routes.

**Rainy season (Jun-Oct):** Flash flooding can close some rural roads. Stick to main highways. The upside — fewer other tourists and lush green scenery.

## Family Road Trip Checklist for Thailand

1. **Download apps:** PTT EV Station PluZ, EA Anywhere, Google Maps, LINE (for communication)
2. **Get a Highway Toll pass** — Easy Pass for expressways
3. **Bring a CCS-2 to CCS-2 cable** — some rural chargers don't have tethered cables
4. **Pack a 3.3 kW emergency charger** — just in case the hotel doesn't have a proper charger
5. **Paper map backup** — some remote areas have spotty 4G
6. **Kids' charging kit** — tablets, snacks, neck pillows, window shades for the car
7. **First-aid kit** with oral rehydration salts for hot-weather driving
