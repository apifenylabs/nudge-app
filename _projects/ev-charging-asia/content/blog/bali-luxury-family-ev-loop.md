# Bali Luxury Family EV Loop — Canggu, Ubud, Jimbaran & Nusa Dua (5-Day Itinerary)

Bali is heaven for families who don't want to compromise on luxury. The island's compact size — just 280 km for this full loop — combined with a growing network of DC fast chargers at luxury hotels and shopping malls, makes it one of Asia's most rewarding EV road trips for discerning families.

This 5-day itinerary loops through Canggu → Seminyak → Ubud → Jimbaran → Nusa Dua, hitting Bali's most exclusive resorts, family-friendly attractions, and cultural experiences. Every stop includes verified EV charging, kid-approved activities, and world-class accommodation.

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Canggu — Surf, Sunsets & Boutique Luxury

**Distance:** 20 km (from Ngurah Rai Airport to Canggu)
**Driving time:** 40 minutes
**Charging:** Charge at the airport or your hotel before heading out

**Morning:** Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport. Pick up your rental EV (BYD Atto 3s and MG 4s are widely available through GoCars and local rental agencies). Charge to 80%+ at the airport's SPKLU DC fast charger (50kW, CCS2) before departing.

**Late Morning to Afternoon:** Check into your Canggu villa. The **Hotel Indigo Bali Seminyak Beach** has 50kW DC charging in their parking area. Alternatively, the **COMO Uma Canggu** offers AC destination charging (7kW) for guests and is steps from the best surf breaks.

**Family Activities:**
- **La Brisa Beach Club** (kids welcome before 6 PM) — coconut tree-framed pool, kids' menu, and live acoustic sets
- **Canggu Surf Lessons** — Bali Surf Academy offers private family lessons (age 5+), 45 min sessions
- **Finns Recreation Club** — bowling, laser tag, trampoline park, and a kids' splash zone

**Lunch:** Shady Shack — organic bowls, smoothies, and a dedicated kids' corner with colouring activities

**Dinner:** Mason — wood-fired Australian fare with a kids' pasta menu. Book the garden table for space with a stroller.

**Luxury Accommodation:** [Hotel Indigo Bali Seminyak Beach](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Hotel+Indigo+Bali+Seminyak+Beach) — rooftop infinity pool, kids' club (ages 4-12), and 50kW DC charging in the garage

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Hotel Indigo Bali Seminyak Beach](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Hotel+Indigo+Bali+Seminyak+Beach&aid=2875669)

**Notes:** Canggu traffic can be fierce after 3 PM. Plan afternoon activities within walking or short scooter distance. Bring a portable fan — many villas use open-air living.

### Day 2: Canggu → Seminyak (8 km)

**Distance:** 8 km
**Driving time:** 25 minutes
**Charging:** Top up at the Seminyak Village Mall DC charger (50kW, 2 slots) while you shop

**Morning:** Short drive south to Seminyak. This is a 10-minute hop, so you won't need to charge.

**Family Activities:**
- **Seminyak Village Mall** — air-conditioned shopping with toy stores, a small indoor play area, and a DC fast charger in the basement
- **Turtle Release at Petitenget Beach** — the Bali Sea Turtle Society releases hatchlings year-round at sunrise. Check their Instagram for release schedules. **This is a bucket-list family moment.**
- **Kiddie Cooking Class at Bali Asli** — hands-on Balinese cooking for ages 5+. Kids learn to make satay lilit and lawar in a family session (pre-book 48h ahead)

**Lunch:** Merah Putih — stunning Indonesian fine dining with a kids' menu. The garden courtyard lets toddlers explore safely.

**Dinner:** Sarong Restaurant — Mek's signature Indian-Indonesian fusion. Family-friendly before 8 PM with a dedicated kids' set menu.

**Luxury Accommodation:** [W Bali – Seminyak](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=W+Bali+Seminyak) — private beach access, WET pool, kids' club (ages 4-12), and AC destination charging for EV guests

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [W Bali Seminyak](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=W+Bali+Seminyak&aid=2875669)

**Notes:** Seminyak has the best shopping on this loop. Charge your EV at Seminyak Village Mall (basement level 2, 50kW DC) while you explore for 2-3 hours. The charger is rarely full on weekdays.

### Day 3: Seminyak → Ubud (35 km)

**Distance:** 35 km
**Driving time:** 1-1.5 hours (winding uphill road)
**Charging:** Charge overnight at your Ubud resort or at the Ubud Monkey Forest DC charger (50kW)

**Morning:** Depart Seminyak after breakfast. The drive to Ubud is a scenic 35 km winding through rice paddies and small villages. The climb from sea level to 300m elevation will reduce range by about 5-8% on the ascent.

**Family Activities:**
- **Ubud Monkey Forest** — a must-visit. 600+ long-tailed macaques in a 13-hectare forest. Kids love it. Keep loose items secured.
- **Tegalalang Rice Terrace** — iconic stepped rice paddies. The "Bali Swing" experience is here (kids over 90cm). Skip the queues by arriving before 9 AM.
- **Bali Zoo** — overnight elephant safari experience, but even a daytime visit includes orangutan feeding and a petting zoo. Book the "Breakfast with Orangutans" (IDR 500k/adult, half-price for kids).
- **Cooking Class at Ubud's Casa Luna** — family-friendly Balinese cooking class. Kids 6+ can join the full session; younger ones can help with desserts.

**Lunch:** Melting Wok Warung — intimate cliff-side dining. The kids' nasi campur is a hit. Book 24 hours ahead.

**Dinner:** Room service or Ubud's night market for street food exploration

**Luxury Accommodation:** [Four Seasons Resort Bali at Sayan](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Four+Seasons+Bali+Sayan) — the iconic coconut-shaped lotus pond entrance, kids' club with Balinese craft workshops, infinity pool overlooking the Ayung River, and 2 AC destination chargers for EV guests

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Four Seasons Resort Bali at Sayan](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Four+Seasons+Resort+Bali+at+Sayan&aid=2875669)

**Notes:** Ubud's roads are narrow and winding. Park your EV at the resort and use the hotel shuttle for local trips. The Four Seasons has 7kW Type 2 chargers — a full overnight charge gives you 150km+ for the next day.

---

### Day 4: Ubud → Jimbaran (40 km)

**Distance:** 40 km
**Driving time:** 1 hour
**Charging:** 50kW DC at Jimbaran's Ayana Resort

**Morning:** Take a final morning in Ubud. Visit the **Ubud Art Market** for souvenirs (kids love the wooden animal puppets and batik kits). Check out by 11 AM.

**Drive:** The 40 km descent from Ubud to Jimbaran is mostly downhill — you'll recoup 3-5% battery through regenerative braking. The road through Sanur and past the airport is well-maintained.

**Family Activities:**
- **Bali Safari & Marine Park** (on the way, 15 min detour) — a full-day experience with safari tram rides through animal enclosures, elephant shows, and a water park zone. Spend the morning here before heading to Jimbaran.
- **Jimbaran Turtle Sanctuary** — run by the local conservation group, this small centre on the beach lets kids hold baby turtles and learn about conservation. **Best time: 9-11 AM or 3-5 PM.** Donation-based entry (IDR 50k suggested).
- **Jimbaran Bay Beach** — calm waters perfect for young swimmers. Low tide creates shallow pools children can safely splash in.

**Lunch:** Jimbaran Bay seafood warungs — pick your fresh fish at the market and have it grilled over coconut husks. Menega Cafe is the most family-friendly with high chairs and a sandpit area.

**Dinner:** The Rock Bar (Ayana Resort) — sunset cocktails for adults while kids enjoy the supervised Kids Club (until 9 PM). The funicular ride down the cliff is an adventure in itself.

**Luxury Accommodation:** [Ayana Resort Bali](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Ayana+Resort+Bali) — 12 swimming pools, private beach, Rock Bar, Kids Club (ages 4-12), and a 50kW DC charger in the main parking area. This is the most EV-friendly luxury resort in Bali.

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Ayana Resort Bali](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Ayana+Resort+Bali&aid=2875669)

**Notes:** Ayana's DC charger is shared among resort guests. Ask the valet to plug in when you arrive — it takes 45-60 min to charge from 20% to 80%. Jimbaran is all about seafood — if your kids are picky eaters, the resort's international buffet is a safe bet.

---

### Day 5: Jimbaran → Nusa Dua → Departure (15 km)

**Distance:** 15 km
**Driving time:** 25 minutes
**Charging:** 50kW DC at Nusa Dua's Bali Collection Mall or the airport

**Morning:** Short drive to Nusa Dua. This is the final leg of your loop and a relaxed one.

**Family Activities:**
- **Bali Collection Mall** — open-air shopping complex with a 50kW DC charger in the parking lot. Toy stores, souvenir shops, and a small indoor play area.
- **Waterbom Bali** (in Kuta, 10 min before the airport) — Asia's best water park. Lazy river, kids' slides, FlowRider surf simulator, and dedicated toddler zone. **Book online for 20% off.** Last entry 4 PM.
- **GWK Cultural Park** — giant Garuda Wisnu Kencana statue, cultural dance performances, and panoramic views of the Bukit Peninsula. Kids love the cultural dance workshops.

**Lunch:** Bali Collection Mall food court or the Nusa Dua Beach Grill at the St. Regis

**Dinner:** Before your flight, grab a final meal at the airport's new premium lounge (priority pass accepted) or at a beachfront cafe

**Luxury Accommodation (if extending):** [The St. Regis Bali Resort](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=St+Regis+Bali+Resort) — lagoon-access villas, butler service, Remède Spa, and a 22kW AC charger for guests. The butler can arrange your EV charging while you enjoy your last day.

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [The St. Regis Bali Resort](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=The+St.+Regis+Bali+Resort&aid=2875669)

**Departure:** Return your rental EV with 30%+ remaining charge at Ngurah Rai Airport. The airport has SPKLU DC chargers (50kW) in the short-term parking area if you need a top-up before drop-off.

## Seasonal Recommendations

### Dry Season (April-October) — BEST TIME
- **Best conditions:** Clear skies, low humidity, minimal rain
- **Range impact:** Near WLTP rating — A/C usage is moderate
- **What's open:** All kids' clubs, water parks, and outdoor activities run at full capacity
- **Book ahead:** Resorts are 70-90% booked in July-August. Book 3+ months out for the Four Seasons and Ayana.
- **Turtle season:** Best chance of seeing hatchling releases at Petitenget Beach (peaks June-September)

### Wet Season (November-March)
- **Challenges:** Afternoon downpours, slippery roads, higher humidity
- **Range impact:** A/C runs harder, reducing range by 8-12%
- **Bright side:** Stunning green rice terraces, fewer crowds at Ubud attractions
- **Indoor backup:** Waterbom Bali (covered), Bali Collection Mall, cooking classes
- **Best deal:** Resort rates drop 30-50% in January-February (excluding Chinese New Year)

### Shoulder Months (April, May, October)
- **Sweet spot:** Good weather, moderate crowds, lower rates
- **Recommendation:** May is arguably the best month — low chance of rain, turtle season starting, and resorts offer shoulder-season promotions

## Family Tips for Bali with an EV

1. **Download these apps before you go:** SPKLU (state charging network), PlugShare (community reports), and Gojek (for backup transport if your EV is charging)
2. **Villas vs. resorts for families:** Villas offer private pools and kitchens (great for nap times and meals), but only resorts with on-site charging make EV ownership seamless. The **Four Seasons Sayan** and **Ayana Resort** hit the sweet spot.
3. **Pack a Level 1 charger:** Most Balinese villas have standard wall outlets. A Level 1 charger gives you 40-50 km overnight — plenty for local day trips.
4. **Narrow roads = smaller car:** The MG 4 is 15cm narrower than a Tesla Model 3, making it far easier to navigate Ubud's tight lanes.
5. **Kids' clubs are your EV charging partner:** While the kids enjoy supervised play at the resort's kids' club (usually free 9 AM-5 PM), plug in your EV for a full charge. Win-win.
6. **Mosquito protection:** Ubud and Jimbaran have mosquitoes at dusk. Pack repellent and consider a portable mosquito net for the car.

## FAQ

**Can I drive an EV around Bali with only 200km range?**  
Yes. The full loop is 280 km, but you'll charge at least 3 times (at your hotels, the mall, or the airport). Any EV with 200km+ real-world range is sufficient.

**Are EV chargers reliable in Bali?**  
Generally yes. SPKLU (government) chargers at malls and the airport have 90%+ uptime. Resort chargers are maintained by the properties. Use PlugShare to check recent check-ins before heading to a station.

**Is it safe to drive in Bali with kids?**  
Yes on main routes (the bypass road, Jalan Raya Ubud, Jalan Raya Kuta). Drive defensively — scooters weave unpredictably. Use child seats (request from your rental provider). Avoid driving 5-8 PM in Canggu and Seminyak when traffic peaks.

**What EV charging app do I need in Bali?**  
The **SPKLU** app covers government DC chargers. Most resort chargers are free-for-guests and don't require an app. **PlugShare** is your best backup for finding and reviewing stations.

**Can I charge an EV at a private villa in Bali?**  
Yes, if the villa has a standard wall outlet. Bring a Level 1 (portable) charger. A standard Indonesian outlet (230V/10A) provides ~2.3 kW — about 15 km of range per hour. Overnight (10 hours) = 150 km.
