# Cebu to Bohol to Siquijor EV Island-Hopping Road Trip: Philippines Visayas Guide 2026

The Philippines is often overlooked for EV road trips — but the Visayas region is quietly building a workable charging network, especially around Cebu, Bohol, and Dumaguete. With short island distances, EV-friendly ferries now operating between major islands, and spectacular family destinations, this three-island loop is one of Asia's most underrated EV adventures.

This guide covers a 7-day family road trip through Cebu City, the beaches of Moalboal and Oslob, the chocolate-colored hills of Bohol, the fireflies of Siquijor, and back via Dumaguete — all in an electric vehicle.

## Route Overview

| Detail | Info |

| Duration | 7 days (can be compressed to 5) |
| Islands visited | Cebu → Bohol → Siquijor → Negros → Cebu |
| Charging stops per day | 1-2 |

| Ferry crossings | 3: Cebu→Bohol, Bohol→Siquijor, Siquijor→Dumaguete→Cebu |
| Best time to go | December-May (dry season in Visayas) |

## Why the Visayas is EV-Ready (Mostly)

The Philippines EV charging landscape has improved dramatically:

- **Cebu City** has a growing network of public chargers — BYD, MG dealerships, and select malls offer CCS2 charging
- **Bohol** has resort-based charging and the government is installing more units
- **Dumaguete** (Negros) has reliable charging at hotels and the university district
- **Short ferry distances** — The longest crossing is Cebu→Bohol at ~2 hours. Perfect for EVs, and some ferries now have charging for passengers
- **Tourism-driven infrastructure** — Resorts in Panglao, Moalboal, and Siquijor are installing EV chargers to attract eco-conscious travelers

**⚠️ But be honest:** Charging outside Cebu City and Panglao is sparse. You'll rely heavily on hotel AC charging (7-22kW) and BYD/MG dealership stations. This is an adventure for prepared drivers — perfect if you like the spirit of EV road trips.

### Ferry Rules for EVs

Filipino ferry operators are still developing EV policies. Current guidelines:

- **OceanJet** (Cebu → Bohol, fast craft, ~2 hrs): Accepts EVs on a case-by-case basis. Book 24 hours ahead and declare. Expect to park on the vehicle deck and keep the car in Neutral with parking brake OFF.
- **Montenegro Lines** (Bohol → Siquijor, Dumaguete → Cebu): More relaxed. EVs accepted without issue.
- **General advice:** Call ahead, bring your EV documentation, and expect port staff to be curious (in a good way).

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrive Cebu City — Pick Up EV & Explore

**Morning:** Arrive at Mactan-Cebu International Airport (CEB). Pick up your rented BYD Atto 3 from BYD Cebu (Mandaue City, 20 min from airport). Rental: PHP 3,000-5,000/day ($52-87 USD).

**First charge:** **BYD Cebu Mandaue** — 60kW CCS2 DC fast charger. Charge to 100% (60-90 min) while doing paperwork. Cost: ~PHP 500-800.

**Cebu City highlights:**
- **Sinulog Shrine** — Historic site of the Santo Niño image. Free entry.
- **Fort San Pedro** — Small Spanish fort in the harbor area. PHP 75 entry.
- **Tops Lookout** — Panoramic Cebu City views. 25 min drive uphill. PHP 100 entry.
- **Carbon Market** — The largest market in Cebu. Try lechon (Cebu-style roast pig) at **CNT Lechon** or **Zubuchon**.

**Charging in Cebu City:**
| Station | Speed | Slots | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|-------|
| BYD Cebu Mandaue | 60kW DC | 2 | Reliable, near airport |
| MG Cebu (Mandaue) | 50kW DC | 2 | Backup, 10 min from BYD |
| SM City Cebu | AC 22kW | 4 | Mall charging, charge while shopping |
| Ayala Center Cebu | AC 22kW | 6 | Downtown, excellent |
| Waterfront Cebu City Hotel | AC 7kW | 2 | Standard socket, free for guests |

**Family dinner:** **STK Ta Bay** (Mandaue) — award-winning seafood in a casual setting. Grilled pampano, sinigang, and fresh lumpia. PHP 500-800 per person.

**Overnight:** **Waterfront Cebu City Hotel** (standard socket EV charging, pool, casino, family-friendly). **Radisson Blu Cebu** (AC charge available, central location at IT Park).

### Day 2: Cebu City → Moalboal (95 km, 2.5 hours)

**Morning (8 AM):** Depart Cebu City south on the Cebu South Coastal Road / N. Bacalso Avenue. A smooth 95 km drive through Cebu's southern towns.

**Drive highlights:**
- **Carcar** — Stop at Carcar Rotunda for *chicharon* (crispy pork belly) and *ampao* (rice crackers). The public market is a food experience. 45 min into the drive.
- **Samboan** — Detour 3 km off the highway to **Lambug Beach** for white sand and clear water.
- **Coastal views** — The road hugs Cebu's west coast with stunning sea views.

**Charge stop:** **BYD Moalboal** — 50kW DC. One of the few fast chargers outside Cebu City. Charge while you have lunch.

**Moalboal highlights:**
- **Pescador Island** — Snorkeling with sea turtles and sardine runs (world-famous sardine ball phenomenon). Boat trip: PHP 1,500-2,000 per person.
- **Panagsama Beach** — The main beach strip with bars and restaurants.
- **White Beach** — Quieter, more family-friendly. 5 min south.
- **Kawasan Falls** — 25 min drive inland. Multi-tiered waterfalls perfect for swimming and canyoneering. PHP 100 entry.

**Overnight:** **Moalboal Blue Orchid Resort** (AC 7kW standard socket, beachfront). **Pescador Suites** (standard socket, pool). Both confirm EV charging.

### Day 3: Moalboal → Oslob (40 km, 1 hour) → Ferry to Bohol

**Morning (6 AM):** Drive 40 km south to **Oslob** for whale shark watching. Arrive by 7 AM to avoid crowds.

**Oslob Whale Shark Experience:** PHP 1,000 per person (30 min). The gentle giants (butanding) are fed daily and swim right alongside you. A bucket-list experience for kids.

**Alternative:** If you skip Oslob, head directly to the port.

**10 AM:** Drive to **Liloan Port** (Oslob) — the port for the ferry to Bohol.

**Ferry: OceanJet** — Liloan, Cebu → Tubigon, Bohol. Duration: ~1.5 hours. Frequency: 4-5 daily. Vehicle + driver: approximately PHP 2,500-4,000 depending on EV size. Book ahead.

**Arrive Bohol (noon):** Disembark at Tubigon Port, 55 km from Panglao (the main tourist hub).

**Charge stop:** **MG Tagbilaran** — 50kW CCS2. Located on the way to Panglao. Charge to 100% (60 min). Have lunch at **Bohol Bee Farm** restaurant (5 min away, famous for organic salads and ice cream).

**Afternoon:** Drive the new Panglao-Tagbilaran bridge (traffic-free connector) and check in.

**Overnight:** **South Palms Resort Panglao** (AC 22kW EV charging, private beach, excellent for families). **Bohol Beach Club** (standard socket, classic Panglao resort).

### Day 4: Explore Bohol (Day trip)

Your EV is charged — now explore Bohol's unique attractions.

**Itinerary:**
| Time | Activity | Location | Travel |
|------|----------|----------|--------|
| 7 AM | Tarsier Sanctuary | Corella | 20 min from Panglao |
| 9 AM | Chocolate Hills | Carmen | 50 km north |
| 11 AM | Man-Made Forest (Bilar) | Bilar | On the way back |
| 12:30 PM | Loboc River Cruise | Loboc | Lunch + floating river |
| 3 PM | Baclayon Church | Baclayon | 16th-century church |
| 5 PM | Return to Panglao | — | Sunset at Alona Beach |

**Chocolate Hills** (Carmen, 50 km): The iconic 1,260+ symmetrical mounds turn brown during dry season. The viewing deck gives a 360° panorama. PHP 100 entry.

**Tarsier Sanctuary** (Corella): See the world's smallest primates up close — these bug-eyed nocturnal creatures are Bohol's cutest residents. PHP 200 entry. **No flash photography** (it damages their eyes).

**Loboc River Cruise:** A floating buffet lunch on a river surrounded by jungle. Live music and a waterfall stop. PHP 850 per person including lunch. Very family-friendly.

**Charging:** No fast chargers on the Bohol loop. You'll run on your Panglao hotel charge. Total driving is ~130 km today — a BYD Atto 3 handles this easily.

**Evening:** **Alona Beach** for dinner and fireworks (many hotels do beach shows). Try seafood at **The Buzzz Cafe** (Bohol Bee Farm's beachfront restaurant).

**Overnight:** Same Panglao hotel.

### Day 5: Bohol → Siquijor (Ferry, 1 hour) + Explore Siquijor

**Morning (8 AM):** Drive to **Tagbilaran Port**. Take the Montenegro Lines ferry to **Siquijor Port** (~1 hour, 3 departures daily). Vehicle + driver: ~PHP 3,000.

**Siquijor** — Known as "the island of fire" (for fireflies and folk magic), Siquijor is actually a serene, family-friendly island with some of the best beaches in the Philippines. The coastal ring road is only 72 km — perfect for an EV.

**Charge stop:** **Coral Cay Resort Siquijor** — AC 7kW standard socket. The best EV option on the island. Also: **U Story Hotel** (standard socket).

**Siquijor highlights:**
- **Salagdoong Beach** — Cliff diving (10m platform) and turquoise water. PHP 50 entry.
- **Cambugahay Falls** — Three-tiered waterfall with rope swings. PHP 30 entry.
- **San Juan Beach** — Sunset along the west coast. Best beach on the island.
- **Balete Tree** — 400-year-old giant balete tree with a spring-fed pool at its base. Stilt walkers offer foot massage (experience, not standard spa!). PHP 20 entry.
- **Butterfly Sanctuary** — Educational, great for kids. PHP 100.

**Overnight:** **Coral Cay Resort** (standard socket EV charging, beachfront cottages). **U Story Hotel** (standard socket, boutique).

### Day 6: Siquijor → Dumaguete (Ferry) → Drive to Cebu via Negros

**Morning (8 AM):** Ferry from **Siquijor Port** to **Dumaguete Port** (Negros Oriental). Montenegro Lines, 45 min. Vehicle + driver: ~PHP 2,500.

**Dumaguete** — "The City of Gentle People." A university town with a beautiful boulevard, good food, and the most reliable EV charging in the Visayas outside Cebu.

**Charge stop:** **MG Dumaguete** — 50kW CCS2 DC. Charge to 100%. Also: **BYD Dumaguete** (50kW CCS2).

**Dumaguete highlights:**
- **Rizal Boulevard** — The famous seaside promenade. Breakfast at **Sans Rival Bistro** (the *sans rival* cake is legendary).
- **Silliman University** — The oldest American-founded university in Asia. Anthropology museum.
- **Pulang Bato Falls** — Red river waterfalls. 30 min drive north.
- **Apo Island** — World-class snorkeling with sea turtles (1-hour boat trip). A full-day optional excursion.

**Midday:** Drive north from Dumaguete through eastern Negros. The road hugs the coast with views of Cebu across the Tanon Strait.

**Route: Dumaguete → Bais → San Carlos → Escalante → Toledo → Cebu City**
Distance: ~200 km via ferry at San Carlos, or drive all the way to Toledo (shorter). Best option: **Ferry from San Carlos to Toledo** (45 min, ~PHP 1,500 vehicle).

**Alternate:** If driving direct, take the Negros Occidental highway (longer but scenic).

**Charge stop:** Mind the range. BYD Atto 3 (420 km) can do Dumaguete → Toledo → Cebu City (~150 km) without charging. An MG ZS EV (320 km) should also make it.

**Arrive Cebu City (6 PM):** Back to the starting point.

**Farewell dinner:** **Lighthouse Restaurant** (Cebu City) — known for *bukol express* (seafood in coconut soup) and grilled panga (fish jaw). PHP 400-600 per person.

**Overnight:** **Radisson Blu Cebu** or **Waterfront Cebu City Hotel**.

### Day 7: Depart Cebu

**Morning:** Return the EV to **BYD Cebu Mandaue** or the rental operator. Most allow airport drop-off.

**Last breakfast:** **Abaca Baking Company** (Taguipuro, near the airport) — farm-to-table café with wood-fired bread, local coffee, and a tropical garden. A perfect ending to the trip.

## Pro Tips

### 1. Book Ferries in Advance

OceanJet (Cebu→Bohol) fills up, especially on weekends. Call 48 hours ahead, tell them you're in an EV, and confirm they'll accept it. Bring a printed EV specification sheet.

### 2. Charge Every Chance You Get

Fast chargers outside Cebu City are limited. Adopt a "top-up whenever available" mentality — even 30 min at a resort AC charger while you have lunch makes a difference.

### 3. Download Offline Maps

Mobile data is unreliable on Siquijor's ring road and between Bohol towns. Download Google Maps offline for all three islands before you leave Cebu.

### 4. Pack a Portable EVSE

A standard Type 2-to-Schuko (household plug) EVSE is essential for this trip. Many smaller hotels only have standard Filipino outlets (220V, 15A). A portable EVSE adds 15-20 km/hour — enough for overnight top-ups.

### 5. The BYD Atto 3 is the Best EV for This Route

With 420 km WLTP range, you can go from Dumaguete to Cebu City (~150 km) without charging. The MG ZS EV (320 km) works too but will need a top-up in Toledo.

### 7. What If You Can't Find a Charger?

The Visayas loop is designed so no single leg exceeds 150 km. If a charger is down:
- **Use resort AC charging** — most hotels are happy to let guests plug in (gratuity appreciated)
- **BYD/MG dealerships** will let you charge even if you didn't buy from them
- **Call ahead** using the PlugShare community phone numbers

### 8. Language

Cebuano is the main language in all four islands. English is widely spoken in tourist areas, hotels, and ferry terminals. Tagalog is understood but less common in the Visayas.

## FAQ

### Can I do this trip in an MG ZS EV (320 km range)?

Yes. The longest single leg is Dumaguete → Cebu City (~150 km), well within range. The MG ZS EV has enough range for every segment. Just charge at every opportunity.

### Are EV ferries safe?

Yes. Philippine ferries handle EVs just like gas cars — with the engine off. OceanJet and Montenegro Lines have carried EVs without issue. Declare your EV when booking, and leave the parking brake off on the vehicle deck (they use wheel chocks instead).

### Do I need an International Driving Permit in the Philippines?

Foreigners can drive with a valid home country driver's license for up to 90 days — but an International Driving Permit (IDP) is recommended and may be required by car rental companies. 1968 Vienna Convention IDP accepted.

### Can I skip Siquijor and do just Cebu + Bohol?

Yes! A 5-day Cebu + Bohol-only loop is more comfortable:
- Day 1: Cebu City + pick up EV
- Day 2: Cebu City → Oslob (whale sharks) → ferry to Bohol
- Day 3: Bohol (Chocolate Hills, tarsiers, Loboc River)
- Day 4: Panglao relaxation + beach
- Day 5: Ferry back to Cebu → depart

Less driving, more relaxing. Great for younger kids.

### Is charging free at Philippine hotels?

Most hotels with EV charging provide it free for guests (included in the room rate). BYD/MG dealership charging costs PHP 300-500 per session. Mall charging (SM, Ayala) is typically free with a minimum spend.

### What adapter do I need?

Philippines uses **Type B** outlets (same as US/NEMA 5-15, 2 flat pins + round ground). Your portable EVSE needs a Type B or universal Schuko adapter. Most rental EVs come with the appropriate cable — confirm when booking.

### Is the driving safe?

Cebu and Bohol roads are good by Philippine standards. Main highways are paved. The Cebu South Coastal Road is excellent. Siquijor's ring road is newly paved. Watch for tricycles, motorcycles, and dogs on side roads.

### How much faster is the EV vs petrol on cost?

Ferry costs are the same. Charging is significantly cheaper:
- **EV**: PHP 3,000-5,000 total charging for the full 7-day trip
- **Petrol (same route)**: PHP 12,000-18,000

That's PHP 9,000-13,000 savings ($155-225) on fuel alone.

## The Bottom Line

The Cebu → Bohol → Siquijor → Dumaguete loop is the most underrated EV road trip in Southeast Asia. It combines island-hopping by ferry, world-class marine life (whale sharks, turtles, sardine runs), unique landscapes (Chocolate Hills, waterfalls), and family-friendly beaches — all connected by manageable driving distances.

Yes, the charging infrastructure outside Cebu City is basic. You'll rely on hotel AC charging and dealership fast chargers. But that's part of the adventure — and the short island distances mean you'll never be more than 100 km from a charge.

For families who love beach destinations, marine encounters, and the novelty of island-hopping in an EV, this trip delivers an unforgettable week at a fraction of the cost of Bali or Thailand.
