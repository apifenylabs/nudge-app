# Hanoi EV Charging Complete City Guide 2026

Hanoi — Vietnam's millennium-old capital of graceful boulevards, chaotic Old Quarter alleyways, and serene lakefront promenades — is rapidly becoming one of Southeast Asia's most EV-friendly cities. With over 50 public charging points now live across 4 competing networks, and VinFast's domestic dominance driving fast infrastructure growth, owning an electric car in Hanoi went from "stressful" to "totally practical" in just 18 months.

This guide covers every charging station in Hanoi, the best EV-friendly hotels for overnight charging, apartment installation tips, cost breakdowns, and the insider hacks that make daily EV life in Hanoi frictionless.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **50+ public charging points** across Hanoi, concentrated in West Lake (Tay Ho), Ba Dinh (government quarter), Cau Giay (tech hub), and major shopping malls
- **4 major networks**: V-Green (VinFast), EVOne, EBOOST, and EVN — all using CCS2 connectors
- **Cost to full charge**: VND 150,000–350,000 (USD $6–15) depending on network and charger type
- **Best area for EV owners**: West Lake (Tay Ho) — most chargers + EV-friendly expat community + street parking availability
- **Hotel charging**: 8+ hotels with dedicated EV parking, mostly free for guests
- **Apartment charging**: Growing rapidly — 15+ condo buildings now have EV parking with installed chargers
- **VinFast VF series dominates** (~70% of EVs on Hanoi roads), but CCS2 is widely available for Tesla, BYD, MG, and other imports

## EV Charging Networks in Hanoi

### 1. V-Green (VinFast) — The Dominant Network

V-Green is Vietnam's largest EV charging network, operated by VinFast. In Hanoi, it accounts for roughly 60% of all public charging points.

**Connector:** CCS2 (compatible with all modern EVs)
**Power:** 60 kW DC, with growing 120 kW installations
**Locations:** Major shopping malls, Vincom centers, VinFast showrooms, selected public parking lots

## Area-by-Area Charging Guide

### Old Quarter & Hoan Kiem (Central Hanoi)

Charging in the Old Quarter is challenging — narrow streets, limited parking, and heritage building constraints. But the edges of Hoan Kiem district are well-served.

**Best options:**
- **Trang Tien Plaza** (EBOOST) — 5 min walk to Hoan Kiem Lake, 60 kW DC
- **VinFast Store Hoan Kiem** (V-Green) — 4x DC 60 kW, walkable to Old Quarter
- **Capella Hanoi** (EVOne) — 120 kW DC, valet charges your car while you explore
- **Hilton Hanoi Opera** (private charger) — 1x Tesla Wall Connector + 1x Type 2
- **Silk Path Grand Hanoi** (private) — 2x Type 2 chargers in basement

**Pro tip:** Park at Trang Tien Plaza (VND 30,000/hour), charge while you walk the Lake, return to a full battery. Cheapest and most practical Old Quarter charging solution.

### West Lake (Tay Ho) — The EV Hub

West Lake is Hanoi's most EV-friendly district. Wide streets, expat population, high EV adoption, and the most charging points per km².

**Best options:**
- **InterContinental Hanoi Westlake** (EVOne) — 120 kW DC, guest or restaurant access
- **Lotte Mall Hanoi** (V-Green) — 4x 120 kW DC
- **Pan Pacific Hanoi** (private) — 2x Type 2, free for restaurant guests
- **Sheraton Hanoi West** (EVOne, nearby) — 2x 120 kW DC
- **Thang Long Coffee charging** — 2x EBOOST 22 kW AC, works great for long brunches
- **Hanoi Club** (private) — 2x Type 2 AC, member/resident access

**Pro tip:** Most West Lake restaurants, cafes, and gyms near Xuan Dieu and Quang An streets now offer wall outlets for granny charging — ask before you park.

### Ba Dinh (Government Quarter)

Embassies, government buildings, and diplomatic residences mean quieter streets but restricted parking.

**Best options:**
- **Lotte Mall Hanoi** (V-Green) — 120 kW DC, Lieu Giai
- **JW Marriott Hanoi** (EVOne) — 4x 240 kW DC, fastest in Hanoi
- **Hanoi International School** (EVOne) — after hours/weekend access
- **Melia Hanoi** (private) — 2x Type 2 AC, guest OK
- **Hotel de l'Opera Hanoi** (private) — 2x Type 2 AC

### Cau Giay & My Dinh (Tech & Corporate Hub)

Home to Vietnam's tech ecosystem, universities, and new urban developments. Excellent charging density.

**Best options:**
- **Keangnam Landmark 72** (EVOne) — 4x 240 kW DC, fastest
- **VinFast Store Cau Giay** (V-Green) — 4x 60 kW DC
- **EBOOST Cau Giay Hub** — 4x 60 kW DC
- **EVN Cau Giay** — 2x 60 kW DC (backup)
- **Indochina Plaza Hanoi** (private) — 2x Type 2

### Hai Ba Trung & Thanh Xuan (South Hub)

Residential corridors connecting to the QL1A highway south.

**Best options:**
- **Royal City** (V-Green) — 8x 120 kW DC, largest single installation
- **Times City** (V-Green) — 6x 120 kW DC
- **Vincom Ba Trieu** (V-Green) — 6x 60 kW DC
- **EVN Hai Ba Trung** — 2x 60 kW DC

## Apartment & Condo EV Charging

Hanoi's condo charging situation has improved dramatically. As of mid-2026, approximately 15 major residential complexes have installed shared EV chargers, with another 20+ in the pipeline.

**Vinhomes complexes** (Vinhomes Riverside, Vinhomes Westpoint, Vinhomes Metropolis, Vinhomes Smart City) lead with V-Green chargers in visitor parking and growing resident-installed options.

**Ecopark Township** (30 min from central Hanoi) has 6x EVOne 120 kW DC chargers and allows resident installation of home wall boxes with management approval.

**Gamuda Gardens** and **Vinhomes Green Bay** both have EBOOST AC charging for residents.

**Installation process for private apartments:**
1. Get written management approval (usually requires certified electrician)
2. Install a 7–22 kW Type 2 wall box (approx. VND 15–25 million / USD $620–1,030)
3. Many buildings use a shared meter — you pay VND 4,000–5,000/kWh (slightly higher than home tariff)
4. Recommend: Zencar or Teison wall boxes available via Shopee Vietnam (VND 8–15 million)

## Hanoi EV Charging: 6 Insider Tips

1. **Avoid midday charging at mall chargers** — peak hours (11:30–13:30) mean long queues at Vincom Ba Trieu and Royal City. Charge before 10 AM or after 2 PM.

2. **Granny charging in the Old Quarter** — Many Old Quarter hotels and Airbnbs will let you run a granny charger from their restaurant/shop outlet overnight. Always ask in advance ("Tôi sạc xe điện qua ổ cắm thường được không?").

3. **VinFast showrooms are your safety net** — There are 12 VinFast stores in Hanoi. Even if the app shows all chargers busy, showrooms usually have 1–2 reserved spots. Ask politely, and they often activate a charger for you.

4. **Tay Ho Sunday strategy** — On Sundays, Xuan Dieu and Quang An streets close for the Walking Street festival. Park at InterContinental Westlake, charge at 120 kW, walk the lake. Perfect Sunday.

5. **Highway departure charging** — If leaving Hanoi southbound on QL1A, top up at the Thang Long Highway EVN station *before* you hit traffic. The V-Green station at Phap Van (15 km south) is usually busy.

6. **Download Grab EV** — Grab's EV taxi fleet uses chargers across Hanoi. If you're stuck, ordering a Grab EV and asking the driver where they charge can reveal hidden chargers not on any map.
