# Top 10 EV-Friendly Hotels in Malaysia: Best Family Stays with EV Charging 2026

**Last updated: May 18, 2026**

## 1. The Ritz-Carlton, Kuala Lumpur

**EV Rating: 9.7/10** | ⚡ **Chargers:** 2× DC 150 kW (Tesla Supercharger) + 4× AC 22 kW (ChargeSini) | **Cost:** Free for guests

KL's premier luxury hotel has Malaysia's best in-hotel EV charging setup. Two Tesla V3 Superchargers in the valet basement deliver 150 kW — enough to add 300 km of range in just 15 minutes. The ChargeSini AC units handle non-Tesla EVs with 22 kW speed.

- **Connectors:** CCS2 (DC), Type 2 (AC), NACS (Tesla)
- **Charging experience:** Valet handles everything. Drop your keys at the entrance and the team rotates cars so yours is always charged. The hotel's "Little Ritz Kids" program includes a scavenger hunt that ends at the charging station.
- **Nearby attractions:** Petronas Twin Towers (0.8 km), Aquaria KLCC (1.2 km), KL Tower, KLCC Park
- **Family highlights:** 6th-floor pool with KL Tower views, dedicated children's wading area, afternoon tea with macarons
- **Pro tip:** Request afternoon charging (3-5 PM) — the valet team pre-cools your cabin, a lifesaver in KL's year-round heat.

## EV Charging in Malaysia: What You Need to Know

### Charging Networks

| Network | Coverage | Connector | App Required | Payment |
|---------|----------|-----------|:------------:|---------|
| ChargeSini | Nationwide | CCS2, Type 2 | Yes | Credit card, e-wallet |
| Gentari | Highway stops + cities | CCS2, Type 2 | Yes | Gentari app |
| JomCharge | Peninsular Malaysia | Type 2 | Yes | JomCharge app |
| Tesla Supercharger | KL, Penang, JB | NACS (CCS2 adapter included) | Yes | Tesla app |
| EV Connection | Langkawi, Penang | Type 2 | Yes | EV Connection app |

### Connector Standards

- **Malaysia uses CCS2 and Type 2** — same as Thailand and Europe
- Tesla owners need a CCS2-to-NACS adapter for non-Tesla chargers (though Tesla Superchargers are now open to all in Malaysia)
- Bring your own Type 2 cable for AC chargers

### Apps to Download Before Your Trip

1. **ChargeSini** — the largest network, covers all major highways and cities
2. **Gentari** — best for highway rest stops (PLUS highway R&Rs)
3. **JomCharge** — popular in Penang and northern Malaysia
4. **PlugShare** — community-checked station reliability

## Tips for Booking EV-Friendly Hotels in Malaysia

- **The Singapore-KL-Penang run** (700 km): Charge twice — once at the Ayer Keroh R&R (Gentari 180 kW) and once at the Ipoh R&R. Book hotels with destination charging for overnight top-ups.
- **KL-Langkawi**: Drive to Kuala Kedah (450 km), charge at the Gentari station there, take the ferry. Most Langkawi hotels have AC charging.
- **Call ahead** — even listed "EV-friendly" hotels sometimes have occupied or broken chargers. Call 24 hours before arrival to confirm.
- **Check the connector** — some older chargers use Type 1 (J1772). Bring an adapter if driving a European or Chinese EV.
