# Top 10 EV-Friendly Hotels in Thailand: Best Family Stays with EV Charging 2026

**Last updated: May 24, 2026**

Thailand is Southeast Asia's premier road trip destination, and the EV revolution has well and truly arrived. With over 9,000 public charging ports nationwide — led by PEA Volta (4,500+ ports), EleX by EGAT (2,000+), and PTT Station's expanding network — families can now explore the Kingdom from the Gulf of Thailand to the Andaman Sea with confidence. Destination charging at hotels has become a competitive advantage, with luxury resorts and boutique properties alike installing everything from 150 kW DC fast chargers to overnight AC stations.

We've ranked Thailand's top 10 EV-friendly hotels based on charger availability, speed, reliability, location, and overall family experience. Whether you're road-tripping from Bangkok to Phuket, island-hopping in Krabi, or exploring the mountains of Chiang Mai, these hotels will keep your EV juiced and your family smiling.

## 1. Siam Kempinski Hotel — Bangkok

**EV Rating: 9.7/10** | ⚡ **Chargers:** 2× DC 150 kW (PEA Volta) + 4× AC 22 kW | **Cost:** Free for guests

Bangkok's most luxurious family hotel sits in the heart of the city at Siam Square, connected to Siam Paragon mall. The Kempinski has invested heavily in EV infrastructure with the fastest DC chargers on our list — a partnership with PEA Volta that delivers a full charge in under 20 minutes for most EVs.

- **Connectors:** CCS2 (DC), Type 2 (AC)
- **Charging experience:** Secure, covered underground parking with dedicated EV bays. The 150 kW PEA Volta DC units are app-activated through the PEA Volta app but the hotel absorbs all charging costs for registered guests. Simply park, plug in, and return to a fully charged vehicle.
- **Nearby attractions:** Siam Paragon (connected walkway), CentralWorld, Jim Thompson House, Lumpini Park, MBK Center
- **Family highlights:** Three outdoor pools including a lagoon-style children's pool, Siam Kempinski Kids' Club with daily activities (Thai dance lessons, fruit carving), connecting family suites, direct BTS Skywalk access
- **Pro tip:** Book a Siam Suite with direct pool access — your kids can jump straight into the lagoon pool from your terrace while your EV charges in the garage downstairs. The Siam Paragon basement has an additional 4× PEA Volta 50 kW chargers as backup.

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Siam Kempinski Hotel Bangkok](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Siam+Kempinski+Hotel+Bangkok&aid=2875669)

## 3. Anantara Chiang Mai Resort

**EV Rating: 9.4/10** | ⚡ **Chargers:** 2× DC 60 kW (PEA Volta) + 2× AC 22 kW | **Cost:** Free for guests

Set along the Mae Ping River in the heart of Chiang Mai's Old City, the Anantara is the most EV-ready hotel in Northern Thailand. The DC fast chargers are in the resort's secure parking area, and the hotel gifted all front-desk staff with basic EV charging knowledge for troubleshooting.

A 30-minute charge adds 180 km — plenty for exploring Doi Suthep, the weekend markets, and the mountain roads around Chiang Mai. The hotel also provides a complimentary Type 2 cable for guests who arrive without one.

## EV Charging in Thailand: What You Need to Know

### Charging Networks

| Network | Coverage | Connector | App Required | Payment |
|---------|----------|-----------|:------------:|---------|
| PEA Volta | Nationwide (4,500+ ports) | CCS2, Type 2, CHAdeMO | Yes (PEA Volta) | Credit card, TrueMoney Wallet |
| EleX by EGAT | Major highways, Phuket, Bangkok | CCS2 | Yes (EleX) | Credit card, Rabbit Line Pay |
| PTT Station EV | Highways (PTT gas stations) | CCS2 | Yes (PTT EV Station) | Credit card, PTT Blue Card |
| EA Anywhere | Southern Thailand | CCS2 | Yes (EA Anywhere) | Credit card |
| Private-Hotel | Hotels & condos | Type 2 (AC) | Varies | Usually free for guests |

### Connector Standards

- **Thailand uses CCS2 and Type 2 (AC)** — same standard as Europe and Vietnam
- CHAdeMO is available at some PEA Volta stations (mostly for older Nissan Leafs)
- Tesla owners need a CCS2-to-NACS adapter for most public chargers
- Bring your own Type 2 cable for hotel and condo AC chargers
- Most DC fast chargers in Thailand use CCS2 with tethered cables

### Apps to Download Before Your Trip

1. **PEA Volta** — Thailand's largest network, essential for highway travel
2. **EleX by EGAT** — Growing fast, excellent coverage in Phuket and resort areas
3. **PTT EV Station** — The best network along major highways (think Shell Recharge but Thai)
4. **EA Anywhere** — Important for southern Thailand (Chumphon, Surat Thani, Phuket)
5. **PlugShare** — Community-verified charger reliability across Thailand
6. **Google Maps** — Download offline maps for remote areas (national parks, islands)

## Tips for Booking EV-Friendly Hotels in Thailand

- **The Bangkok-Phuket Run** (850 km): Thailand's best-covered EV corridor. PTT Station has DC fast chargers (120-150 kW) every 80-120 km along Highway 4. Key stops: PTT Phetchaburi (km 170), PTT Prachuap Khiri Khan (km 310), EA Anywhere Chumphon (km 460). Book hotels in Hua Hin and Phuket with destination charging.
- **Bangkok-Chiang Mai** (700 km): Route via Highway 1 with PTT chargers at Nakhon Sawan (km 240) and Lampang (km 520). Central Festival Chiang Mai has 50+ EV charging stalls. Anantara Chiang Mai charges for free.
- **Phuket-Krabi-Koh Samui loop**: Requires ferry crossings. Krabi and Koh Samui both have PEA Volta chargers at ferry piers. Top up before boarding the car ferry.
- **Call ahead** — confirm charger availability, type (DC vs AC), speed, and whether charging is included
- **Bring a portable charger** — Thai 220V outlets give you about 15 km/hour. Overnight at a friend's house or homestay adds 150 km of emergency range.
- **Check PlugShare before booking** — community reports often reveal if a hotel's charger is actually working or has been blocked by a parked ICE vehicle.
- **Book EV-friendly hotels with guaranteed EV bays** — some hotels have specific EV parking spots that can be reserved in advance.

### Driving Tips for Families

- **Traffic**: Bangkok traffic is intense — use your hotel's EV charging to explore by BTS/Skytrain within the city, then use your EV for intercity road trips
- **Ferry to Koh Samui**: The Raja Ferry from Donsak (Surat Thani) to Koh Samui accepts EVs. Book your ticket in advance and arrive 1 hour early
- **Range anxiety in the mountains**: Chiang Mai and Mae Hong Son's mountain roads significantly reduce EV range. Always charge to 80%+ before heading into the hills
- **Regenerative braking**: The descent from Doi Suthep or Phu Tub Berk can add 10-15% range — enjoy the free energy!
