# Top 10 EV-Friendly Family Resorts in Bali (2026 Guide)

**Last updated: May 17, 2026**

Bali is Asia's ultimate family holiday destination, and for EV-driving families, it's becoming even better. The island's compact size — just 153 km wide — means no journey takes more than 2 hours, and a growing network of SPKLU (government) and hotel-installed chargers keeps you powered throughout your stay.

We've ranked Bali's top 10 EV-friendly family resorts based on charger availability, kids' club quality, location, and overall family experience. Every resort on this list has verified on-site charging as of May 2026.

## 1. Ayana Resort Bali — Jimbaran

**Family EV Score: 9.8/10** | ⚡ **Chargers:** 2× DC 50 kW + 4× AC 22 kW | **Cost:** Free for guests

Bali's most EV-ready resort. Ayana sits on 90 hectares of clifftop paradise with 12 swimming pools, a private beach, and the iconic Rock Bar. Their charging setup is unmatched.

- **Charging experience:** The DC charger in the main parking area can take an EV from 20% to 80% in about 45 minutes. Hand your keys to the valet on arrival and they'll rotate vehicles through the chargers.
- **Kids' club:** Ayana Kids Club offers supervised Balinese dance lessons, batik painting, and fish feeding. Open 9 AM-5 PM daily. Free for guests.
- **Family highlights:** Private beach with calm waters, 12 pools, Rock Bar sunset (kids welcome before 7 PM), giant chess and ping pong
- **Nearby attractions:** Jimbaran Bay seafood, GWK Cultural Park, Uluwatu Temple (25 min drive)
- **Best villa:** Ocean Cliff Villa — private plunge pool and the butler handles your EV charging

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Ayana Resort Bali](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Ayana+Resort+Bali&aid=2875669)

## Tips for a Smooth Bali EV Family Holiday

- **Rent the right EV:** MG 4 is the best choice for Bali — it's 15 cm narrower than a Tesla Model 3, making it far easier on Ubud's tight roads.
- **Bring a Level 1 charger:** Many private villas and smaller hotels don't have EV charging. A portable charger from a standard Indonesian 230V outlet gives you 15 km/hour — 150 km overnight.
- **Book 3 months ahead for peak season:** July-August and December-January are peak family season. Resorts like Ayana and Four Seasons Sayan book out 3+ months in advance.
- **Trust the valet:** Most high-end Bali resorts offer valet charging. Hand over your keys at check-in and your car is fully charged by morning.
- **Kids' clubs = charging time:** The best family resorts have 8+ hour kids' club hours. Drop the kids and charge your EV simultaneously — it's a productivity hack for parents.
