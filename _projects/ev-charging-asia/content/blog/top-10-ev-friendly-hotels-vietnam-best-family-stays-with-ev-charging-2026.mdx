## 1. InterContinental Saigon — Ho Chi Minh City

**EV Rating: 9.6/10** | ⚡ **Chargers:** 2× DC 60 kW (VinFast V-Green) + 4× AC 22 kW | **Cost:** Free for guests

HCMC's leading luxury hotel has embraced Vietnam's VinFast ecosystem with purpose-built DC fast chargers in the basement carpark. The dedicated EV bays are covered, well-lit, and monitored by 24/7 security.

- **Connectors:** CCS2 (DC), Type 2 (AC)
- **Charging experience:** Self-park on B2 near the elevator bank. A 25-minute charge adds 180 km of range — perfect for exploring HCMC or heading out on a Mekong Delta day trip. The hotel uses the VinFast E-Pin app for billing but absorbs the cost for guests.
- **Nearby attractions:** Notre-Dame Cathedral (200 m), Ben Thanh Market (500 m), Saigon Zoo & Botanic Gardens (1 km), Bui Vien Walking Street
- **Family highlights:** Rooftop infinity pool with city views, kids' club, Vietnamese cooking classes for children, spacious family suites
- **Pro tip:** Book a Club InterContinental room for lounge access — charge your EV while enjoying complimentary afternoon tea and evening cocktails with skyline views.

## EV Charging in Vietnam: What You Need to Know

### Charging Networks

| Network | Coverage | Connector | App Required | Payment |
|---------|----------|-----------|:------------:|---------|
| VinFast V-Green | Nationwide (4,000+ ports) | CCS2 | Yes (E-Pin) | E-Pin wallet, credit card |
| EVOne | Da Nang, Nha Trang, Dalat | CCS2, Type 2 | Yes | Credit card |
| Charge & Go | HCMC, Hanoi | Type 2 | Yes | Mobile wallet |

### Connector Standards

- **Vietnam uses CCS2 and Type 2** — same as Thailand and Europe
- VinFast's V-Green network only supports CCS2
- Tesla owners need a CCS2-to-NACS adapter
- Bring your own Type 2 cable for hotel AC chargers

### Apps to Download Before Your Trip

1. **VinFast E-Pin** — essential for all V-Green chargers, recharge via wallet or international card
2. **EVOne** — growing network in central Vietnam
3. **PlugShare** — community-checked station reliability
4. **Google Maps** — download offline maps for coastal areas with weak signal

## Tips for Booking EV-Friendly Hotels in Vietnam

- **The HCMC-Hoi An-Da Nang run** (1,050 km): Needs 2-3 charging stops at V-Green stations (Long Khanh, Phan Rang, Nha Trang, Quang Ngai). Book hotels with destination charging at Nha Trang and Hoi An.
- **Hanoi-Ha Long Bay** (170 km): Easy single-charge trip. Charge at the VinFast station in Hanoi before departing, then top up at Vinpearl Ha Long's mainland chargers.
- **Hanoi-Sapa** (300 km): More challenging — VinFast coverage gaps between Hanoi and Lao Cai. Check PlugShare before attempting.
- **Call ahead** — confirm charger type (DC vs AC), speed, and whether charging is free or paid
- **Bring a portable charger** — Vietnamese 220V outlets give you ~15 km/hour. Overnight = 150 km, enough for local exploration.
