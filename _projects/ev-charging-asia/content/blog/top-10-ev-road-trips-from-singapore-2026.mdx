# Top 10 EV Road Trips from Singapore (2026 Guide)

**Last updated: May 17, 2026**

Singapore is the perfect launchpad for EV road trips. The island's small size means you're never more than 30 minutes from the Malaysia border, and the North-South Expressway offers some of Southeast Asia's best-maintained highways with charging stations at every major R&R stop.

We've ranked the top 10 EV road trips from Singapore based on driving ease, charging reliability, family-friendliness, and overall adventure factor. Whether you have a weekend or a full week, there's an EV trip for you.

## 1. Singapore → Kuala Lumpur via Malacca (350 km)

**Trip Rating: 9.8/10** | **Duration:** 3-4 days | **Difficulty:** Very Easy

The classic first EV road trip from Singapore. Flat highways, excellent charging infrastructure, and world-class stops for the whole family.

- **Route:** Singapore → Johor Bahru → Ayer Keroh (Malacca) → KL
- **Charging:** Shell Recharge Pagoh (180 kW), Malacca DC (50 kW), KLCC (50 kW)
- **Total charging cost:** ~SGD 20-30
- **Kid highlights:** LEGOLAND Malaysia, Malacca Duck Tour, KL Bird Park, Petrosains Discovery Centre
- **Where to stay:** The Majestic Malacca (has destination charging) or Mandarin Oriental KL

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [The Majestic Malacca](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=The+Majestic+Malacca&aid=2875669)
**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Mandarin Oriental Kuala Lumpur](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Mandarin+Oriental+Kuala+Lumpur&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** Leave Singapore before 7 AM to beat causeway traffic. The 180 kW charger at Pagoh R&R gives you 80% in 15 minutes — perfect while kids play at the R&R playground.

## 2. Johor Bahru Weekend Escape (15 km)

**Trip Rating: 9.6/10** | **Duration:** 1-2 days | **Difficulty:** Very Easy

No charging needed for this quick hop across the causeway. JB offers family attractions at half the price of Singapore.

- **Route:** Singapore → Woodlands Checkpoint → JB City Centre
- **Charging:** Not needed — your EV has plenty of range for JB exploration and return
- **Kid highlights:** LEGOLAND Malaysia, Angry Birds Activity Park, Austin Heights Water Park, Johor Premium Outlets
- **Where to stay:** DoubleTree by Hilton JB (chargers in carpark) or Amari Johor Bahru

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [DoubleTree by Hilton Johor Bahru](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=DoubleTree+by+Hilton+Johor+Bahru&aid=2875669)
**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Amari Johor Bahru](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Amari+Johor+Bahru&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** Travel Tuesday-Thursday for shortest causeway queues. The return queue at 4-7 PM Sunday can be 2+ hours.

## 3. Malacca Heritage Trip (240 km)

**Trip Rating: 9.4/10** | **Duration:** 2-3 days | **Difficulty:** Easy**

A shorter version of the KL trip, focusing on Malacca's UNESCO-listed heritage. Perfect for a long weekend.

- **Route:** Singapore → Pagoh → Ayer Keroh (Malacca)
- **Charging:** Shell Recharge Pagoh (180 kW) — one stop is enough for the full round trip
- **Kid highlights:** Malacca Duck Tour, Malacca River Cruise, Jonker Walk Night Market, The Shore Oceanarium
- **Where to stay:** The Majestic Malacca (AC charging) or Casa del Rio

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [The Majestic Malacca](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=The+Majestic+Malacca&aid=2875669)
**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Casa del Rio Melaka](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Casa+del+Rio+Melaka&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** Jonker Walk Night Market runs Friday-Sunday only. Plan your trip to include at least one evening there for satay celup and cendol.

## 4. Penang Island Adventure (740 km)

**Trip Rating: 9.2/10** | **Duration:** 4-5 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate**

The full North-South Expressway run from Singapore to Penang. Malaysia's western corridor has excellent charger coverage.

- **Route:** Singapore → Pagoh → Ipoh → Penang
- **Charging:** Pagoh R&R (180 kW), PLUS Tapah R&R (120 kW), Ipoh Selatan (120 kW), Penang Gurney Plaza (22 kW)
- **Kid highlights:** Batu Caves, Ipoh cave temples, Penang Hill funicular, street food trail
- **Where to stay:** DoubleTree Resort by Hilton Penang (chargers available) or The Edison George Town

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [DoubleTree Resort by Hilton Penang](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=DoubleTree+Resort+by+Hilton+Penang&aid=2875669)
**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [The Edison George Town](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=The+Edison+George+Town&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** The Penang Bridge is EV-friendly — avoid the ferry which has no charging. Park at Gurney Plaza for AC charging while you enjoy the hawker centre.

## 5. Ipoh & Cameron Highlands (580 km)

**Trip Rating: 9.0/10** | **Duration:** 3-4 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate**

Ipoh's heritage and Cameron Highlands' cool tea plantations make this a unique EV trip. The climb to Cameron Highlands consumes battery — plan accordingly.

- **Route:** Singapore → Pagoh → Ipoh → Cameron Highlands → return
- **Charging:** Pagoh R&R (180 kW), Ipoh Selatan (120 kW), Cameron Highlands (50 kW at Equatorial Hotel)
- **Kid highlights:** Ipoh cave temples, Lost World of Tambun, tea plantation tours, strawberry picking
- **Where to stay:** The Banjaran Hotsprings Retreat Ipoh (AC charging) or Cameron Highlands Resort

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [The Banjaran Hotsprings Retreat](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=The+Banjaran+Hotsprings+Retreat&aid=2875669)
**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Cameron Highlands Resort](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Cameron+Highlands+Resort&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** The Cameron Highlands ascent from Tapah is 45 km of winding uphill — expect 15-20% extra battery consumption. Charge fully in Ipoh before heading up.

## 6. Desaru Coast Getaway (80 km)

**Trip Rating: 9.3/10** | **Duration:** 2-3 days | **Difficulty:** Very Easy

Desaru is Singapore's closest beach destination, reachable via the new coastal highway. No charging needed for the round trip.

- **Route:** Singapore → Woodlands/Link2 → Desaru Coast
- **Charging:** Not needed, but available at Desaru Coast Conference Centre if you're staying multiple days
- **Kid highlights:** Desaru Coast Adventure Waterpark, Fruit Farm, ostrich farm, firefly park
- **Where to stay:** Anantara Desaru Coast Resort & Villas (EV charging available) or The Westin Desaru Coast

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Anantara Desaru Coast Resort & Villas](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Anantara+Desaru+Coast+Resort+%26+Villas&aid=2875669)
**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [The Westin Desaru Coast](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=The+Westin+Desaru+Coast&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** Use the Second Link (Tuas) to Mersing instead of the causeway — less traffic and the coastal road is a scenic drive.

## 7. Genting Highlands (360 km)

**Trip Rating: 8.8/10** | **Duration:** 2-3 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate

Genting's cool mountain air and theme parks make it a hit with families. The steep climb is manageable with careful planning.

- **Route:** Singapore → Pagoh → KL → Genting Highlands
- **Charging:** Pagoh R&R (180 kW), KLCC/One Utama (50 kW), Genting Skyway parking (50 kW)
- **Kid highlights:** Genting SkyWorlds Theme Park, Awana Cable Car, Snow World, Ripley's Believe It Or Not
- **Where to stay:** Resorts World Genting — First World Hotel has AC charging in the parking complex

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [First World Hotel Genting Highlands](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=First+World+Hotel+Genting+Highlands&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** Charge to 80%+ at KL before the Genting ascent. The 20 km climb from Gohtong Jaya gains 1,000m elevation — plenty of regen on the way down.

## 8. Kuantan & Cherating Beach (370 km)

**Trip Rating: 8.7/10** | **Duration:** 3-4 days | **Difficulty:** Easy

Malaysia's east coast offers quieter beaches and a more relaxed vibe. Chargers are less dense here, so plan carefully.

- **Route:** Singapore → Mersing → Cherating → Kuantan
- **Charging:** ChargEV stations at Mersing (50 kW) and Kuantan (50 kW)
- **Kid highlights:** Cherating Turtle Sanctuary, Kuantan Sungai Pandan Waterfall, Teluk Cempedak beach
- **Where to stay:** Club Med Cherating Beach (no public EV charging — bring Level 1 charger) or The Zenith Kuantan (has AC charging)

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Club Med Cherating Beach](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Club+Med+Cherating+Beach&aid=2875669)
**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [The Zenith Kuantan](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=The+Zenith+Kuantan&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** East coast chargers are sparse — charge at every opportunity. The 240 km stretch from Mersing to Kuantan has zero reliable DC fast chargers.

## 9. Phuket, Thailand Epic (1,500 km)

**Trip Rating: 8.5/10** | **Duration:** 7-10 days | **Difficulty:** Hard**

For the truly adventurous, a Singapore-to-Phuket EV road trip is the ultimate Southeast Asian road trip. Crossing two international borders requires preparation.

- **Route:** Singapore → KL → Penang → Hat Yai → Phuket
- **Charging:** Extensive network through Malaysia; PTT stations in Thailand. Cross-border at Padang Besar.
- **Kid highlights:** Penang street food, Hat Yai floating markets, Phuket Elephant Sanctuary, Phi Phi Islands
- **Where to stay:** Anantara Mai Khao Phuket (DC 50 kW) or The Slate Phuket (AC 22 kW)

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Anantara Mai Khao Phuket](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Anantara+Mai+Khao+Phuket&aid=2875669)
**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [The Slate Phuket](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=The+Slate+Phuket&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** You'll need both Malaysian (Type 2/CCS2) and Thai (CCS2) charging apps. Download PTT EV Station PluZ before crossing. Ensure your insurance covers both countries.

## 10. Kota Tinggi & Mersing Nature (140 km)

**Trip Rating: 8.6/10** | **Duration:** 1-2 days | **Difficulty:** Very Easy**

A short nature-focused trip from Singapore to Johor's wilder east coast. Waterfalls, fireflies, and the gateway to Malaysia's islands.

- **Route:** Singapore → Kota Tinggi → Mersing
- **Charging:** Not needed for round trip — 140 km total
- **Kid highlights:** Kota Tinggi Waterfall, Mersing firefly cruise, crocodile farm, Endau Rompin National Park
- **Where to stay:** Mersing's EVO Hotel or Kota Tinggi Golf & Resort

**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [EVO Hotel Mersing](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=EVO+Hotel+Mersing&aid=2875669)
**→ Book this hotel on Booking.com:** [Kota Tinggi Golf & Resort](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Kota+Tinggi+Golf+%26+Resort&aid=2875669)
- **Pro tip:** Bring mosquito repellent — the waterfall and firefly areas have plenty of mosquitoes at dusk. The waterfall has a 3 km dirt access road — EVs with good ground clearance (BYD Atto 3) handle it well.

## Border Crossing Guide: Singapore → Malaysia

### What You Need

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Passport | Valid 6+ months, no visa needed for most nationalities |
| Vehicle registration | Original V5 or equivalent |
| Autopass card | Malaysia Autopass (RM 10) for the Touch 'n Go lane |
| Touch 'n Go card | Topped up — minimum RM 100 suggested |
| Insurance | Malaysia third-party insurance (RM 20-30, buy at border) |
| RFID tag | For Singapore tolls — ensure your in-vehicle unit works |

### Best Times to Cross

| Crossing | Weekday Best Time | Weekend Avoid |
|----------|:-----------------:|:-------------:|
| Woodlands Causeway | Before 7 AM or after 10 PM | Friday 4-9 PM (out), Sunday 3-8 PM (return) |
| Tuas Second Link | Before 8 AM or after 9 PM | Saturday 8-11 AM (out), Sunday 2-7 PM (return) |
