# Da Nang EV Charging Complete City Guide 2026

Da Nang — Vietnam's coastal gem straddling the Han River, with the Marble Mountains to the south and the legendary Hai Van Pass to the north — is quietly becoming the country's most EV-friendly city for the growing number of residents and tourists driving electric. While Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City have more charging points in absolute terms, Da Nang's compact layout, wide boulevards, and rapid resort infrastructure investment mean that range anxiety is lower here than almost anywhere in Vietnam.

This guide covers every charging station in Da Nang, the best EV-friendly resorts along My Khe and Non Nuoc beaches, condo charging options for residents, cost comparisons with other Vietnamese cities, road trip planning for Hai Van Pass and Hoi An, and the insider hacks that make EV travel in Da Nang genuinely enjoyable.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **30+ public charging points** across Da Nang, concentrated around the city centre (Hai Chau), Son Tra peninsula, the beach strip (Ngũ Hành Sơn), and shopping malls
- **2 major networks**: V-Green (VinFast, dominant) and EV One (premium), plus growing hotel-installed Type 2 AC chargers
- **Cost to full charge**: VND 225,000–412,000 (USD $9–17) depending on network and charger type
- **Best area for EV owners**: My Khe Beach strip (Ngũ Hành Sơn / Phước Mỹ) — most resort charging, wide streets, easy parking
- **Resort charging**: 8+ luxury resorts with dedicated EV bays, most offering free overnight charging for guests
- **Apartment/condo charging**: Growing fast — 10+ beachfront condo buildings now have shared EV parking
- **Smaller city, less anxiety**: Da Nang is only 30 km north to south — even budget EVs with 200 km range cover the city multiple times on a single charge

## EV Charging Networks in Da Nang

### 1. V-Green (VinFast) — The Dominant Network

V-Green is Vietnam's largest EV charging network, operated by VinFast. In Da Nang, it accounts for roughly 70% of all public charging points — reflecting the city's strong VinFast adoption.

**Connector:** CCS2 (compatible with all modern EVs), GB/T (VinFast native)
**Power:** 60 kW DC at most locations, with growing 120 kW installations at major malls
**Locations:** Shopping malls, VinMart convenience stores, VinFast showrooms, selected petrol stations

## Area-by-Area Charging Guide

### My Khe Beach & Phước Mỹ (The Beach Strip)

The beachfront strip along Vo Nguyen Giap street is Da Nang's most EV-friendly area. Resort charging, wide boulevards, and easy parking make daily EV living a breeze.

**Best options:**
- **AEON Mall Da Nang** (V-Green) — 6x 120 kW DC, newest and fastest
- **Vincom Center Da Nang** (V-Green) — 6x 60 kW DC, beach road
- **Hyatt Regency Danang** (EV One) — 120 kW DC
- **Premier Village Danang** (EV One) — 120 kW DC
- **Pullman Danang Beach Resort** — 2x Type 2 AC
- **Melia Danang** — 2x Type 2 AC

**Pro tip:** Park at AEON Mall Da Nang (free parking), connect to a 120 kW DC charger, walk across the footbridge to My Khe Beach for a swim. Return in 30 minutes to 80% charge.

### City Centre (Hai Chau & Thanh Khe)

The business and administrative heart of Da Nang has good charging density thanks to VinFast's city coverage.

**Best options:**
- **Lotte Mart Da Nang** (V-Green) — 4x 60 kW DC
- **VinFast Store Da Nang** (V-Green) — 4x 60 kW DC, Nguyen Van Linh
- **Co-op Mart Da Nang** (V-Green) — 4x 60 kW DC
- **Big C Da Nang** (V-Green) — 4x 60 kW DC
- **Da Nang International Airport** (V-Green) — 4x 60 kW DC
- **Danang Gold** (EV One) — 2x 240 kW DC, fastest in city centre

**Pro tip:** Lotte Mart and VinFast Store are within 1 km of the Da Nang Cathedral (churches) and Han Market. Charge while you explore the city centre on foot.

### Son Tra Peninsula

Home to the InterContinental Sun Peninsula Resort and the Linh Ung Pagoda (with Vietnam's tallest Lady Buddha statue). Charging is limited to the resort.

**Best options:**
- **InterContinental Danang** (EV One) — 120 kW DC (valet charging)

**Pro tip:** If you're not a resort guest, charge in the city centre before heading up Son Tra. The mountain road to the pagoda and viewpoints is steep — expect 20–30% higher consumption than flat driving. A full charge from the city gives you more than enough range for the round trip.

### Non Nuoc & Marble Mountain Area

The southern beach stretch near Marble Mountains, Non Nuoc Beach, and the Danang Marriott Resort.

**Best options:**
- **Danang Marriott Resort & Spa** (private) — 2x Type 2 AC
- **TIA Wellness Resort** (private) — 2x Type 2 AC
- **Naman Retreat** (private) — 2x Type 2 AC

**Pro tip:** This area connects directly to Hoi An (30 min south). Charge at your resort overnight, then drive to Hoi An Ancient Town fully charged. Marble Mountain's parking lot does not have EV charging.

## Apartment & Condo EV Charging

Da Nang's beachfront living culture means many residents live in high-rise apartments with underground parking. EV charging in these buildings is growing fast.

**Beachfront buildings with EV charging:**
- **Monarchy by the Beach** (Phước Mỹ) — 2x Type 2 AC, resident access
- **The Olea** (Ngũ Hành Sơn) — 2x Type 2 AC, visitor access
- **Sun Cosmo Residence** (Phước Mỹ) — EV parking zone with owner-installed boxes
- **Harmony Tower** (Ngũ Hành Sơn) — 2x V-Green DC, shared resident access
- **Risemount Premier Danang** (Phước Mỹ) — Management approved for wall box installation
- **Cocobay Towers** (Ngũ Hành Sơn) — Shared AC chargers in basement

**Installation process for private apartments:**
1. Speak to building management — most beachfront condos now have EV policies
2. Install a 7–22 kW Type 2 wall box (approx. VND 15–25 million / USD $620–1,030)
3. Many buildings charge a flat VND 3,500–5,000/kWh via shared meter
4. Popular brands: Zencar, Teison, or BYD wall boxes — available via Shopee Vietnam

**Pro tip:** If your building doesn't have charging yet, ask about granny charging (slow charging from a standard wall outlet). Da Nang's mild weather means battery thermal management uses less energy, making granny charging overnight more efficient than in hotter cities.

## Road Trips from Da Nang

Da Nang is the perfect base for EV road trips in central Vietnam. Its central location puts Hoi An, Hue, and Ba Na Hills all within a single charge.

### Hai Van Pass (Da Nang to Hue) — 100 km, 2–3 hours

The Hai Van Pass is Vietnam's most iconic driving road — 21 km of twisting mountain asphalt with panoramic views of the South China Sea. It's also a serious EV challenge.

**Charging strategy:**
- Start with a full charge in Da Nang (AEON Mall or Vincom Center)
- Consumption increases 30–50% on the climb — budget 200 km of range for this 100 km segment
- No charging stations on the pass itself — the nearest is at Lang Co Beach (15 km north of the pass)
- Once you descend into Hue, V-Green DC chargers are at Vincom Hue and AEON Mall Hue

**Pro tip:** The pass is busiest from 9 AM to 11 AM (tourist buses). Start at 6:30 AM for near-empty roads, misty views, and cooler temperatures that help battery efficiency. Carpooling or driving downhill regeneratively helps preserve range.

### Hoi An (30 km south, 40 min)

The easiest EV day trip from Da Nang. Hoi An's ancient town is a UNESCO World Heritage site, and the road is flat and well-maintained.

**Charging:**
- **Four Seasons The Nam Hai** — 4x Type 2 AC (if staying or dining)
- **Hoi An Vincom** — 2x V-Green 60 kW DC, near ancient town
- Most Hoi An hotels have 1–2 Type 2 chargers for guests

**Pro tip:** Your My Khe Beach hotel charging will easily cover a Da Nang → Hoi An round trip (60 km total). No need to charge in Hoi An unless you're staying overnight.

### Ba Na Hills (40 km west, 1 hour)

Home to the famous Golden Bridge, Ba Na Hills is a popular day trip. The road climbs 1,400 metres in elevation.

**Charging strategy:**
- Charge fully in Da Nang before departure
- Expect 40–50% higher consumption on the climb
- No public charging at Ba Na Hills cable car station — budget your return range carefully
- A Tesla Model 3 or VinFast VF8 makes the round trip on a single 80% charge from Da Nang
- Budget EVs (VF5, VF6) may need a top-up — charge at AEON Mall Da Nang before heading up

### Son Tra Peninsula (15 km round trip)

The "Monkey Mountain" loop is the most accessible EV day trip.

**Charging:**
- Charge at Vincom or InterContinental if you're a guest
- The round trip from city centre is only 30 km — any EV can do it without charging
- Son Tra's dense forest means cooler temperatures — good for battery efficiency

## 5 Insider Tips for EV Life in Da Nang

1. **Resort charging is your best friend** — If you're staying at a beach resort and it offers Type 2 AC charging, use it. Overnight charging covers 200+ km. Da Nang has the highest density of resort EV charging in Vietnam.

2. **Avoid AEON Mall on Saturday afternoons** — The 120 kW chargers get queues of 2–3 cars between 2 PM–5 PM on weekends. Go before 11 AM or after 7 PM. Vincom Center is a reliable backup.

3. **Ask your apartment about granny charging** — Da Nang's mild climate means you don't need high-power charging at home. A standard wall outlet (granny charger) adds 100+ km overnight, which covers a full day of city driving.

4. **Hai Van Pass timing matters** — The morning mist (5:30 AM–8 AM) is stunning and keeps battery temperatures cool. Midday sun can increase AC load by 15–20%. If you're range-anxious, do the pass early.

5. **Vietnamese EV Facebook groups are gold** — Join "Hội xe điện Đà Nẵng" (Da Nang EV Club) on Facebook for real-time charger status, new installation announcements, and local tips not found on any app. The community is active and helpful.

## Final Verdict

Da Nang in mid-2026 is arguably Vietnam's most **enjoyable** city to drive an electric car. Not the most advanced — that's still Hanoi and HCMC — but the most pleasant. The combination of:

- Wide, well-maintained boulevards with easy parking
- High density of resort charging on the beach strip
- Short distances that make even budget EVs practical
- Excellent gateway location for Hai Van Pass, Hoi An, and Ba Na Hills road trips
- Growing apartment charging in beachfront condos

...makes Da Nang the city where range anxiety fades fastest. For most visitors and many residents, a single weekly charge at a mall or an overnight top-up at a resort covers 100% of driving needs.

**Da Nang's three best charging locations cover 90% of needs:**

- **Beach resort guests** → AEON Mall Da Nang (V-Green, 6x 120 kW) or resort AC charging
- **City centre errands** → Lotte Mart Da Nang (V-Green, 60 kW)
- **Hai Van Pass departure** → Vincom Center Da Nang (V-Green, 6x 60 kW) — charge full, then go

Da Nang is proof that EV-friendliness isn't just about the number of chargers. It's about city design, driving culture, and hospitality infrastructure. And in all three, Da Nang is Vietnam's star performer.
