# Top 10 EV-Friendly Hotels in Bali: Best Family Stays with EV Charging 2026

**Last updated: May 24, 2026**

Bali has embraced electric vehicles faster than any other Indonesian island. With PLN's SPKLU (Stasiun Pengisian Kendaraan Listrik Umum) public charging network expanding from 50+ stations in 2025 to over 120 in 2026, plus private networks managed by ION (Indonesia On) and hotel-backed destination chargers, EV-driving families can now explore the Island of the Gods with confidence. The island's compact size — just 153 km from Gilimanuk in the west to Padangbai in the east — means a single charge covers most day trips, making destination charging at hotels the critical factor.

We've ranked Bali's top 10 EV-friendly hotels based on charger availability, speed, reliability, location, and overall family experience. Whether you're surfing Canggu's waves, meditating in Ubud's rice terraces, or snorkelling Nusa Dua's coral reefs, these hotels will keep you charged and comfortable.

## 1. The Mulia — Nusa Dua

**EV Rating: 9.6/10** | ⚡ **Chargers:** 2× DC 50 kW (PLN SPKLU) + 4× AC 22 kW (ION) | **Cost:** Free for guests

Nusa Dua's premier luxury resort boasts the most comprehensive EV charging infrastructure in Bali. The Mulia's dedicated EV charging pavilion features both DC fast chargers and AC units, all covered and monitored by 24/7 security. - **Charging experience:** Valet charging is included — drop your keys at the main entrance and the team handles everything. The DC chargers deliver 50 kW, adding 250 km of range in just 30 minutes. The hotel rotates vehicles overnight so every EV guest wakes up with a full battery.

- **Family highlights:** 5 dedicated pools including a kids' aqua park, Mulia Kidz Club with Balinese craft workshops, 3-acre lagoon pool, private beach with non-motorised water sports

## EV Charging in Bali: What You Need to Know

### Charging Networks

| Network | Coverage | Connector | App Required | Payment |
|---------|----------|-----------|:------------:|---------|
| PLN SPKLU | Nationwide (120+ stations nationwide, 30+ in Bali) | CCS2, CHAdeMO, Type 2 | Yes (PLN Mobile) | Credit card, QRIS |
| ION (Indonesia On) | Bali (15+ stations) | CCS2, Type 2 | Yes (ION App) | Credit card, QRIS |
| Hotel destination chargers | Growing across major resorts | Type 2 (mostly) | Varies (usually free for guests) | Complimentary |

### Connector Standards

- **Indonesia uses CCS2 and Type 2** — aligned with ASEAN's unified standard
- PLN's SPKLU stations offer CCS2 (fast) and a few CHAdeMO (older Nissan Leaf) units
- Tesla owners need a CCS2-to-NACS adapter (available at most ION stations)
- Bring your own Type 2 cable for hotel AC chargers — many hotels provide them, but it's better to have your own

### Apps to Download Before Your Trip

1. **PLN Mobile** — required for all PLN SPKLU stations
2. **ION App** — for the ION charging network around Bali
3. **PlugShare** — community-checked station reliability across the island
4. **Google Maps** — download offline maps of Bali for areas with patchy signal
5. **Gojek** — not charging-related, but essential for ride-hailing and food delivery in Bali

### Where to Find Public Chargers in Bali

- **Ngurah Rai Airport** (arrivals carpark): PLN SPKLU 50 kW DC × 2
- **Beachwalk Shopping Centre, Kuta**: ION 50 kW DC × 4
- **Plaza Renon, Denpasar**: PLN SPKLU 50 kW DC × 4
- **Galeria Mall, Bali Collection** (Nusa Dua): ION 22 kW AC × 2
- **Seminyak Village**: PLN SPKLU 50 kW DC × 2

- **Lippo Mall Kuta**: ION 50 kW DC × 2

## Tips for Booking EV-Friendly Hotels in Bali

- **Bali is small enough for single-charge day trips.** Most locations on the island are within 80 km of each other — a typical EV with 300+ km range can circle the entire island on one charge.
- **Ubud and Lovina require the most planning.** These areas have fewer public chargers and hillier terrain which reduces range. Arrive with 60%+ battery.
- **The Bukit Peninsula (Uluwatu)** has steep access roads. Regenerative braking helps — the drive down to Padang Padang or Bingin Beach actually recharges your battery.
- **Call ahead** — confirm charger type (DC vs AC), speed, and whether charging is free or paid. Most premium hotels offer complimentary charging.
- **Bring a portable charger** — Indonesian 220V outlets give you about 15 km per hour. Overnight at a homestay = 150 km, enough for most day trips.
- **Check the ferry situation** for Nusa Penida or Lembongan — rental EVs are not permitted on ferries. Park at Padangbai or Sanur harbour and catch a speedboat.
- **Traffic in Seminyak-Canggu-Kuta** can be brutal during peak season (July-August, December-January). A 10 km drive can take 45+ minutes. Plan charging accordingly.
