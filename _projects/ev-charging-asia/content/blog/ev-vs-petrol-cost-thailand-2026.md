# Cost Analysis: EV vs Petrol Car for Family Travel in Thailand 2026

Thailand is Southeast Asia's most popular road trip destination for families. But which powertrain — electric or petrol — actually saves you money on a real 3,000 km family journey? We ran the full comparison for a family of four driving from Bangkok to Phuket, Chiang Mai, and back.

## Key Takeaways
- EV saves approximately 60% on this trip — saving ~฿11,484 ($320 USD) on a 2-week family holiday
- EV charging costs ฿0.69/km vs ฿2.92/km for petrol — about 28% the cost per km
- The 3,000 km loop required 4.3 hours of charging time vs 30 minutes of petrol stops
- Thailand's EV subsidy extends through 2027 under the "EV 3.5" package — up to ฿150,000 off
- Bangkok-Phuket and Bangkok-Chiang Mai routes are Thailand's best-covered EV corridors

## Thai Government EV Incentives (Still Active in 2026)

| Incentive | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Purchase subsidy | Up to ฿150,000 for EVs under ฿2M |
| Excise tax cut | Reduced from 8% to 2% |
| Road tax reduction | ฿600-1,200/year vs ฿3,000-8,500 for petrol |
| EV parking | Free at government lots and most PTT stations |
| HOV lane access | EVs get HOV lane access in Bangkok |

**Note:** EV subsidy extends through 2027 under "EV 3.5" package. Buyers who order before June 2026 still get the full ฿150,000 subsidy.

## Best EV Routes

### Bangkok → Phuket (850 km)
Thailand's best-covered EV route. PTT Station has DC fast chargers (120-150 kW) every 80-120 km along Highway 4. Key stops: PTT Phetchaburi (km 170), PTT Prachuap Khiri Khan (km 310), EA Anywhere Chumphon (km 460).

### Bangkok → Chiang Mai (700 km)
Route via Highway 1: PTT Station Nakhon Sawan (km 240), PTT Station Lampang (km 520), Central Festival Chiang Mai (50 stalls).

## When Does Petrol Still Win?

1. **Remote routes** with no fast chargers (deep Isaan, Mae Hong Son loop)
2. **Rental car flexibility** — EV rentals are 20-30% pricier than petrol and limited
3. **Tight schedules** — 4.3 hours of charging vs 30 minutes of petrol stops over 14 days

## Annual Savings Summary

If you do this trip + daily city driving (15,000 km/year total):
