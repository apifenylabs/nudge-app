### 3. **Content Strategy - The Hybrid Model**

#### **A. Directory Pages (Fact-Based)**
- **Format:** Clean cards with essential info
- **SEO Focus:** Location + activity keywords
- **Examples:** "Tokyo Disneyland", "Ueno Zoo Tokyo", "KidZania Tokyo"

#### **B. Guide Pages (Storytelling)**
- **Format:** Mini-blog style with personal narrative
- **SEO Focus:** "How to", "Tips", "Guide" keywords
- **Examples:** "How to Save Money at Tokyo Disney", "Tokyo with Toddlers Guide"

#### **C. Comparison Pages (Decision Help)**
- **Format:** Tables, pros/cons, recommendations
- **SEO Focus:** "vs", "comparison", "which is better"
- **Examples:** "Disneyland vs DisneySea Tokyo", "Best Tokyo Hotels for Families"

#### **D. Tips Pages (Practical Value)**
- **Format:** Actionable advice, lists, checklists
- **SEO Focus:** "Tips", "tricks", "secrets", "hacks"
- **Examples:** "27 Tokyo Disney Tips 2026", "Hidden Gems in Tokyo"
