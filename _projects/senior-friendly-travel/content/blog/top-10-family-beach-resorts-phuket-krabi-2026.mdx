# Top 10 Family Beach Resorts in Phuket & Krabi 2026

Choosing between Phuket and Krabi for a family beach holiday is a wonderful dilemma. Both offer stunning beaches, warm Andaman Sea waters, and plenty of family-friendly resorts — but they have very different vibes.

Phuket: Big, developed, huge variety of resorts, nightlife, direct flight connections. Krabi: Quieter, more laid back, epic limestone karst scenery, fewer crowds.

We've put together the top 10 family beach resorts across both destinations, comparing room types, kids clubs, beach quality, and prices so you can pick the perfect fit.

---

## Phuket Family Beach Resorts

### 1. Club Med Phuket — Best All-Inclusive for Families

**Price:** $250–400/night (all-inclusive)

The gold standard for all-inclusive family holidays in Thailand. Everything is included — meals, drinks, activities, and the legendary kids' club.

**Room Types:** Family rooms (sleep 4), connecting rooms available
**Kids Club:** Petit Club (2–3), Mini Club (4–10), Passworld (11–17). Runs 9 AM to 9 PM.
**Beach Quality:** Good — Kata Beach is clean with gentle waves. Sunbeds available.
**Pools:** Freeform pool with kids' section. Separate activities pool for aqua aerobics.

**Why families love it:** Genuine time off. Kids eat with their club groups, leaving parents to enjoy adult-only dining and spaces.

### 2. Sunwing Resort Kamala — Purpose-Built for Toddlers

**Price:** $120–250/night

Sunwing is designed exclusively for families with young children. The entire resort is kid-focused from the ground up.

**Room Types:** Family suites with bunk beds, kitchenettes. Sleeps up to 5.
**Kids Club:** Sunwing Kids Club (1–12 years). Water play zone, daily activities, shows.
**Beach Quality:** Excellent — Kamala Beach is one of Phuket's best family beaches. Calm, shallow, clean.
**Pools:** Kids' water park with pirate ship, small slides, splash pads. Dedicated toddler pool.

**Why families love it:** The water park alone keeps kids happy for days. Safe, gated resort with pool-facing rooms.

### 3. Holiday Inn Resort Phuket Patong — Best Value with Waterslides

**Price:** $100–200/night

Don't let "Patong" scare you — this Holiday Inn is a resort bubble with massive lagoon pools and waterslides, minutes from Patong Beach.

**Room Types:** Family rooms (sleep 4), connecting rooms available
**Kids Club:** Planet Trekkers (4–12). Video games, arts, outdoor activities. 8 AM to 6 PM.
**Beach Quality:** Good — Patong Beach is busy but well-maintained. 5-min walk.
**Pools:** Massive lagoon pool complex with waterslides, splash pads, kids' pool.

**Why families love it:** Best value on the list. Kids Stay & Eat Free program saves a fortune.

### 4. JW Marriott Phuket Resort & Spa — Large Resort, Turtle Releases

**Price:** $150–350/night (breakfast included)

A massive resort on Mai Khao Beach with a unique turtle release program that kids absolutely love.

**Room Types:** Deluxe rooms (sleep 3), one-bedroom suites (sleep 4)
**Kids Club:** Kids Club (4–12). Thai cooking, Batik painting, beach games. 9 AM to 5 PM.
**Beach Quality:** Excellent — Mai Khao is long, quiet, and natural. Part of Sirinat National Park.
**Pools:** Multiple pools including family pool, adult-only pool, and kids' splash area.

**Why families love it:** The turtle release program is unforgettable. Huge grounds feel like a jungle resort.

### 5. Centara Grand Beach Resort Phuket (Karon) — Direct Beach Access

**Price:** $130–280/night

Directly on Karon Beach with multiple pools and dining options. A solid, reliable family choice.

**Room Types:** Family suites (sleep 4), connecting rooms, club rooms with lounge access
**Kids Club:** Kids Club (4–12) and Teens Club (13–17). Variety of daily activities.
**Beach Quality:** Excellent — Karon Beach is wide, clean, and less crowded than Patong.
**Pools:** Multiple pools including kids' pool with water features.

**Why families love it:** Direct beach access and plenty of dining variety without leaving the resort.

## Krabi Family Beach Resorts

### 6. Centara Grand Beach Resort & Villas Krabi — Best Krabi Family Resort

**Price:** $150–350/night

The standout family resort in Krabi. Located on the stunning Railay Beach (accessible only by boat), it feels like a private island paradise.

**Room Types:** Family suites, one-bedroom villas with private pools, connecting rooms
**Kids Club:** Kids Club (4–12) with rock climbing wall, cooking classes
**Beach Quality:** Exceptional — Railay Beach is world-famous with limestone cliffs, clear water, soft sand.
**Pools:** Free-form lagoon pool, kids' pool with water features, adults-only pool

**Why families love it:** The unique Railay location (only by boat) feels exclusive. Kids can rock climb, kayak, and explore caves.

### 7. Avani Ao Nang Cliff Krabi — Great Views, Good Value

**Price:** $90–200/night

Modern resort perched on a cliff overlooking Ao Nang. Excellent value for families wanting Krabi's main beach town.

**Room Types:** Family rooms (sleep 4), suites with sea views
**Kids Club:** AvaniKids Club (4–12). Daily programs, indoor playroom, outdoor playground.
**Beach Quality:** Good — Ao Nang Beach is 10-min walk. Slightly busy but lots of dining options nearby.
**Pools:** Infinity pool overlooking the bay. Separate kids' pool.

**Why families love it:** Modern, clean, great value. Walking distance to Ao Nang's restaurants and longtail boat pier.

### 8. Holiday Inn Resort Krabi Ao Nang Beach — Family-Friendly Chain Reliability

**Price:** $90–180/night

The Holiday Inn standard of family-friendliness you can count on. Located right on Ao Nang Beach.

**Room Types:** Family rooms (sleep 4), connecting rooms, suites
**Kids Club:** Planet Trekkers (4–12). Same program as Phuket — reliable and engaging.
**Beach Quality:** Good — Ao Nang Beach with sunset views. Beachfront location.
**Pools:** Lagoon pool with kids' section. Poolside restaurant.

**Why families love it:** Affordable, reliable, great location. Kids Stay & Eat Free.

### 9. Phulay Beach Resort — Luxury with Kids' Activities

**Price:** $200–450/night

A luxury resort that still genuinely welcomes families. Stunning architecture and five pools.

**Room Types:** Deluxe rooms (sleep 3), one-bedroom suites (sleep 4), villas
**Kids Club:** Kids Club with indoor playroom, outdoor activities. Cooking classes.
**Beach Quality:** Good — private beach area on Nammao Bay. Clean and quiet.
**Pools:** Five pools including dedicated kids' pool with slide.

**Why families love it:** Luxury without being stuffy about kids. Beautiful grounds, excellent service.

### 10. Dusit Thani Krabi Beach Resort — Classic Family Resort

**Price:** $130–280/night

Long-established resort on a beautiful stretch of Khao Thong Beach. Spacious rooms and mature gardens.

**Room Types:** Family rooms (sleep 4), suites, villas with private pools
**Kids Club:** Dusit Kids Club (4–12). Thai crafts, outdoor games, movie screenings.
**Beach Quality:** Very good — quiet stretch of beach with stunning sunset views.
**Pools:** Large free-form pool, kids' pool, adults-only pool.

**Why families love it:** Spacious rooms, quiet beach, reasonable prices. Mature gardens are beautiful for evening strolls.

## Phuket vs Krabi: Which Is Better for Your Family?

| Factor | Phuket | Krabi |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Resort variety | Huge — dozens of family options | Smaller but quality-focused |
| Beach quality | Varies — some excellent, some crowded | Generally excellent, quieter |
| Family activities | Water parks, elephant sanctuaries, shows | Rock climbing, island hopping, hot springs |
| Restaurants | Endless options, international cuisine | Fewer but good quality, local focus |
| Nightlife | Patong is wild (avoid with kids) | Mellow, family-friendly |
| Getting there | Direct flights from most Asian cities | Fly to Krabi Airport or Phuket + transfer |
| Budget | Mid to high | Low to mid |

## Family Tips for Phuket & Krabi

- **Avoid Patong with young kids** — It's loud, crowded, and not family-friendly at night
- **Choose a resort with a kids club** — Even a few hours of supervised care transforms your trip
- **Check the monsoon timing** — Best months: November to April (high season, higher prices)
- **Book a resort with breakfast included** — Thailand resort breakfasts are legendary and save $20–40/day
- **Pack reef-safe sunscreen** — Many Thai marine parks ban harmful sunscreens

## The Verdict

For Phuket, **Sunwing Kamala** is unbeatable for families with toddlers, while **Club Med** is the best all-inclusive for any age. In Krabi, **Centara Grand Railay** offers a once-in-a-lifetime family experience on a stunning beach accessible only by boat. If you're on a tighter budget, **Holiday Inn Krabi Ao Nang** or **Avani Ao Nang Cliff** deliver excellent value.
