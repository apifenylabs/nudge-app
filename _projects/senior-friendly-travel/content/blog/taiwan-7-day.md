# Taiwan with Kids: Complete 7-Day Itinerary (2026)

Taiwan is one of Asia's most underrated family destinations. Efficient high-speed rail, incredible night markets, stunning nature, and exceptionally friendly locals — all at a fraction of the cost of Japan or Singapore. This 7-day itinerary covers the highlights without rushing.

## Key Takeaways
- Taiwan offers Japanese-style efficiency at half the price — a family of 4 can do 7 days for $800–1,500 total
- Taipei is incredibly stroller-friendly with wide MRT stations and elevators everywhere
- Taroko Gorge (Day 4) is a must — kids under 12 enter for free
- Night market food is safe, cheap, and toddler-friendly — bubble tea at $1.50, fried chicken, mango shaved ice
- Book high-speed rail 4 weeks ahead for 20% discount

## Day 1: Arrive in Taipei

### Afternoon — Check In + Explore Ximending

Ximending is Taipei's youth district — think Shibuya but more relaxed. Pedestrian-only streets, street performers, quirky shops, and endless food options. Kids love the giant LED screens and the general buzz.

### Evening — Dinner at Ximending Night Market

**Must-try with kids:**
- Grilled squid (chewy, mild, kids love the texture)
- Taiwanese sausage wrapped in sticky rice
- Bubble tea from a street stall ($1.50 USD)
- Mango shaved ice (bingsoo-style — a guaranteed hit)

### Where to Stay

Best family areas: **Ximending** (central, fun, great food) or **Daan** (quieter, parks, family-friendly). Expect $60–150/night.

## Day 2: Taipei Must-Sees

### Morning — Taipei Zoo + Maokong Gondola

One of Asia's best zoos. Lush, well-maintained, and incredibly affordable at just $2 USD entry. Highlights include the Giant Panda House (Tuan Tuan and Yuan Yuan), koala exhibit, Formosan Animal Area (Taiwan's native species), and Children's Zoo with petting area.

From the zoo, take the Maokong Gondola up the mountain. [Book Maokong Gondola tickets on Klook](https://www.klook.com/search/?keyword=Maokong+Gondola&aid=38VWJMX) and request a "crystal cabin" with glass bottom for an extra thrill. At the top: tea houses with stunning city views.

### Evening — Shilin Night Market

Taipei's largest and most famous night market. **Kid-friendly eats:** hot star fried chicken (huge, crispy, addicting), oyster omelette, pepper pork buns, and fried milk (sweet, crispy outside, custard inside).

## Day 3: Jiufen + North Coast

### Day Trip to Jiufen (40 min from Taipei)

Jiufen is the mountain town that inspired Spirited Away (Studio Ghibli). Narrow alleys, red lanterns, and hillside teahouses create a magical atmosphere. Must-do: walk Old Street, try taro balls (sweet chewy dessert), visit Shengping Theatre, and take photos at the viewing platform.

### Afternoon — Yehliu Geopark

On the way back, stop at Yehliu Geopark. Incredible rock formations carved by the sea — the "Queen's Head" is the most famous. Good for 1–2 hours. Wear good shoes as the rocks can be uneven.

## Day 4: Taroko Gorge

### Take the Train to Hualien

High-speed train to Hualien (2 hours from Taipei). Budget-friendly and scenic.

### Taroko Gorge Highlights

Taroko is Taiwan's most famous natural wonder — a marble-walled canyon with turquoise rivers.

**Kid-friendly trails:**
- Shakadang Trail (flat, 1.5 km, follows the river, great for swimming in summer)
- Baiyang Waterfall Trail (waterfall at the end, fun tunnels to walk through)
- Xiangde Temple Trail (short walk to a temple with great views)

**Pro tip:** Kids under 12 get free entry to Taroko National Park. [Book Taroko Gorge tours on Klook](https://www.klook.com/search/?keyword=Taroko+Gorge+tour&aid=38VWJMX).

### Evening — Stay at Hualien

Hualien is a relaxed coastal city. Dongdamen Night Market has excellent food and a children's play area.

## Day 5: Sun Moon Lake

### Travel to Sun Moon Lake

Take the train from Hualien to Taichung (3 hours), then bus to Sun Moon Lake (1.5 hours).

### Activities

- **Bike around the lake** — one of the world's most scenic bike paths. Flat and paved. Rent bikes with child seats or tandem attachments for a 12 km loop.
- **Cable car ride** — panoramic lake views from above
- **Formosan Aboriginal Culture Village** — theme park + indigenous culture
- **Boat cruise** to Ita Thao village for traditional Aboriginal food

## Day 6: Taichung

### Morning — National Museum of Natural Science

One of Asia's best natural history museums. The dinosaur exhibit is world-class and the Science Center has hands-on exhibits for kids. Budget 2–3 hours.

### Afternoon — Rainbow Village + Carton King

**Rainbow Village:** A veterans' village painted in every color by a 90-year-old former soldier. Free entry, great photo spot, 20 minutes max. **Carton King:** Everything is made of cardboard — furniture, decorations, even a cardboard train kids can ride. Bizarre but brilliant fun.

### Evening — Fengjia Night Market

Taichung's biggest night market and arguably the best in Taiwan for food variety.

## Day 7: Taipei Final Day

### Morning — Taipei 101 + Elephant Mountain

Visit Taipei 101 (quick elevator up and down — the view is spectacular). For active families, hike Elephant Mountain (15 minutes up, 100 steps). Best free view of Taipei 101.

### Afternoon — Dihua Street + Dadaocheng Wharf

Dihua Street is Taipei's oldest street — traditional shops selling dried goods, herbs, and teas. Near Dadaocheng Wharf with a great riverside park and playground.

### Evening — Final Night Market

Hit your favorite night market one last time, or try Ningxia Night Market (smaller, more traditional, excellent seafood).

## Transportation Tips

- **EasyCard** — Get one at any MRT station. Works for metro, buses, bike share, and convenience stores.
- **High-Speed Rail** — Book 4 weeks ahead for 20% discount. Kids under 6 are free (no seat).
- **Taxis** — Cheap and metered. Uber available in major cities.
