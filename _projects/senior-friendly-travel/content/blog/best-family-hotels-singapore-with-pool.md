# Best Family Hotels in Singapore with Pool

A good pool can make or break a family holiday in Singapore. The city is hot (year-round 28-33°C), and after a morning of sightseeing, there's nothing better than letting the kids splash while you relax with a cold drink.

Singapore has some of the best hotel pools in Asia — from massive lagoon pools to dedicated kids' splash zones with water slides. Here are our top picks for families.

---

## 1. Resorts World Sentosa — Hard Rock Hotel

**Pool:** Lagoon-style swimming pool with waterslides, rock grottos, and a dedicated kids' area.  
**Best for:** Families planning to visit Universal Studios or S.E.A. Aquarium.

The Hard Rock Hotel at Resorts World Sentosa has arguably the best family pool on the island. It's huge — more like a water park than a hotel pool. There's a winding lagoon with multiple depths, two water slides (one for younger kids, one for older), and a sandy beach entry area where toddlers can play safely.

**Room tip:** Book a "Family Room" with bunk beds for the kids and a separate king bed for parents. Rooms from SGD 280/night.

**Nearby attractions:** Universal Studios (2 min walk), S.E.A. Aquarium (3 min walk), Adventure Cove Waterpark (5 min walk).

## 2. Shangri-La Singapore

**Pool:** Three-tiered free-form pool with waterfall, spa jets, and a separate kids' pool with water cannons.  
**Best for:** Families who want a resort experience within the city.

The Shangri-La Singapore's pool area is legendary among Asian families. The main pool has a waterfall feature and spa jets for adults, while the dedicated kids' pool has water cannons, a mini slide, and shallow water (0.3m depth). The surrounding tropical gardens give it a resort feel despite being in the middle of Orchard Road.

**The kids' club (Buds)** is a standout — free for hotel guests, with a dedicated indoor playground, cooking classes, and supervised activities from 9am-6pm.

**Room tip:** Tower Wing rooms are the newest (renovated 2022). Horizon Club rooms include breakfast and afternoon tea. From SGD 350/night.

**Pro tip:** Book a Klook pass for the Singapore Zoo or Night Safari in advance — the hotel is a 15-minute drive away.

## 3. Marina Bay Sands

**Pool:** Iconic infinity pool on the 57th floor — **adults only** (no kids allowed). But the hotel has a separate **3rd-floor family pool** with cabanas, a wading pool, and splash zone.  
**Best for:** Families who want the iconic skyline view and don't mind the pool restriction.

Yes, the famous infinity pool is adult-only. But the family pool on level 3 is actually excellent — it has a large wading pool for toddlers, cushioned cabanas, and direct access to the Raffles Boulevard restaurants.

**Reality check:** The family pool is great, but if pool time is your top priority, skip Marina Bay Sands and go for Sentosa or Shangri-La. You're here for the room, the view, and the casino proximity — not the family pool.

**Room tip:** Premier Garden View rooms are the best value. Family of 4 can share one Premier room (has a sofabed). From SGD 450/night.

## 4. Village Hotel Sentosa

**Pool:** Singapore's largest resort-style pool at 137m long, with a dedicated children's pool and water slides.  
**Best for:** Budget-conscious families who still want great pool amenities.

Don't let the word "Village" fool you — this is a full-service resort with an incredible pool complex. The 137m pool snakes through the property with a bridge over it, a jacuzzi area, and a 1m shallow kids' zone. There are also two water slides that kids aged 5+ love.

**Value proposition:** At SGD 200-260/night, this is significantly cheaper than the Hard Rock or Shangri-La while still offering excellent pool facilities.

**Room tip:** Family Deluxe rooms sleep 4 and have a balcony. Ask for a pool-facing room.

[Search Village Hotel Sentosa on Booking.com →](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?aid=2875669&ss=Village+Hotel+Sentosa+by+Far+East+Hospitality)

## 5. Pan Pacific Singapore

**Pool:** Crescent-shaped outdoor pool with city views, plus a toddler wading pool.  
**Best for:** Families who want Marina Bay proximity without the Marina Bay Sands price.

The Pan Pacific's pool isn't the biggest (35m lap pool + 0.5m children's pool), but it has spectacular views of Marina Bay and the Singapore skyline. The poolside service is excellent — they bring fruit skewers and cold towels to loungers.

**Location:** Connected to Suntec City Convention Centre and within walking distance of Gardens by the Bay. The MRT is a 5-minute walk.

**Room tip:** Harbour View rooms overlook the bay. Family rooms with connecting doors available. From SGD 280/night.

## What to Pack for Pool Days in Singapore

- **Swim nappies** — Available at Cold Storage but expensive, bring from home
- **UV swimsuits** — The Singapore sun is intense even at 4pm
- **Water shoes** — Pool decks get hot
- **Floats / arm bands** — Singapore hotels don't always provide these
- **Dry bag** — For wet swimwear when you check out

**Travel tip:** Most Singapore hotels have pool towels at the pool deck, so you don't need to pack towels. They also provide kids' bathrobes and slippers at mid-range and luxury hotels.
