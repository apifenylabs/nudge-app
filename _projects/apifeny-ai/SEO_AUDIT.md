## 3. Technical SEO Fixes Needed

### Critical
- [ ] **Blog posts need H1 title tags** — JSON-based posts may not render schema-valid H1s for each page
- [ ] **Verify each blog page has unique meta description** — JSON content may fall through to default
- [ ] **Internal linking between playbooks ↔ blog ↔ tools** is weak — add "Related" sections everywhere

### Important
- [ ] **Add `alt` text to all images** — SVG illustrations on homepage lack alt attributes
- [ ] **Enable breadcrumbs** with schema.org BreadcrumbList on playbook + tool pages
- [ ] **Add FAQ schema** on playbook pages for featured snippets

### Nice-to-Have
- [ ] **Blog post reading time** — verify calculation is visible in SERP
- [ ] **Add `lastmod` dates** to sitemap entries for blog posts
- [ ] **Create category/tag pages** for blog with their own meta + pagination
