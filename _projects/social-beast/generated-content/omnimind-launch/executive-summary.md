# OmniMind Distribution — Executive Summary

## The Core Problem
We have a product but zero audience. Most launch advice assumes you have warm followers to activate. We don't. So the strategy is **build audience → then launch**, not the reverse.

## The Strategy in 4 Phases

| Phase | Timeline | Goal | Key Actions |
|-------|----------|------|-------------|
| **0: Foundation** | Week 1 | Create conditions for distribution to work | SEO blog post, dev.to/Medium, Twitter account, r/selfhosted presence, OpenClaw plugin directory |
| **1: Community Launch** | Week 2 | Get real users and social proof | 4 Reddit posts staggered over 10 days (selfhosted → openclaw → AI_Agents → SideProject) |
| **2: Social Amplification** | Week 2-3 | Convert Reddit traffic into following | Twitter launch thread, LinkedIn cross-post, targeted community replies |
| **3: Product Hunt** | Week 3-4 | Cap the campaign with a spike | Launch PH with warm audience, 10 prepped comments, post-launch blog |
| **4: SEO Compounding** | Week 4+ | Build lasting traffic | Comparison content ("Mem0 alternative"), direct search terms, long-tail |

## Why Not PH First
PH with zero existing audience = wasted. By week 3 we'll have Reddit traction, GitHub stars, and real installs to reference. PH converts better with social proof.

## Key Tactical Decisions
- **r/selfhosted first** (not r/openclaw) — bigger sub, stronger self-hosting ethos, less AI saturation
- **Comparison content** — "Supermemory alternative" SEO captures competitor searchers
- **Staggered Reddit posts** — same product, different angles per sub, 3 days apart
- **No ads** — $0 budget, all organic
- **Tradeoffs honest** — builds credibility, Reddit respects it
- **Install in 1 command** — README leads with `openclaw plugins install`, not docs

## Go/No-Go Gates
Phase 0→1: Blog post published, accounts set up, README polished
Phase 1→2: 2 Reddit posts with 50+ upvotes combined (otherwise rethink)
Phase 2→3: 15+ GitHub stars, 5+ real installs
Phase 3→4: PH top 10 OR 50+ stars OR 10+ installs
