1/ 
Your AI agents have a 20-minute memory.
Here's how I fixed that with 3 databases and a cron job that runs every night 🧵

2/ 
The problem: every time I started a new OpenClaw session, my agent forgot everything from yesterday. 
Not "vaguely forgot" — completely blank.
Context windows don't save across sessions. They're not supposed to.

3/ 
Most "solutions" are cloud APIs.
Send your agent's conversations to some server → they vectorize it → you pay $30-50/month.
Your private conversations. On someone else's machine. Monthly.

4/ 
I wanted:
- 100% local
- No monthly bill
- Works offline
- My agent actually learns over time, not just stores raw text

So I built OmniMind.

5/ 
Three embedded databases, each for one job:
• LanceDB → semantic search ("what did we say about X?")
• Kuzu → relationship graphs ("how do these concepts connect?")
• SQLite → audit trail ("when was this stored and who accessed it?")

No servers. No API calls. Just a plugin.

6/ 
The part I'm most proud of: the Memify worker.
Every night it scans raw conversation logs, finds patterns, and compresses them into higher-level knowledge. 
Your agent gets smarter while you sleep. No input needed.

7/ 
One interesting learn: I started with Qdrant (great tool). Switched to LanceDB because embedding it inside a Node.js plugin was seamless. Zero infrastructure.
Kuzu was the right call for graph — embedded, columnar, no server.

8/ 
Current state:
✅ 24/24 tests passing

✅ v0.3.0 tagged and released

9/
