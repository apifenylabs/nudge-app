## Monetization Strategy
1. **Commission Rates:** 4-20% depending on product category
2. **Recurring Commissions:** Monthly subscriptions (30-50% lifetime)
3. **High-Ticket Items:** Big commissions on expensive products/services
4. **Local Services:** Commission on booked appointments
5. **Digital Products:** 50-75% commissions on courses, ebooks

## Value-First Approach
1. **Authentic Recommendations:** Only promote products we'd use ourselves
2. **Transparent Disclosure:** Clear affiliate disclosure on all content
3. **Helpful Content:** Solve problems first, monetize second
4. **Quality Over Quantity:** Fewer, better-converting links
5. **User Trust:** Never sacrifice trust for short-term commissions
