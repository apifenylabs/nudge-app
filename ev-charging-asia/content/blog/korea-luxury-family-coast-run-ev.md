# Korea Luxury Family Coast Run — Seoul → Gangneung → Sokcho (5-Day EV Road Trip)

Korea's east coast is a spectacular playground for luxury-loving families. The 450 km loop from Seoul to Gangneung and Sokcho along the East Sea offers dramatic coastal views, world-class hot springs resorts, premium ski resorts, and some of Asia's best family attractions — all connected by Korea's superb charging infrastructure.

This 5-day itinerary combines Korea's most exclusive accommodations with unforgettable family experiences: feeding seals at the aquarium, pedalling a rail bike along the coast, exploring Seoraksan National Park, and soaking in ocean-view hot springs. Best of all, Korea's highway rest stops offer free DC fast charging, making this one of Asia's most cost-effective luxury EV road trips.

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Seoul → Gangneung (250 km)

**Distance:** 250 km
**Driving time:** 2.5-3 hours
**Charging stops:** Yeoju Rest Stop (free DC fast charger), Gangneung City Hall (free DC charger)

**Morning — Depart Seoul (9 AM)**
Start your journey from Seoul with a full charge. Most Seoul hotels and apartments have overnight AC charging (5-7kW). If you need a top-up, the **Lotte World Mall** in Jamsil has 200kW DC chargers in its underground parking (free with mall purchase of KRW 30k+).

Take the **Gyeongbu Expressway (Route 1)** east towards Gangneung. This is one of Korea's most scenic highways, cutting through the Taebaek Mountains.

**Charging Stop:** **Yeoju Rest Stop (여주휴게소)** — approximately 100 km from Seoul. Every Korean highway rest stop has DC fast chargers (50-200kW, CCS1). This one has 4 stalls at 100kW. Charging is **completely free** on Korea's highway network. Use the 15-20 min charge to:
- Visit the rest stop's convenience store for snacks (try the sweet potato lattes and corn dogs)
- Let the kids stretch at the indoor play corner (small climber and slide)
- Take photos of the surrounding mountain views

**Arrive Gangneung (12:30 PM)**
Gangneung is the cultural heart of Korea's east coast — home to coffee culture, surfing beaches, and the famous Gangneung Danoje Festival.

**Lunch:** **Terarosa Coffee (테라로사)** — Gangneung's most famous café. The brunch menu (avocado toast, eggs benedict, house-made pastries) is superb, and the coffee is legendary. Kids love the chocolate croissants and the garden play area.

**Family Activities (Afternoon):**
- **Gangneung Olympic Park** — venue for the 2018 Winter Olympics. Visit the Pyeongchang Olympic Stadium (free entry), try the curling experience (KRW 5,000/person, age 6+), and walk the Olympic Plaza.
- **Gangmun Beach** — one of Korea's best family beaches. Calm waves, life guards, and a playground on the sand. Surfboard rentals available for older kids (ages 8+).
- **Chamsori Gramophone & Edison Museum** — a quirky and fascinating museum with 3,500+ gramophones and Edison inventions. Kids love the interactive exhibits (crank a 1910s phonograph, watch an 1890s film projector).

**Dinner:** **Gangneung Hyundai Tourist Hotel's buffet dinner** — Korean-Japanese-Western buffet with a dedicated kids' corner (pizza, chicken nuggets, fruit, ice cream machine). Book the 6:30 PM seating for sunset views.

**Luxury Accommodation:** [Seamarq Hotel Gangneung](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Seamarq+Hotel+Gangneung) — designed by renowned architect Kim Swoo-geun, this iconic concrete-and-glass hotel sits right on the beach. Ocean-view suites, indoor pool, kids' playroom, and 2 AC EV chargers (7kW) in the parking lot. The sunrise from the rooftop infinity pool is unforgettable.

**Notes:** Gangneung's famous coffee street (Anmok Beach) has dozens of ocean-view cafes. If the kids still have energy after dinner, walk to **Bossa Nova Coffee** for a hot chocolate and live jazz.

### Day 2: Gangneung (Full Day) — Coffee, Coast & Culture

**Distance:** 30 km (local driving)
**Driving time:** 1 hour total
**Charging:** Overnight charge at Seamarq Hotel covers the day

**Morning — Gangneung Cultural Tour (9 AM-12 PM)**
- **Ojukheon House (오죽헌)** — UNESCO World Heritage site, birthplace of Korea's most famous scholar Yulgok. The bamboo grove and traditional hanok architecture are beautiful. Kids can try wearing hanbok (traditional Korean clothes) for free in the visitor centre.
- **Gyeongpodae Pavilion** — a stunning 14th-century pavilion overlooking Gyeongpo Lake and the East Sea. Walk the 4.3 km lakeside trail — flat, stroller-friendly, with wildflowers and bird-watching platforms.
- **Gyeongpo Beach** — shallow waters perfect for toddlers. Rent a pedal boat (KRW 15,000/30 min) for a family paddle on the lake.

**Lunch:** **Sungwoo Jangeo (성우장어)** — Gangneung's best grilled eel restaurant. Eel is a Korean delicacy and incredibly healthy. The set menu (KRW 35,000/person) includes eel grilled over charcoal, side dishes, and soup. Kids who prefer milder flavours can order the pork belly (samgyeopsal) set.

**Afternoon — Coffee & Coast (1-5 PM)**
- **Anmok Coffee Street (안목해변)** — 1.5 km of coffee shops lining the beach. Let the kids play on the sand while you enjoy world-class coffee. **BONA VITA** has a dedicated kids' corner with toys and colouring books.
- **Gangneung Rail Bike (정동진 레일바이크)** — this is the highlight for kids. A 5.2 km pedal-powered rail cart running along the coast, through tunnels with laser light shows and sound effects. **Two-seater: KRW 30,000, four-seater: KRW 40,000.** Book the 3 PM slot for the best afternoon light.
- **Jeongdongjin Beach** — famous for the "Sun Cruise" resort shaped like a cruise ship. Walk the beach's "time capsule" park where couples leave their wishes.

**Dinner:** **Manseok Dakgalbi (만석닭갈비)** — Gangneung-style spicy stir-fried chicken. Ask for "anjoo" (안주) level spiciness — it's milder and kid-friendly. The cheese-topped version is a hit with children.

**Luxury Accommodation:** Continue at [Seamarq Hotel Gangneung](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Seamarq+Hotel+Gangneung) (same as night 1)

**Notes:** Gangneung Rail Bike tickets sell out on weekends in summer. Book online at least 3 days in advance. The 4-seater bike lets your family pedal together — easier for younger children who get tired.

### Day 3: Gangneung → Sokcho via Seoraksan (80 km)

**Distance:** 80 km
**Driving time:** 1-1.5 hours
**Charging stops:** Seoraksan National Park parking lot (free DC charger, 50kW)

**Morning — Drive to Sokcho (9 AM departure)**
The 80 km drive from Gangneung to Sokcho along Route 7 is one of Korea's most beautiful coastal roads. Mountains to your left, the East Sea to your right. Take it slow — there are scenic overlooks every 5-10 km.

**Charging:** No need to charge on this short journey. Your Seamarq overnight charge (7kW x 10 hours = 70km) plus the remaining range from Day 1 gives you plenty for the 80 km drive.

**Late Morning — Seoraksan National Park (설악산국립공원)**
Korea's most famous national park is a 10-minute drive from Sokcho. **Entry fee:** KRW 3,500/adult, kids under 13 free. Start at the park's visitor centre.

**Family-Friendly Hikes in Seoraksan:**
- **Biryong Falls (비룡폭포)** — the easiest and most rewarding family hike. 1.6 km each way, 40 min. Gentle incline, paved path, stroller-friendly for the first 800m. Ends at a stunning 30m waterfall with a pool at the base.
- **Geumganggul Cave (금강굴)** — slightly harder (2 km, steep stairs), but the cave temples are magical for kids. Only for families with older children (ages 7+).
- **Seoraksan Cable Car (설악산 케이블카)** — the easiest way to see the park. A 7-min cable car ride to Gwongeumseong Fortress (800m elevation). **KRW 15,000/round trip, age 3+.** Book online 24h ahead — queues can be 1 hour+ in peak season.

**Lunch:** **Seoraksan Ssambap (설악산쌈밥)** — a restaurant near the park entrance serving ssambap (rice wrapped in leaves with grilled pork and vegetables). The set meal includes 12+ side dishes (banchan). Kids love wrapping their own "mini burritos."

**Early Afternoon — Arrive Sokcho (2 PM)**
Check into your Sokcho hotel. The drive from Seoraksan to Sokcho is only 10 km.

**Family Activities (Afternoon):**
- **Sokcho Lotte Resort Water Park (롯데리조트 워터파크)** — indoor and outdoor pools, water slides, lazy river, and a kids-only splash zone with mini slides (age 3+). **KRW 35,000/adult, KRW 25,000/child.** Open until 8 PM.
- **Sokcho ABABA Kimchi School (아바마마 김치학교)** — a hands-on kimchi-making class for the whole family. 90-min session, English-speaking instructors. Kids learn to make kimchi and get a certificate. **KRW 30,000/person.**

**Dinner:** **Mum's Table (엄마밥상)** — Sokcho's most family-friendly restaurant. Korean home-style cooking served in a warm, casual setting. The haemul pajeon (seafood pancake) and doenjang jjigae (soybean stew) are legendary. Budget: KRW 40,000 for a family of 4.

**Luxury Accommodation:** [Sokcho Lotte Resort](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Sokcho+Lotte+Resort) — 5-star resort with direct access to the water park, indoor pool, kids' club with Korean craft workshops, and 4 AC EV chargers (7kW) in the underground parking. Ocean-view family suites sleep 4 comfortably. The resort also offers overnight EV valet charging — just hand your key card to the concierge.

**Notes:** Charge your EV at the Lotte Resort's AC chargers overnight. A full 10-hour charge (7kW) gives you 70km+ — all you need for Day 4.

### Day 4: Sokcho Exploration — Aquarium, Rail Bike & Hot Springs

**Distance:** 20 km (local driving)
**Driving time:** 30-40 min total
**Charging:** Overnight charge at Lotte Resort covers the day

**Morning — Sokcho Aquarium (속초아쿠아리움) (9:30 AM-12 PM)**
Korea's largest aquarium outside Seoul, featuring 20,000+ marine creatures. The East Sea tank is a massive 25m underwater tunnel with sharks, rays, and sea turtles. Don't miss:
- **Seal Feeding Show** (10:30 AM and 2 PM) — playful harbour seals perform tricks and take a fish from the trainer's hand
- **Penguin Walk** (11 AM) — Humboldt penguins waddle through the aquarium hall
- **Touch Pool** — kids can touch starfish, sea cucumbers, and small sharks (supervised)
- **"I'm a Mermaid" Photo Zone** — dress-up photo booth with mermaid costumes

**Entry:** KRW 25,000/adult, KRW 18,000/child (under 12). **Book online for 20% off.**

**Lunch:** **Sokcho Jungang Market (속초중앙시장)** — Sokcho's legendary covered market. Try:
- **Sundae (순대)** — Korean blood sausage, surprisingly mild and kid-friendly
- **Eomuk (어묵)** — fish cake skewers in hot broth, KRW 1,500 each
- **Hotteok (호떡)** — sweet Korean pancakes with cinnamon and honey, KRW 1,000
- **Fresh Squid —** grilled on the spot, chewy and delicious

The market has seating areas and is stroller-friendly.

**Early Afternoon — Sokcho Rail Bike (속초레일바이크) (1:30-3 PM)**
A different rail bike from Gangneung's — this one runs 4 km along the coast with spectacular East Sea views. The highlight is a **500m tunnel** that plays K-pop music and projects animations on the walls — kids are mesmerised.

**KRW 25,000/2-seater, KRW 35,000/4-seater.** Book the 1:30 PM slot. Takes about 50 minutes round trip.

**Mid-Afternoon — Cheoksan Hot Springs (3:30-6 PM)**
Sokcho sits on natural hot springs, and **Cheoksan Hot Springs (척산온천)** is the area's best family facility. Outdoor pools with ocean views, indoor hot spring baths (separate gender), and a kids' pool at 35°C — perfect for toddlers.

- **KRW 15,000/adult, KRW 10,000/child**
- **Bring swimsuits and towels** (rental available: KRW 5,000 each)
- **Spicy ramyeon at the snack bar** — a post-bath tradition

**Dinner:** **Manseong Sashimi Restaurant (만성회집)** — Sokcho's best hwe (raw fish) restaurant. For families who prefer cooked options, they offer grilled fish, crab stew, and bibimbap. The set menu (KRW 60,000/person) includes 5 types of sashimi plus side dishes.

**Luxury Accommodation:** Continue at [Sokcho Lotte Resort](https://www.booking.com/search.html?ss=Sokcho+Lotte+Resort) (same as night 3)

**Notes:** The Aquarium and Rail Bike are both within 5 km of the Lotte Resort. You won't need to drive all day. The hot springs are a 10-minute drive from the hotel. Charge your EV to 80%+ overnight — you'll need it for the return to Seoul tomorrow.

### Day 5: Sokcho → Seoul (200 km)

**Distance:** 200 km
**Driving time:** 2.5-3 hours
**Charging stops:** Hongcheon Rest Stop (free DC 100kW), Seoul hotel overnight

**Morning — Final Sokcho Morning (8-10 AM)**
- **Yeonggeumjeong Pavilion (영금정)** — a sunrise viewpoint on the rocky Sokcho coast. If you can manage a 6:30 AM departure, the sunrise over the East Sea is spectacular. Kids can watch for seals on the rocks below.
- **Sokcho Lighthouse (속초등대)** — a short 15-min climb to the 51m lighthouse. Free entry, panoramic views of the city and sea.
- **Abai Village (아바이마을)** — a unique North Korean defector settlement founded during the Korean War. Try the Abai-style cold noodles (물냉면) at one of the village's documentary-featured restaurants.

**Late Morning — Return to Seoul (10:30 AM departure)**
Depart Sokcho via the Seoul-Yangyang Expressway. This is a newer highway (opened 2017) with beautiful mountain views.

**Charging Stop:** **Hongcheon Rest Stop (홍천휴게소)** — approximately 100 km from Sokcho, about 1.5 hours into the drive. Every Korean rest stop has free DC chargers. The Hongcheon rest stop has 100kW CCS1 chargers with 4 stalls. Perfect 15-minute stop for:
- Hotteok and coffee
- Leg stretch at the rest stop's walking path
- Views of Hongcheon's river valley

**Arrive Seoul (1:30 PM)**
Enter Seoul via the Gangbyeon Expressway. Avoid entering between 5-7 PM when the Olympic Expressway is gridlocked.

**Lunch in Seoul:** **Maple Tree House (메이플트리하우스)** in Itaewon — premium Korean BBQ with an English menu and kid-friendly service. The galbi jjim (braised short rib, deboned) is perfect for children. Budget: KRW 80,000 for a family of 4.

**Final Charging:** Park at your Seoul accommodation. Most luxury hotels in Gangnam and Jongno have AC charging (5-7kW). The **Four Seasons Hotel Seoul** has 6 AC chargers and 2 DC fast chargers in its parking garage.

## Seasonal Recommendations

### Spring (March-May) — BEST TIME for Cherry Blossoms
- **Weather:** 10-20°C, mild and sunny. Perfect for outdoor activities.
- **Gangneung Cherry Blossoms:** Gyeongpo Lake is surrounded by cherry trees — the Gyeongpo Cherry Blossom Festival runs early April.
- **Seoraksan:** Spring flowers bloom from mid-April. Water levels at Biryong Falls are at their best.
- **Range impact:** Ideal — no A/C or heating needed. Real-world range matches WLTP.
- **What's open:** All attractions, all trails. Cable car and rail bikes run full schedule.
- **Crowds:** Moderate. Book Seoraksan cable car 3+ days ahead.

### Summer (June-August) — Beach & Water Park Season
- **Weather:** 22-30°C, humid but cooler than Seoul. Ocean breezes help.
- **Beaches:** Gangmun Beach, Gyeongpo Beach, and Sokcho Beach are in full swing. Umbrellas and lounge chairs available for rent (KRW 15,000/day).
- **Water Parks:** Sokcho Lotte Water Park and Gangneung Ocean City are open 9 AM-8 PM.
- **Range impact:** A/C reduces range by 8-12%. Plan one extra 15-min charging stop.
- **Crowds:** Peak — July-August weekends are packed. Book accommodation 2+ months ahead.

### Autumn (September-November) — Foliage Season
- **Weather:** 8-20°C, crisp air, clear skies. The most beautiful season.
- **Fall Foliage:** Seoraksan's autumn colours peak mid-to-late October. Biryong Falls trail is drenched in red and gold.
- **Gangneung Coffee Festival:** Early October — the city's famous coffee scene hosts tastings, workshops, and live music.
- **Range impact:** Optimal — near WLTP rating.
- **Crowds:** High in Seoraksan (fall foliage tourists). Visit on weekdays.

### Winter (December-February) — Ski & Hot Springs Season
- **Weather:** -5 to 5°C. Snow in the mountains (Seoraksan gets 1-2m).
- **Skiing:** Alpensia Resort (near Gangneung) and Yongpyong Resort (30 min from Gangneung) are world-class. The 2018 Olympic slopes are open. **Family ski lessons:** KRW 100,000/hour (English-speaking instructor).
- **Hot Springs:** Cheoksan Hot Springs is magical in winter — soaking in outdoor pools while snow falls.
- **Range impact:** Heating reduces range by 15-25%. Plan one extra charging stop. The battery may pre-condition while plugged in.
- **Road conditions:** The mountain road to Seoraksan may require snow chains. Check weather before departing Sokcho.
- **What's limited:** Cable car closes in heavy snow. Some rail bike slots may be suspended.
- **Recommendation:** Rent a **Kia EV9 with snow-rated tires** or a **Genesis GV70 Electrified** with AWD for winter confidence.

## Essential Apps & Tips for Korea

### Apps to Download
- **KakaoMap** (카카오맵) — best navigation for Korea. Shows EV charging stations with real-time availability.
- **EV Infra** (전기차 인프라) — Korea's comprehensive EV charging app. Shows all networks (KEPCO, SK Signet, GS Caltex, Hyundai).
- **Naver Map** — alternative navigation with EV routing support.
- **Kakao T** — taxi and rideshare backup.

### Family Pro Tips
1. **Most chargers are free on Korean highways** — rest stops operated by KEPCO/Korea Highway Corporation charge KRW 0. You only pay for private chargers at hotels and city parking lots.
2. **Korea's rest stops are family destinations** — they have clean bathrooms with changing tables, convenience stores, cafés, and often children's play areas. Use them even if you don't need a charge.
3. **Highway tolls** — pay with Hi-pass (short-range RFID) or cash. Hi-pass is recommended for speed. Rent a car with Hi-pass installed.
4. **International Driving Permit** — required for non-Korean licence holders. Get one before you arrive. An IDP in any UN-recognised format is valid for 1 year.
5. **Child car seats** — Korean law requires them for children under 6 years. Most rental companies provide them (KRW 10,000-15,000/day). Book in advance.
6. **Tesla vs. Korean EVs** — Korean EVs (Hyundai, Kia, Genesis) get priority at some brand-specific chargers. For maximum compatibility, rent a Hyundai Ioniq 5 or Kia EV6 — they work on all networks.
7. **Seoraksan in summer** — bring mosquito repellent and water shoes for creek crossings on the hike.
8. **Language:** Most attractions have English signage. Highway signs are bilingual (Korean + English). Download Google Translate offline for restaurant menus.
