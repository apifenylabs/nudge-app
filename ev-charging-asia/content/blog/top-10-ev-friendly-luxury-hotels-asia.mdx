## 1. Four Seasons Bangkok + Capella Bangkok — The Bangkok hotel charging hub

**Charging:** 2x 22 kW AC + DC fast charging | **Price:** $350-$800/night | **Family Rating:** 9.5/10

The Four Seasons Bangkok and Capella Bangkok share the Chao Phraya River complex with a **dedicated EV charging hub** that includes both AC and DC chargers. It's the best hotel charging setup in Bangkok — and possibly all of Southeast Asia.

**Why families love it:**
- **Ultra-luxury family suites** with separate living areas and river views
- **Kids' Club** — supervised activities from Thai cooking classes to art workshops
- **Zero-edge pool** overlooking the river — safe for toddlers with shallow section
- **Charging redundancy** — if one charger is occupied, three others are available
- **Valet charging** — drop your EV keys at the door, and the car is fully charged by morning

> 💡 **Family tip:** The Capella's personal assistant service can arrange for your EV to be charged at a specific time. If you're planning a day trip to Ayutthaya, ask for a 7 AM full charge.

**Nearby attractions:** Grand Palace, Wat Arun, Asiatique Night Market, ICONSIAM shopping mall (with 22 kW chargers)

**Book:** Four Seasons Bangkok — Book your stay on Booking.com

## 2. The Ritz-Carlton, Bali — Best for Indonesian EV road trippers

**Charging:** 4x 22 kW AC | **Price:** $280-$600/night | **Family Rating:** 9.3/10

The Ritz-Carlton Bali in Nusa Dua offers **four dedicated EV charging bays** with 22 kW AC chargers — the most of any luxury hotel in Indonesia. For families driving from Bali's Ngurah Rai Airport or from Sanur, this is a perfect charging hub.

**Family amenities:**
- **Ritz Kids** program — Balinese dance, kite-making, and eco-walks
- **Lazy river** — a hit with children of all ages
- **Multiple pools** including a shallow kids' pool
- **Beachfront dining** — seafood barbecue dinners entertain the whole family

> 💡 **EV tip:** The chargers are located in the underground parking near the main lobby. They're well-lit, covered, and reserved for guests. No additional fee.

## 3. Marina Bay Sands — Singapore's EV charging powerhouse

**Charging:** 12x 22 kW AC | **Price:** $400-$1,200/night | **Family Rating:** 9.0/10

Singapore's most iconic hotel also has the largest hotel EV charging deployment in the city-state. **12 AC chargers** in the basement parking mean you'll almost always find a free spot. Charging costs SGD 15 per session (about $11 USD).

**Family appeal:**
- **Infinity pool** — the world's most famous rooftop pool (50 m, incredible views)
- **ArtScience Museum** — interactive Future World digital playground for kids
- **Sands SkyPark** observation deck
- **Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands** — luxury shopping and dining under one roof

**Charging tip:** Use the Marina Bay Sands chargers during off-peak hours (9 PM - 8 AM) to avoid competition. The hotel's location at the end of Singapore's central expressway makes it a perfect first stop for road-trippers arriving from Malaysia.

## 4. Mandarin Oriental, Tokyo — Japan's best EV-friendly luxury stay

**Charging:** 2x 22 kW AC valet charging | **Price:** $600-$2,500/night | **Family Rating:** 8.8/10

The Mandarin Oriental Tokyo in Nihonbashi offers **free valet EV charging** — you hand over your keys at the entrance, and staff park and charge your EV. For families arriving in Tokyo after a long drive, this is pure gold — no searching for parking, no managing cables.

**Family services:**
- **Mandarin Oriental's Kids' Program** — origami classes, sushi-making, kimono dress-up
- **Family concierge** — pre-arranged baby gear, child-friendly restaurant reservations
- **Skyline views** — the 38th-floor lobby offers breathtaking Tokyo panoramas
- **Deep-soaking bathtubs** — perfect after a long day exploring Tokyo with kids

> 💡 **EV tip:** Japan uses CHAdeMO for DC fast charging and Type 1 (SAE J1772) for AC. If you're driving an imported European or Chinese EV, confirm with the hotel that they have the correct cable adapter.

## How to Book EV-Friendly Luxury Hotels in Asia

When booking your luxury hotel stay with an EV, follow these tips:

1. **Filter on Booking.com** — Use the "EV charging" filter under facilities to find charging-ready hotels
2. **Call ahead** — Confirm the charger type (Type 2 / CCS-2 / CHAdeMO), speed, and if it's reserved for guests
3. **Check for fees** — Most luxury hotels offer free charging, but some (Marina Bay Sands) charge per session
4. **Bring backup** — Pack your Type 2 cable and a 3-pin emergency charger (3.3 kW) just in case
5. **Download hotel app** — Many luxury hotel apps (Four Seasons, Shangri-La) now show real-time charger availability
