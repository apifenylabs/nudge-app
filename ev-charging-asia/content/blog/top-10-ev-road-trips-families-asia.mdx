# Top 10 EV Road Trips for Families Across Asia — Ultimate Ranking for 2026

**Last updated: May 16, 2026**

Asia is a road trip paradise for EV-driving families. The continent's diverse landscapes — from tropical coastlines to mountain temples, modern megacities to ancient ruins — paired with rapidly improving charging infrastructure, make family EV road trips more accessible than ever.

We've ranked the **top 10 family-friendly EV road trips across Asia** based on:
- **Ease of driving** — road quality, signage, traffic patterns
- **Kid-approved stops** — activities that genuinely entertain children
- **Charging reliability** — availability and density of DC fast chargers
- **Seasonal suitability** — best times of year
- **Overall family fun factor** — the X-factor that makes memories

## 1. Singapore → Kuala Lumpur via Malacca (350 km)

**Family Rating: 9.8/10** | **Duration:** 3-4 days | **Difficulty:** Very Easy

The ultimate starter family EV road trip in Southeast Asia. Flat highways, excellent charging, and spectacular stops.

- **Distance:** 350 km | **Charging:** Shell Recharge at Pagoh R&R (180 kW — 15 min), Malacca DC (50 kW), KLCC (50 kW)
- **Total charging cost:** ~SGD 20-30
- **Kid highlights:**
  - **LEGOLAND Malaysia** (20 min off route in Johor) — full day of fun
  - **Malacca River Cruise** — pirates and historic buildings
  - **Malacca Duck Tour** — amphibious vehicle ride
  - **KL Bird Park** — 3,000 birds in a free-flight aviary

**Family-friendly tips:**
- Leave Singapore early morning (before 7 AM) to beat Woodlands Causeway traffic
- Charge at Pagoh R&R while the kids play in the children's playground
- Spend one night in Malacca for the Jonker Walk Night Market (weekends only)
- In KL, park at Suria KLCC (has 50 kW DC chargers) and visit the Petronas Discovery Centre

> 💡 **Pro tip:** The Singapore–KL route is so well-served by chargers that even a 200 km range EV can make this trip with one stop. **Book your stay on Booking.com** — both Malacca and KL have excellent EV-friendly hotels.

## 2. Bangkok → Chiang Mai (696 km)

**Family Rating: 9.5/10** | **Duration:** 3-5 days | **Difficulty:** Easy

Thailand's most iconic road trip has become fully EV-friendly in 2026 thanks to PTT's nationwide charging network. This route takes you from the central plains through UNESCO-listed Sukhothai to the misty mountains of the north.

- **Charging:** PTT EV Station Phichit (150 kW), PEA Volta Lampang (120 kW)
- **Kid highlights:**
  - **Ayutthaya Elephant Palace** — ethical elephant interaction
  - **Sukhothai Historical Park** — cycleable temple ruins
  - **Chiang Mai Night Safari** — nocturnal animal tram ride
  - **Doi Suthep cable car** — panoramic temple views

**Family-friendly tips:**
- Stop at PTT Phichit — it has a 7-Eleven, clean toilets, and a shaded picnic area
- Spend two nights in Chiang Mai; one isn't enough
- Book the Night Safari tickets online to skip queues
- Hotel charging in Chiang Mai is increasingly common — Four Seasons has 50 kW DC chargers

## 3. Kuala Lumpur → Penang (370 km)

**Family Rating: 9.3/10** | **Duration:** 3-4 days | **Difficulty:** Easy

The North-South Expressway from KL to Penang is Malaysia's best-maintained highway, with charging stations at every major R&R stop. Penang itself is a family food paradise.

- **Charging:** Plus Xcessory Tapah R&R (180 kW), PLUS Ipoh Selatan R&R (120 kW)
- **Kid highlights:**
  - **Batu Caves** — colourful Hindu shrine, climb the 272 steps (or take the funicular)
  - **Ipoh** — cave temples and the best white coffee in Malaysia
  - **Penang Hill** — funicular railway to the top
  - **Penang street food** — char kway teow, laksa, cendol

**Family-friendly tips:**
- Visit Batu Caves on the way out of KL (early morning to beat heat and crowds)
- Ipoh is worth an overnight stop — try The Banjaran Hotsprings Retreat (has EV charging)
- On Penang, park your EV at Gurney Plaza (has 22 kW chargers) and walk to the food stalls
- Cross the Penang Bridge (not the ferry) with an EV — simpler and faster

## 4. Tokyo → Hakone → Nagoya (350 km)

**Family Rating: 8.8/10** | **Duration:** 3-4 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate

Japan's EV infrastructure is among Asia's best, and this route showcases everything from neon-lit Tokyo to the iconic Mount Fuji.

- **Charging:** e-Mobility Power stations at Odawara, Gotemba, Shizuoka
- **Kid highlights:**
  - **Fuji-Q Highland** — thrill rides with Fuji views
  - **Hakone Ropeway** — volcanic views and Owakudani black eggs
  - **Hakone Pirate Ship** — cruise across Lake Ashi
  - **Nagoya Lego Discovery Centre** — indoor Lego theme park
  - **Sakura season** (late March–April) — cherry blossoms along the route

**Family-friendly tips:**
- Japan uses CHAdeMO and Type 1 (J1772) — EU/Chinese EV owners need adapters
- Book a **Japanese toll road pass (ETC card)** in advance
- Most Japanese EV charging requires a membership card or smartphone app — download eMP Charge before departure
- Hotel charging is widely available — the **The Prince Hakone Lake Ashinoko** has 2x 22 kW chargers

## 5. Hong Kong → Guangzhou → Shenzhen (200 km)

**Family Rating: 8.5/10** | **Duration:** 2-3 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate

The Greater Bay Area road trip connects three of China's most dynamic cities with excellent EV charging infrastructure. This is also the route where China's homegrown charging standards (GB/T) are dominant.

- **Charging:** TELD (T Good) DC chargers in Nansha, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen
- **Kid highlights:**
  - **Hong Kong Ocean Park** — marine life and thrill rides
  - **Chimelong Safari Park** (Guangzhou) — drive-through safari with pandas
  - **Canton Tower** (Guangzhou) — observation deck with kids' activities
  - **Shenzhen Splendid China** — miniature landmarks from across China

**Family-friendly tips:**
- EVs crossing from Hong Kong to mainland China need **two sets of charging cables**: Type 2/CCS-2 for HK, GB/T for mainland
- Download **Alipay + AutoNavi (Gaode)** for navigation and EV charging payments
- Chinese charging stations are plentiful — Shenzhen alone has over 50,000 public chargers
- The Humen Pearl River Bridge has heavy traffic on weekends; travel mid-week if possible

## 6. Bali Ngurah Rai → Ubud → Candi Dasa (120 km)

**Family Rating: 8.7/10** | **Duration:** 3-5 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate

Bali's relatively short distances make it perfect for EV exploration, though charging infrastructure is still developing. This route covers the best of Bali's culture, nature, and beaches.

- **Charging:** PLN (state utility) DC chargers at Sanur and Ubud
- **Kid highlights:**
  - **Ubud Monkey Forest** — sacred monkey sanctuary
  - **Tegalalang Rice Terraces** — iconic stepped paddies
  - **Bali Safari & Marine Park** — drive-through safari
  - **Candi Dasa snorkelling** — calm bay perfect for beginners

**Family-friendly tips:**
- Charge at Sanur before heading to Ubud — the uphill drive to central Bali consumes more battery
- Ubud has narrow, winding roads — drive carefully and watch for motorbikes
- Ritz-Carlton Bali (Nusa Dua) has 4 x 22 kW chargers, while Four Seasons Jimbaran has 2 x 22 kW
- Download **PLN Mobile app** to locate and pay at Indonesia's state-run chargers

## 7. Seoul → Gyeongju → Busan (400 km)

**Family Rating: 9.0/10** | **Duration:** 4-5 days | **Difficulty:** Easy

South Korea's highways are EV-friendly, with fast chargers every 40-60 km. The Seoul–Gyeongju–Busan route takes you through Korea's ancient capital to the vibrant coastal city of Busan.

- **Charging:** Korea EV Charging Service (K-EV) at expressway service areas — 200 kW DC widely available
- **Kid highlights:**
  - **Everland** (Yongin) — Korea's largest theme park, on route from Seoul
  - **Gyeongju National Museum** — kids can try on traditional Korean costumes
  - **Bomun Lake** (Gyeongju) — pedal boats and bike rentals
  - **Haeundae Beach** (Busan) — sandcastle competitions and Sea Life Aquarium
  - **Gamcheon Culture Village** — colourful houses, art installations

**Family-friendly tips:**
- Korea's expressway service areas are world-class — clean bathrooms, playgrounds, and plenty of food options
- Most Korean EV chargers use **CHAdeMO and Type 1** — CCS-2 is only on new infrastructure
- Download **EVinfra** app for real-time charger availability
- Gyeongju's historic sites are spread out — having an EV for the "Ancient Capital Tour" is ideal

## 8. Taipei → Taroko Gorge → Hualien (200 km)

**Family Rating: 8.6/10** | **Duration:** 3-5 days | **Difficulty:** Moderate**

Taiwan's east coast is a stunning drive of sheer marble cliffs, turquoise Pacific waters, and hot springs. The Suhua Highway improvement project (completed 2020) made this route much safer and easier.

- **Charging:** Noodoe DC chargers at Hualien and Taroko visitor centre
- **Kid highlights:**
  - **Taroko National Park** — Shakadang Trail is flat and easy for kids
  - **Qingshui Cliffs** — dramatic coastal cliffs visible from the road
  - **Zhuilu Old Trail** — for adventurous older kids (permit required)
  - **Hualien night market** — the best food in eastern Taiwan
  - **Zhiben Hot Springs** (Taitung) — soak tired muscles after driving

**Family-friendly tips:**
- The Suhua Highway has tunnels and sharp curves — drive slowly and use headlights even during day
- Taroko Gorge road is narrow in sections — EVs with 360-degree cameras (BYD Seal, NIO ET5) have an advantage
- Noodoe chargers require a Taiwan SIM card and app — get one at the airport
- Book stays in Hualien City for dining options; the Lavender Cottage B&B has EV charging

## 9. Ho Chi Minh City → Da Lat (310 km)

**Family Rating: 7.5/10** | **Duration:** 3-4 days | **Difficulty:** Hard

Da Lat is Vietnam's hill station gem — cool air, pine forests, waterfalls, and a distinctly European vibe. The drive from HCM City involves steep mountain passes that are challenging but rewarding.

- **Charging:** VinFast charging stations at designated stops — limited but growing
- **Kid highlights:**
  - **Datanla Waterfall** — alpine coaster ride through the forest
  - **Crazy House** — surreal architecture kids love exploring
  - **Da Lat Flower Gardens** — seasonal blooms
  - **Strawberry and coffee picking** — U-pick farms around the city

**Family-friendly tips:**
- This is the most challenging drive on the list due to mountain roads and limited charger density
- Charge to 100% before leaving HCM City — the next reliable DC charger is at Bao Loc (150 km)
- Driving time is 5-7 hours due to winding roads — break it into two days
- VinFast chargers use GB/T standard — Chinese EVs are fine, but non-Chinese brands may need an adapter

## 10. Mumbai → Lonavala → Pune (150 km)

**Family Rating: 7.0/10** | **Difficulty:** Hard** | **Duration:** 2-3 days

India's EV infrastructure is still in its early stages, but the Mumbai–Pune expressway is the most EV-ready route in the country. Lonavala's hill station atmosphere makes it a popular weekend escape.

- **Charging:** Tata Power DC chargers at Lonavala (30 kW), limited availability
- **Kid highlights:**
  - **Imagica Theme Park** (on route) — India's largest theme park
  - **Lonavala hill stations** — Bhushi Dam, Lion's Point, sunset views
  - **Tiger's Leap** — short, manageable hike with panoramic views
  - **Karla Caves** — ancient Buddhist cave temples, climbable for older kids

**Family-friendly tips:**
- India's EV charging is dominated by **Tata Power and MG's Superfast Network**
- Download **Tata Power EZ Charge** app and top up your account before departure
- The Mumbai traffic exiting the city can take 1-2 hours alone — plan for this
- Stick to the Mumbai–Pune Expressway (not the old highway) — it's safer and has better EV coverage
- Consider an **MG ZS EV** rental from Mumbai — MG has the best charging support network

### Essential Gear for Family EV Road Trips in Asia

- **CCS-2 cable** — not all chargers have tethered cables
- **Type 2 to Type 2 cable** — for hotel AC charging
- **Emergency 3-pin charger** — 3.3 kW, just in case
- **100W USB-C car charger** — keeps tablets and phones charged
- **Window sunshades** — rear windows for sleeping kids
- **Snack organizer** — clip-on back-of-seat snack containers
- **Travel medicine kit** — motion sickness tablets, rehydration salts, basic first-aid
- **Charging adapters** — travel adapters for different countries' power sockets

## The Verdict

Southeast Asia dominates the list for **ease of family EV road tripping**, with **Thailand, Malaysia, and Singapore** having the most established charging ecosystems. Japan and South Korea offer better infrastructure but more complex payment and membership requirements.

For a first-time family EV road trip in Asia, start with the **Singapore → KL route** — it's the easiest, most family-packed, and most EV-reliable route on the continent. Once you've got confidence, challenge yourself with the **Bangkok → Chiang Mai epic** or the **Taipei → Taroko Gorge coastal drive**.
