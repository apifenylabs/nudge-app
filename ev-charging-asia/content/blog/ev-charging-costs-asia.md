# EV Charging Costs Across Asia: Country-by-Country Price Guide (2026)

EV running costs vary wildly across Asia. Charge a 60kWh battery at home in Thailand and you'll pay roughly 156 THB (HK$34). Do the same in Japan and it's roughly 1,400 JPY (HK$73) — more than double. And public DC fast charging? The spread is even bigger. This guide breaks down real costs per kWh, per full charge, and per 1,000 km across six major Asian markets.

## Key Takeaways
- Home charging is 50-75% cheaper than public DC in every country — prioritise home or workplace charging
- Thailand and India are the cheapest countries for EV running costs per km
- Singapore's public charging is the most expensive in SE Asia at SGD 0.55-0.80/kWh
- Japan's minute-based CHAdeMO pricing can trap unwary drivers — stick to CCS2 per-kWh stations
- Malaysia has the widest gap between home and public pricing (up to 5x)

## 🇹🇭 Thailand

| Charging Type | Rate | Full Charge (60kWh) | Cost per 1,000km |
|--------------|------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Home (TOU off-peak) | 2.60 THB/kWh | 156 THB (HK$34) | 398 THB (HK$87) |
| Home (standard) | 3.88 THB/kWh | 233 THB (HK$51) | 594 THB (HK$130) |
| Public DC (EA Anywhere) | 7.11 THB/kWh | 427 THB (HK$93) | 1,088 THB (HK$237) |
| Public DC (PTT PluZ off-peak) | 6.00 THB/kWh | 360 THB (HK$79) | 918 THB (HK$200) |

**Verdict:** Home charging savings are enormous. A TOU meter is essential. Monthly cost for 1,500 km on off-peak: ~598 THB.

## Smart Savings Tip

For road-trippers crossing borders: charge in the cheaper country before crossing. A full charge in Johor Bahru (Malaysia) costs ~RM35 (HK$62), but the same charge in Singapore costs S$18 (HK$105). That HK$43 difference adds up fast over multiple charges.
